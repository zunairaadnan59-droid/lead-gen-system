About Us
crafting unforgettable bridal journeys
A Bridal Boutique in the Heart of Babylon, NY
Village Bridal has been recently updated and completely transformed since its new ownership in January 2020. Village Bridal is now owned by Omnibus, a family owned business that has built and maintained a stellar reputation in women’s evening wear in the community for over 30 years. We are excited to bring the best experience to each and everyone that comes through our doors!
Specializing in finding our brides the wedding gown of their dreams as well as Mother of the Bride/Groom, veils, jewelry, & accessories. We provide in-house alterations for your convenience and pride ourself on exceptional service and attention to detail. Appointments are preferred in order to provide you with the best one on one experience with a dedicated bridal stylist.
