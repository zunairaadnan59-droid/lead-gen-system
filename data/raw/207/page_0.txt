At Village Bridal, we believe finding your wedding dress should feel as beautiful as the day itself. Our curated collection of designer bridal gowns blends modern romance with timeless elegance, offering silhouettes and details that reflect your unique love story.
Located in the heart of Babylon, New York, our boutique provides an intimate, personalized experience designed to help you say yes with confidence.

Our collection is thoughtfully curated to ensure that every bride, no matter her shape or size, can find her dream gown. We believe that beauty comes in all forms, and we are committed to making every bride feel confident, comfortable, and stunning on her big day.
OUR COLLECTION
Come celebrate the most important day of your life in a perfect gown from Village Bridal.
Village Bridal is a family owned and operated boutique with over 30 years in the community and industry. We are a full service bridal salon that offers a huge selection of bridal attire, evening wear, and bridesmaids dresses from today's top designers and manufacturers.
﻿
No matter what style you may be looking for, our professional bridal consultants will give you their undivided attention and provide you with the highest level of service you deserve to help you find the perfect dress for your wedding day. We also offer a full collection of wedding day accessories to complete your wedding day look! At Village Bridal, we are here to help make this one of your favorite parts of the wedding planning experience.
