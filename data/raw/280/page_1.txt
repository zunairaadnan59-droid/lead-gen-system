ABOUT LAS AMERICAS PREMIUM OUTLETS
®
4211 Camino de la Plaza, San Diego, CA 92173

With more than 190 stores, Las Americas Premium Outlets® is the area's largest outlet center, located just 20 minutes south of downtown San Diego and 20 minutes north of Tijuana. Stroll through beautiful outdoor courtyard settings and shop at designer and name brand stores: Kate Spade New York, H&M, Karl Lagerfield Paris, Nike Factory and more. Dine at Achiote Mexican Restaurant and enjoy the authentic flavors of Mexico, or sit down and enjoy the variety of restaurant options at the Food Court. Area attractions include beautiful beaches, SeaWorld, The world-famous San Diego Zoo, Balboa Park, Old Town and Seaport Village. At the crossroads of two countries , two major cities and the busiest international border crossing in the world, Las Americas Premium Outlets® has transformed the region into a place to stop and visit.

By Car
From San Diego
Las Americas Premium Outlets is conveniently located from all areas of San Diego, just 20 minutes south of downtown off two freeways:
From I-5 exit right on Dairy Mart Road, go 1/8th mile and turn left onto Camino de la Plaza. The center is one mile on the right.
From I-805 exit on Camino de la Plaza, the last exit before the International border crossing into Mexico. Turn right and go 1/8th mile, center is on the left.
From Mexico
Cross into U.S. on I-805 north and exit right on San Ysidro Blvd. and turn right on Camino de la Plaza. The center is on the left.

By Bus
From San Diego
From the south area take 906/907 Bus to Las Americas Premium Outlets.
From the downtown area take 929 Bus and connect with Iris Avenue Trolley Station.
Mexicoach
Shopping Excursions to Las Americas Premium Outlets from the Tijuana Tourist Terminal located in downtown Tijuana on Avenida Revolucion between 6th & 7th Street.
By Trolley
San Diego Trolley
Take the San Diego Blue Line trolley to Las Americas Premium Outlets. Exit at the San Ysidro Transit center located at the international border. Las Americas Premium Outlets is located a short five minute walk across the pedestrian friendly bridge and one block up on the right.
By Taxi
By Pedicab Taxi
Eco Pedicabs transports passengers between the San Ysidro Trolley stop and/or U.S. side of the border and the Las Americas Premium Outlets roundabout. Please call 619-446-6876 for pick up.
