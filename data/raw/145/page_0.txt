ENSEMBLE  Boutiques
With two locations in Boston’s Historic North End and in Moultonborough, New Hampshire on Lake Winnipesaukee. Ensemble offers a carefully curated, amazing collection of  Gifts, Women’s clothing, Jewelry, Home decor, Furniture, and Vintage Finds for a creative lifestyle.
Our products consist of a group of quality on trend designs from around the globe. We pride ourselves on providing a limited number of each style so that our clients can acquire pieces that are unique.  Our assortment changes seasonally, and sometimes even daily.
We are always happy to help with items for you, your home and gifts for everyone on your list! Be sure to check our current hours listed below.
We have moved! Not to worry, our new location is only a few blocks from our original space. If you continue down towards 114 Salem St you will see LoContes Restaurant. From there you will go a short ways down Baldwin Pl to our new location at 2 Baldwin Pl Boston, Ma, 02113. We are very excited to assist you with your home furnishing needs! All are welcome, please stop by!
