WHO WE ARE
IT ALL BEGAN WITH A DREAM
Scout & Molly’s Boutique
began with a dream.
A place where women would feel comfortable shopping, a place where they didn’t have to worry about what they were wearing when they walked in the store, a place where women could count on a friendly face and an honest opinion.
CORE VALUES
You Matter
We emphasize the worth and importance of every individual.
We are Transparent
We value open communication, honest advice, and guidance in decision-making.
We are Ethical
Our integrity and honesty create a trusting experience.
We are Diligent
We’re dedicated to perfecting every detail, every time.
We are Innovative
We embrace cutting edge trends and fresh ideas that define our approach.
Our Promise
At Scout & Molly’s, customer service is our number one priority. Whether you are shopping for your new favorite pair of jeans, or the perfect dress to make a statement, our stylists will be with you every step of the way. You can always count on a warm welcome and a fun, top- notch, no-pressure shopping experience that’s based on respect, honesty, and kindness.
That’s our Promise.
OUR PROMISE
Every Scout & Molly’s Boutique is staffed with a friendly team of stylists who provide fashion and fitting expertise, helpful feedback and an honest opinion, the way a good friend would.
We are proud to carry a diversity of
brands
and a plethora of chic styles to create an enjoyable and fulfilling shopping experience for our customers. No outfit is complete without fun, stylish accessories. Customers can count on Scout & Molly’s to offer a wide selection of the latest from jewelry to handbags.
