Manifesto
our passion
Essentique is an all natural beauty care brand established in 2016. The founder, Mondonna, a biologist with years of lab experience, began to formulate and test her own hair, skin, and aromatherapy products because she couldn’t find the products that satisfied her requirements. Over the years, Mondonna had developed her own holistic natural beauty care regimen and lifestyle and needed products that were highly effective, all natural and clean, with no harm to the body, to another being, or to the planet. The initial formulations for the product line took several years to complete, and soon after she established her boutique and lab in Palo Alto, where every bottle and jar continues to be hand-made and fresh.
Essentique was established to break with the norms of the industry and provide boutique quality, clean, and natural products to improve the overall well-being of our customers and of the planet. Our line currently includes everything from hair and skin care to aromatherapy and perfume oils. Every ingredient in every product is thoroughly researched and included with purpose. We never stop looking for new ways to improve the quality and effectiveness of our products. This is what we do. We want you to discover the essence of your own natural beauty. This is our passion
