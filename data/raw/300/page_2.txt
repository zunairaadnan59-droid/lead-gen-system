Our priority is that our customers’ needs are met. Everyone deserves to be pampered. Because we love to provide our clients with personalized service we request you book your appointment in advance.  Our team is committed to meeting those needs. The DLB team are experts in ONE-BY-ONE (eyelash) application.  Meaning each lash unit is applied to each of your natural lashes transforming your eyes tremendously after a single session. For a service that is as unique as you are, the lash choices are geared to suit your eyes only. We combine a variety different lash thicknesses, lengths, curl and even color to your eye lashes.
