Our Story
Pinky of Greenwich was born
...  after the marriage of my two greatest passions... color (specifically pink) and beautiful ribbons.  Originally, I hand wove colorful ribbons into decorative pillows. This then evolved into the creation of handbags. The Pinky collection continued to develop and began to include fabrics such as European faux fur, animal prints, "Chanel" inspired tweeds, florals, Pucci prints and more. While the look is clearly classic, there is always a detail that sets it aside form others. A "Bow" in many forms, shapes and sizes became  a must. Nothing is ever made without a ribbon on it somewhere.
As time has passed our merchandise assortment has expanded greatly. With ever changing trends we now  carry a variety of women's apparel collections such as Gretchen Scott, Patty Kim, Krazy Larry, Lysee, IBKUL, Dizzie Lizzie to name a few, plus  many different personal  accessories, handbags and jewelry.  Great colorful looks in both faux fur and real coats, ponchos, wraps, dresses, silk jackets,tops, tunics, leggings  and much more!  Hope you will peruse, enjoy, and think PINK!
