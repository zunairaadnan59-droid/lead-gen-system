Welcome to our
magical
space
Ready to dive into a world of beauty and glamour? Step through our doors and enter a magical pink wonderland where all your hair and fashion dreams come true.
We’re Amy and Monique, best friends who turned a lightbulb moment in 2018 into Pink Lemon Salon. With hearts full of passion and a dash of sass, we created a lively haven where clients can unwind, have fun, and leave feeling absolutely fabulous! By 2020, we added a boutique, transforming Pink Lemon into the ultimate one-stop beauty extravaganza. In 2023, we took a bold step by moving to the historic Sage Hill Shopping Center, making our dream even bigger and better.
We've designed an enchanting space where you come in as a client but leave as family. Every detail, from the decor to the staff to the merch, has been thoughtfully curated to create a super fun and inviting experience. Our talented team is here to glam you up from head to toe, giving you the ultimate salon and shopping experience you deserve.
So, what are you waiting for? Visit us at Pink Lemon and let the magic begin! Our mission? To spread beauty and joy from head to toe, sprinkling a little magic and glamour wherever we go!
XO, Amy & Monique
