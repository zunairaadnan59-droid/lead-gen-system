We are glad you found us
Lou Lou boutiques was established in 2004 when it was a single brick-and-mortar location on the quaint cobblestone streets of Middleburg, Virginia. For over 20 years, we have increased our footprint to include locations in five different states. Family owned, Tara and Ben Wegdam still oversee the day-to-day operations of our boutiques.
Tara is from Nashville, Tennessee and has spent her whole life in and around retail, following in her parents’ retail footsteps. Ben was born and raised in the Netherlands and ended up in retail directly after graduating from college in Rotterdam.
We are big on entertainment. For loulou that includes a fantastic product selection to choose from, in a friendly and inviting environment with fun and knowledgeable store associates. And that is why we do not sell online. We cannot communicate the compassion and entertainment of an in store experience through a computer.
While we also stock fashionable apparel and home goods, we are truly an accessories boutique at our core, full of vibrant and exciting finds that our savvy shoppers can’t find anywhere else! We are passionate about boutique retailing and our community, family, and locally sourced products are what we value. Come check us out !
What We Sell
From everyday essentials to standout statement pieces, our curated collection features clothing, scarves, handbags, custom jewelry, and gifts—each chosen for its quality, charm, and individuality. Whether you're refreshing your wardrobe or searching for something special, you'll find timeless staples, unique accents, and meaningful pieces that reflect your personal style. With a mix of well-loved brands and one-of-a-kind finds, we make it easy to dress up, gift well, and feel your best.
How LouLou stands out
Lou Lou Boutique is all about thoughtful style and genuine connection. From our exclusive custom jewelry—crafted with care from U.S.-sourced materials—to the handpicked brands we carry, every detail reflects our passion for helping you look and feel your best. We’re here to make fashion personal, fun, and uniquely you.
Custom Jewelry
Our custom jewelry is more than just an accessory—it’s a story. Each piece is thoughtfully designed and crafted using materials sourced from a variety of small makers and trusted suppliers across the U.S. From delicate charms to bold statement pieces, our collections reflect the care, creativity, and quality we believe every woman deserves.

Whether you're searching for a subtle everyday staple or something that makes a statement, we’re here to guide you every step of the way. Crème de la Crème
The Crème de la Crème brand encompasses five brick and mortar shops along with an online boutique inspired by French and Italian living with a touch of Southern hospitality. This mix brings a curated collection of unique and intriguing finds. Our home and flagship store is located in the heart of Hunt Country in the village of Middleburg, Virginia. Our four other locations are part of the eclectic and locally owned shops of downtown Frederick, Maryland, downtown Leesburg, Virginia, historic downtown Alexandria, Virginia, and in Richmond, Virginia in historic Carytown Court.
