About Our Company
Serving the Roaring Fork Valley with Excellence
Dedicated to Quality Flooring in Aspen, CO for Over 15 Years
Aspen Carpet & Floors has proudly served Aspen and the Roaring Fork Valley for more than 15 years, bringing an elevated approach to every project. Our mission is simple: deliver flooring solutions that combine design-forward style, expert craftsmanship, and long-lasting performance. We believe in making the process seamless for our clients—whether you’re a homeowner upgrading your living space or a designer sourcing premium materials. With a curated selection that includes carpet, hardwood, waterproof flooring, tile, and custom STARK rugs, our showroom offers options for every vision. Most importantly, we strive to create lasting partnerships built on trust, artistry, and exceptional service.

Working with Aspen Carpet & Floors is more than flooring selection. It’s curated product, designer-grade guidance, artisan installation, and a service promise we stand behind at every step.
