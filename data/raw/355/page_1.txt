About Us
Pet City Houston is a family-owned and operated pet store. We have been in business for over 30 years and are known as Houston’s number-one-rated pet store. We carry everything from fish, marine fish, reptiles, exotics, puppies, birds, and more. If you are looking for unique items for your dog, fish or reptiles, then Pet City is the store for you. Thank you for shopping local and supporting our store. Service after the sale is something you will come to expect when you shop at Pet City Houston.
We are a proud member of our community and that is why our involvement with you is so important. That is why we have wonderful community programs that help and benefit the ones who are in need.
