Our Story
FUN AND UNIQUE CLOTHING!
Traffic was born in 2002 with the intention of bringing fun and unique clothing to the heart of Old Town Basalt and the Roaring Fork Valley. Those who were craving distinctive apparel, shoes, and accessories found it in Traffic.
Not only do we strive to meet your fashion needs, most importantly, we are dedicated to the highest level of serving our Traffic family.
