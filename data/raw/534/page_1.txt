Our Team
Earth Wind & Fire Boutique is a unique women’s retail store offering specialty clothing, jewelry, and accessories. We are a sophisticated gem nestled in the heart of the world-famous Pike Place Market in beautiful downtown Seattle, Washington.
Join us and step into an exciting world of fashion and elegance. We feature exquisite hand-crafted jewelry, unique handbags, and other refined accessories. We have a wide selection of clothing sizes, including petite and plus sizes. Need a one-of-a-kind piece? You’ve found the right place.
The majority of our products are designed and produced in the United States. Our merchandise is known for being distinctive and not readily available at other stores in the area.
Meet our team!
Originally from Peru, Danny is an engineer and self-made entrepreneur. He moved to Seattle in 2012 to pursue his dreams and now he owns and manages one of the most unique boutiques in the city. Danny’s vision for EWF is to provide stunning, quality products while delivering an exceptional customer experience
Erica loves being part of the Pike Place Market family and is artistic and genuine, with a good eye for fashion. She is always happy to help customers find their next favorite piece. She loves her job and customers love her!
Upon receiving her BFA from Syracuse University with a major in illustration, Debbie started out as a graphic designer. She found a new passion while attending the Pratt Fine Arts Center, taking courses in glass beadmaking and metal smithing. She handcrafts necklaces, bracelets, and earrings and sells her jewelry at craft shows throughout the area. She has a flair for fashion and is great with people so she is a perfect fit at Earth, Wind and Fire Boutique!
