Chicago's Online Formal Dress Store | Prom, Homecoming & Quinceañera Dresses
Since 1984, Peaches Boutique has grown from a Chicago storefront into one of the largest online formal dress stores in the country, shipping prom, homecoming, quinceañera, and special occasion dresses to customers in all 50 states and worldwide. Our 20,000-square-foot Chicago showroom carries more than 30,000 dresses at any given time, spanning hundreds of designers and sizes 000 through 32W.
Shop Formal Dresses Online, Shipped Nationwide
You do not have to be in Chicago to shop at Peaches Boutique. Our online store carries 4,500+ unique styles from top designers including Sherri Hill, Jovani, Morilee, and Jasz, with new styles added every season. All in-stock orders typically ship the next business day to any U.S. address or worldwide, so no matter where you are, your dress arrives fast.
Visit Our Chicago Dress Boutique
Our Chicago location is one of the largest formal dress boutiques in the country. Spanning over 20,000 square feet with 45 private fitting rooms, our in-store experience is built for serious dress shopping. Our team is here to guide you through thousands of styles and help you find the right fit, the right look, and the right dress for your occasion.
Formal Dresses in Every Size & Color
We carry sizes 000 through 32W across our entire collection in a variety of colors, because every person deserves to find their dress. From ball gowns and sequin styles to flowing chiffon and bold two-piece looks, our selection covers every occasion, every taste, and every budget.
