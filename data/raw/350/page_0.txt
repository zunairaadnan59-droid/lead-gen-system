Featured Artist
Clementine Cavanagh
Clementine spent her entire school years taking extensive art classes and apprenticing under an established Maine artist into adulthood. She explored a large variety of mediums from ink, watercolor, oil, to ceramics and sculpture.
Clementine’s work is contemporary and expressionistic. With bold colors, line and patterns she aims to grasp feminine leisure and the art of celebration with rich interiors.
