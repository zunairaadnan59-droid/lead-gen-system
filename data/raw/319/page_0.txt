VANELi
Inspired by the effortless styles worn on the cobblestone streets of Italy, VANELi was founded on the belief that every woman deserves the perfect fit and comfort in every step – without ever having to sacrifice looking her very best. Our designers comb the globe to source the highest quality materials and discover new sources of inspiration. We love blending practicality with playfulness and of-the-moment details, offering women of all generations coveted footwear. Our wide range of sizes and widths combined with an ever-growing selection of silhouettes make VANELi a perfect choice for every occasion You will find tried-and-true classics with modern and innovative updates on shoes you will never want to take off!
