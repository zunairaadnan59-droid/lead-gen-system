Rose Quartz & Black Tourmaline 2.75ft 304kg Swiveling Heart Chair - Brazil
Description
Part sculpture, part seat. Carved by hand in Brazil, this rose quartz heart chair gives a familiar form real weight. At 304 kg, it’s substantial, yet the soft curves and deep seat keep it inviting, shaped to feel like it’s holding you rather than simply supporting you. The stone shows a clean pink with a subtle blue undertone, lending a cooler, more refined presence across the polished surface.
Natural black tourmaline inclusions are preserved along the edges, adding contrast and texture while grounding the piece visually. Set on a swiveling base, the rotation is smooth and stable. The seat is generously carved and genuinely comfortable, designed to be used as much as it is admired.
A rare balance of scale and intimacy. Something people will naturally be drawn to.
Height: 33 Inches
Width: ~4 ft
Depth: ~3 ft
Weight: 1109 kg
