What is the Shape Method?
The Shape Method is an exercise philosophy created by pilates practitioner and former professional dancer Jennifer McCamish that uses the principles of Pilates, interval/overload training and movement technique to achieve maximum results for toning and fitness while building a foundation of injury prevention that allows for lifelong functional movement.
