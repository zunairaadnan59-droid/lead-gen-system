About Us
Renaissance Design is a GFRC manufacturing company and has been in business over 20 years. We have completed over 200 projects ranging from new construction and building restoration throughout the continental USA.
In addition to the commercial work, we offer a large line of home furnishings that include planters, fountains, tables, pedestals, seating, statuary, and a wide variety of fireplace mantels ranging from traditional to contemporary.
The company is located in Lincoln Nebraska and that is about as centrally located as it gets, which gives the company easy logistical and geographic access to cities and locations throughout the continental United States.
