Mac and Me
Mac and Me was founded in 2014 on the love between mother and daughter. Mac (daughter) and Marie (mom) design every piece side by side, creating timeless and comfortable pieces for women of all generations. Every detail, down to stitch colors and grommets, has been thoughtfully designed for the beautiful women who wear Mac and Me.
Our famous denim will lift you up, hold you in, and make you forget you’re wearing jeans. Our tops and jewelry are limited-run, must-have closet staples. And every piece of clothing is lined with our trademark - “You Are So Loved!”
