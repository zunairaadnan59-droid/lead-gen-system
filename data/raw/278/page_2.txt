About San Diego Jewelry Buyers
Get a free appraisal
(619) 236-9603
Free, verbal, in person. No obligation to sell.
San Diego Jewelry Buyers® (SDJB) is the leading gold, silver, platinum, diamond, watch, and luxury goods buyer in Southern California. Whether you have an antique or estate piece of jewelry, a luxury watch, or a designer handbag, SDJB provides the best appraisals and convenient same-day cash or cash loans.
Our Process
Once you walk through our door, one of our friendly staff members will greet you and offer you a cup of freshly brewed espresso, coffee, or tea. You will then be escorted to a private buying room where you will meet one-on-one with one of our expert buyers.
Our buyer will carefully examine your piece of jewelry or luxury asset. They will look at it under high magnification and will closely inspect it from every angle. As they work, they will tell you what they see and how it affects the item’s value.
Once they have completed their complimentary verbal appraisal, your buyer will give you valuable information about your jewelry or luxury asset and will make a formal cash offer to purchase your item. You are under no obligation to sell your item.
You are welcome to take the information that you have learned from our free verbal appraisal and visit other jewelry buyers to find out what they can offer.
Schedule Your Free Consultation
If you do choose to sell your item to us, we are happy to offer 4 different ways for you to sell your jewelry or luxury asset:
Sell My Jewelry Direct.
The quickest, easiest way to sell your jewelry for cash.
You sell your item to us and receive instant cash.
Get a Temporary Loan.
Pawn your item and receive a cash loan on the spot. No credit check required.
Perfect for new business owners who cannot qualify for a traditional bank loan. No minimum and no maximum.
Sell My Jewelry on Consignment.
Receive up to 30% more cash than if you sell your jewelry or luxury item direct.
Sell My Jewelry from Home.
Can’t make it to our downtown San Diego office? No problem.
You can sell your jewelry or luxury item from the comfort of your own home. We will provide a complimentary Mail from Home Kit that includes detailed instructions and will provide free shipping. Our top priority is to ensure your jewelry is safe and trackable.
Our Buyers
Our buyers are experienced jewelry dealers, diamond cutters, and GIA Graduate Diamonds diploma holders.
Unlike many jewelry or estate buyers, SDJB’s buyers are extremely knowledgeable and experienced in all aspects of their field. They have a combined 50+ years of experience in buying and selling jewelry. Our team’s experience and dedication to providing expert appraisals guarantees you receive the best information and price for your item.
Our buyers look not just at the weight of your item or its carat weight, but at the setting, the clarity, and the potential to be re-purposed into an even more beautiful piece of jewelry. They see the intricate details of each piece and can therefore provide a thorough appraisal and a fair cash offer.

What makes this different from a pawn shop counter?
The appraisal is done in front of you and explained. You get a free verbal appraisal with no fee and no obligation, whether you sell, borrow or walk out with the piece. Our loan fees are capped and posted openly, which most lenders in this category do not do.
Who am I actually dealing with?
The family that owns the place. Kevin Keller, a GIA Graduate Diamonds diploma holder, is usually on the counter himself, and his son Maxwell runs operations. There is no call center and no regional manager to escalate to: the person who values your piece is the person who decides what we can pay for it.
Do I have to sell?
No. Plenty of people come in to find out what something is worth and leave with it. You can also borrow against a piece and keep ownership, or place it on consignment. The appraisal costs nothing either way.
Where are you and how do I park?
907 6th Ave in the Gaslamp Quarter, downtown San Diego. We reserve a free parking space for our customers in the ACE lot directly across the street from the store, marked with a sign that reads Reserved for San Diego Jewelry Buyers. There is metered street parking right out front too, and paid lots on the same block. A buyer meets you at your car.
How do I know you are legitimate?
Do not take our word for it. The California license number below is public and searchable, the Better Business Bureau profile is a third party we do not control, and the eBay store carries 27 years of feedback that cannot be edited after the fact.
We have been at the same downtown address since 2006, which is the part that is hardest to fake: walk in and see it. If you want to speak to other dealers who have traded with us, call and we will put you in touch directly.
