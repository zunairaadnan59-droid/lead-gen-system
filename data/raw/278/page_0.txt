How it works
From appraisal to an offer, with you in control.
1
Bring it in
Bring your piece and a valid government-issued photo ID. Weekday walk-ins are welcome; call ahead to discuss your item with the right buyer.
2
We examine it
Loupe, scale and tester, at the counter, while you watch. We tell you what we are looking at and why it matters to the value.
3
You get an offer
A cash figure, explained. There is no fee and no obligation either way.
4
You get paid
Same day, in cash, by check or by wire. Or borrow against the piece instead, keeping ownership while we hold it as collateral.
