We’re Komune
Komune is a new fashion retailer focused on championing emerging designers and creatives.
We seek to provide an experience-driven initiative that establishes a place of discourse and discovery for those passionate about thoughtful design.
Our objective is to showcase the design philosophy and process behind the designers we stock through our media and in-store experiences.
