Marina B Fine Jewelry
Marina Bulgari, a third-generation member of the renowned Bulgari jewelry family, left her family’s Maison in 1976 to forge her own path by founding Marina B, quickly earning global recognition for her bold, timeless designs. She revolutionized fine jewelry by pioneering innovative gemstone cuts and reimagining classic forms, creating pieces that were both glamorous and effortless while empowering women to buy jewelry for themselves—an idea that reshaped the industry.

oliver Smith Jeweler's diamond difference
At Oliver Smith Jeweler, we are committed to offering natural, ethically sourced diamonds of the highest quality. Our diamonds are sourced through the Kimberley Process, ensuring they are conflict-free and responsibly mined. We select GIA-certified stones for our loose diamonds, and only use gem quality diamonds in our ring settings.
