About Little Tyke Learning Center
For more than 50 years, Little Tyke Learning Center has proudly served families as a locally owned and operated early education provider. With over 12 locations, we offer trusted, high-quality programs for children from 6 weeks through 5 years, designed to support growth at every stage of early development. As a Texas Rising Star–recognized provider, our centers combine experienced educators, purposefully designed curriculum, and strong family partnerships to create safe, nurturing environments where children can build a strong foundation of success.

Certification for Exceptional Early Learning
As a four-star-rated Texas Rising Star early learing center, Little Tyke demostrates the highest level of care and stands apart in the industry for high standards of success. Our learning centers exceed the Texas Rising Star criteria by complying with DFPS licensing, maintaining qualifications for directors and caregivers, establishing staff orientation and training,  providing a healthy and safe environment, writing curriculum goals, and producing positive caregiver to child interactions.

About the President
Alex Little has held numerous leadership positions on both the state and Fort Worth Affiliate boards of the Texas Licensed Child Care Association (TLCCA) over the last 30 years and continues to hold his role within the organization in high regard. Through his advocacy and involvement with TLCCA, Alex has worked to help keep licensed child care affordable, accessible, and sustainable for working families across Texas.
As a second-generation owner of Creative Child Care, Inc. dba Little Tyke Learning Centers, Alex carries forward a family legacy rooted in faith, hard work, and a genuine passion for early childhood education. Having grown up in the business himself, Alex understands firsthand the lifelong impact that loving teachers and strong early learning foundations can have on a child’s future. He is also the proud parent of two Little Tyke graduates.
Alex believes deeply in family-style, servant-minded leadership. He passionately views every teacher, administrator, cook, bus driver, and team member — regardless of title — as part of the Little Tyke family. His leadership philosophy centers around supporting people first, believing that when educators feel valued, respected, and encouraged, children thrive naturally.
Under Alex’s leadership, Little Tyke strives to be one of the premier privately owned early education companies in Texas. The organization proudly emphasizes that it is not only family-owned, but also locally operated and locally loved. Alex lives and works in the very communities the company serves and remains actively involved in day-to-day operations, spending dedicated time each week visiting campuses, engaging with children, encouraging staff, and building meaningful relationships with families.
Today, Alex owns and operates 12 learning centers throughout Tarrant, Johnson, and Parker counties, employing more than 150 early education professionals. Little Tyke’s culture is strengthened by long-tenured, dedicated employees, with many team members serving the company for decades — a reflection of the family atmosphere, loyalty, and commitment that have defined the organization since 1972.
Alex remains passionate about preserving the traditional values of relationship-driven child care while continuing to move the company forward through innovation, leadership development, and a commitment to excellence in early care and education.
