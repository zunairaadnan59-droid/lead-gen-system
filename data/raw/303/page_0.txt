About
At One Health Boutique,
we bring personalized wellness and community together—creating a space where you can move, heal, and grow with intention.
Guided by our licensed chiropractors, Dr. Lan Moro and Dr. Matthew Kelly, our team integrates Chiropractic Care, Massage Therapy, and Corrective Exercise to deliver a modern, results-driven approach to your health.
Located in the heart of San Diego, our boutique includes a private chiropractic suite, infrared sauna, two relaxing massage rooms, and a spacious 2,800 sq. ft. training area designed for private personal training.
