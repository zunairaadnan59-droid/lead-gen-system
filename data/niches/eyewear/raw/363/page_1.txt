About PRO EYECARE in Greenwich
PRO EYECARE has a convenient location in the heart of Greenwich. We pride ourselves on offering a high level of family eye care for everyone. From eye exams to eye care products, we’re here to ensure that you get the vision support that you need.
Learn About Dr. Inna Lazar
When you visit us at PRO EYECARE, you’ll be greeted by Dr. Inna Lazar. The optometrist received her Bachelor of Science Degree in Psychology and minored in Biology. She, then, received her Doctor of Optometry Degree from Salus University.
Dr. Inna Lazar has done a significant amount of clinical training, covering everything from diagnosing and managing ocular diseases to pediatric and geriatric exams. She has trained with some of the top hospitals in the United States, including the Einstein Hospital in Philadelphia and The Eye Institute in Philadelphia.
Dr. Inna Lazar loves working with her patients throughout Connecticut, particularly children. She has also volunteered with the Head Start program, where eye exams and initial screenings are provided to children throughout Philadelphia. Dr. Lazar is passionate about eye care and continues to stay up on the latest trends. She’s married and loves spending time with her family. She plays tennis and loves to cook.
Explore Our Services
At PRO EYECARE, we’re here to provide you with a wide array of services. We provide pediatric eye care to ensure that children get their vision tested. If glasses are needed, we can identify the prescription and help to find frames that are capable of lasting throughout all of the different extracurricular activities.
Our family eye care services cover glasses, contact lens exams, and more. Should you be experiencing other problems, we’re here to help. We offer such services as:
Eye exams
Dry eye treatment
Ortho-K
Our friendly office staff will answer questions, recommend products, and talk to you about all that we can help with. It can provide you with confidence that you’re in good hands. When you’re ready to book with an optometrist, call our office to do so.
Book Your Eye Appointment Today
Vision is one of the most important parts of your overall health, influencing everything from daily comfort to long-term quality of life. Even small changes in your eyesight can affect reading, driving, screen use, and overall visual clarity. That’s why routine eye exams play a key role in maintaining healthy vision at every age.
At PRO EYECARE, we provide comprehensive eye exams and personalized care for patients in Darien and Greenwich, CT, whether you’re noticing blurry vision, need a new prescription, or are simply due for your annual checkup. Our team makes it easy to stay on top of your eye health with convenient scheduling and trusted providers.
