What We Do
Dry Eye Therapy
Dry Eye can be disruptive and life altering to your quality of life. Our office uses progressive technology and FDA approved treatments to help manage dry eyes such as Optilight.
Myopia Control
Did you know that it is possible to slow down the progression of myopia? At our office, myopia is treated as a disease that can be prevented from progressing to protect children's vision.
Preventative Eye Care
We provide comprehensive examination of your eye health, visual function, glasses, contact lenses, LASIK, custom contact lenses, cataracts and diabetic exams.
Comprehensive Eye Examinations
Your eye exam is the foundation of lifelong eye health. Our doctors evaluate vision, eye health, and early signs of conditions such as
glaucoma, macular degeneration, and cataracts
.
We use advanced diagnostic technology to ensure your prescription and eye health are carefully monitored.
Myopia Management
Myopia is becoming increasingly common in children and can progress quickly.
Our doctors provide specialized treatments designed to
slow myopia progression
and protect long-term vision using evidence-based myopia management strategies.
Dry Eye Treatment
Dry eye can cause irritation, blurred vision, and discomfort throughout the day. We provide advanced diagnostics and modern treatment options including
OptiLight IPL therapy
, customized to relieve symptoms and improve long-term eye comfort.
Contact Lens Services
We offer comprehensive contact lens fittings including daily lenses,
specialty lenses, toric lenses for astigmatism, multifocal lenses
, and custom lenses for patients with unique vision needs.
Medical Eye Care
Our doctors diagnose and treat a wide range of eye conditions including
red eyes, eye infections, styes, glaucoma, macular degeneration, and diabetic eye disease
, providing personalized medical care for your eyes.
Eyewear and Vision Correction
Our optical center offers a curated selection of designer frames and advanced lens technology.
Our team helps you choose glasses and lenses that provide optimal vision, comfort, and style.
We carry a wide range of brands.
