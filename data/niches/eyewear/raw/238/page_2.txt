About
Optical Expressions
— Premier Eye Care in Scottsdale & Phoenix, Arizona
At Optical Expressions, we believe eye care should feel personal, comfortable, and tailored to you. For decades, patients across Scottsdale and Phoenix have trusted our team to deliver a higher level of care that blends advanced technology with genuine relationships.
If you’re looking for a more thoughtful, boutique experience than what large corporate chains offer, you’ll find it here. From routine exams to premier treatments, we focus on helping you see clearly and feel confident.

Years of History
We’ve been part of the Arizona community for over 60 years, serving generations of families with consistent, high-quality care. What started as a vision to provide more personalized eye care has grown into a trusted name across Scottsdale and Phoenix.
Over the years, we’ve helped thousands of patients improve their vision, manage eye health conditions, and find eyewear that truly fits their lifestyle. Many of our patients return year after year because they know they’ll receive attentive, individualized care every time.
Today, with locations including Hilton Village, Willo, and our Biltmore office, we continue to build on that legacy while embracing modern advancements in eye care.
Our Mission & Values
Our mission is simple: provide personalized eye care that puts your needs first.
We take a different approach than large retail chains. Instead of rushing appointments or offering one-size-fits-all solutions, we focus on building long-term relationships and delivering care that feels thoughtful and thorough.
What that means for you:
You’ll have time to ask questions and understand your eye health.
We tailor your treatment plan specifically to your lifestyle.
You’ll receive honest recommendations, not sales pressure.
Your comfort and experience are always a priority.
Our licensed team is proud to be a part of the Arizona community and provide personalized care that helps you see clearly.

Optometrist Team

If you’re searching for a trusted optometrist in Phoenix, we deliver personalized care backed by years of hands-on experience and a commitment to upholding a national standard of care.

Whether you’re visiting for glasses, contact lenses, or a comprehensive eye health evaluation, Dr. Wilken focuses on creating a comfortable experience where you feel heard and supported every step of the way.

He focuses on delivering thorough, detail-oriented exams that go beyond basic vision checks, helping patients better understand their eye health and available treatment options.

Comprehensive
Eye Care Services
We offer various
optometry services
to our patients. Our number one goal is to help you get ahead of potential diseases or conditions that could affect your vision and overall health. The best way to do that is to start with a comprehensive exam, where our doctors can evaluate your eye health and recommend treatments to help correct or preserve your eyesight.
Routine Eye Care & Preventive Services
Regular eye care is the foundation of healthy vision. Our
comprehensive eye exams
go beyond checking your prescription; they evaluate your overall eye health and help detect conditions early, often before symptoms appear.
During your visit, we assess:
Vision clarity and prescription accuracy
Eye coordination and focus
Early signs of conditions like glaucoma or macular degeneration
Annual exams are one of the most effective ways to protect your long-term vision and maintain overall eye health. We also provide family eye care, making it easy for patients of all ages to receive reliable care in one place.

Contact Lenses & Specialty Vision Solutions
If you need a more customized solution, we offer
advanced contact lens fittings
designed for comfort and long-term wearability.
Our services include:
Standard contact lens exams
Specialty lenses for hard-to-fit eyes
Astigmatism and custom lens options
Orthokeratology (Ortho-K) for non-surgical vision correction
Because contact lenses sit directly on the eye, proper fit is essential. Our team uses detailed measurements and modern diagnostics to ensure your lenses feel comfortable and perform exactly as they should.

Advanced Eye Care & Medical Services
For patients who need more than routine care, we provide advanced services designed to treat specific conditions and improve overall eye health.
Dry Eye Treatment – Our TempSure dry eye treatment offers a non-invasive solution to relieve irritation and improve tear function.
Phoenix LASIK & Cataract Co-Management – If you’re considering LASIK surgery in Phoenix, we provide expert LASIK co-management to guide you through pre- and post-surgical care.
Myopia Management – We offer treatments designed to slow the progression of nearsightedness, especially in children.
Visual Field Testing – Helps detect changes in peripheral and central vision that may indicate underlying conditions.

We provide guidance and care for:

What makes Optical Expressions different from other eye care providers in Arizona?
