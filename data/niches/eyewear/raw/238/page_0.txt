Frequently Asked Questions
Do you accept VSP and other vision insurance plans?
Yes, our eye doctors accept VSP, EyeMed, and most major vision insurance plans. Contact us to verify your specific coverage and benefits before your appointment.
Can you see patients for eye emergencies and urgent conditions?
We provide same-day emergency eye care for conditions such as eye infections, injuries, sudden vision changes, and foreign objects in the eye. Call us immediately for urgent situations.
What designer eyeglass frames do you carry?
Our optical boutique features premium designer frames from leading brands, including options for every style and budget. If you’re looking for an eye doctor with designer frames near you, schedule a consultation to browse our complete selection of
designer eyeglasses
.
Do you provide comprehensive eye exams for children?
Yes, our
children’s eye care specialist
offers school vision screenings, early detection of vision problems, and child-friendly examination techniques.
How often should I have an eye exam?
Most adults need comprehensive vision exams every 1-2 years, while children and seniors may need more frequent checkups. Our eye doctors in Scottsdale and Phoenix will recommend the right schedule for your individual needs.
