About Paris Optique
Family owned and operated since 1986, we have been fortunate to service valley clients for over 30 years. Our intent remains the same as the day we opened: to provide Arizona’s consumers with the most fashion-forward, and luxurious eyewear on the market.
Paris Optique continues to be a truly independent optical store, offering a wide array of shapes, colors, and designs. Our unique assortment of exclusive brands, handmade eyewear, has something for everyone.
