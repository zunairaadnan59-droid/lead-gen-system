With State-of-the Art Eye Care
The Eye Care Center of Napa Valley is a comprehensive ophthalmology practice with three convenient locations across Napa Valley and Northern California. Our board-certified Ophthalmologists and Optometrists are highly skilled and experienced in the diagnosis, treatment and prevention of eye disorders, and place clear, healthy vision as our top priority.

Cataracts
Cataracts
Clouding of the normally clear lens of the eye
LASIK & Refractive Surgeries
LASIK & Refractive Surgeries
Vision correcting procedures performed in-house (LASIK) or during cataract surgery
Glaucoma
Glaucoma
A disease affecting the optic nerve that increases eye pressure which can lead to blindness
Macular Degeneration
Macular Degeneration
A disease of the macula that causes blurred vision or a blind spot in the visual field.
Diabetic Retinopathy
Diabetic Retinopathy
A complication of diabetes, this disease damages blood vessels in the tissue at the back of the eye.
Dry Eyes
Dry Eyes
A condition where tears aren’t able to provide adequate lubrication for the eyes.
Keratoconus
Keratoconus
An eye disease where the cornea thins and gradually bulges outward into a cone shape
Oculoplastics Surgery
Oculoplastics Surgery
Treatment of disorders of the eyelids, tear drainage system and orbit/eye socket.
Glasses & Contacts
Glasses & Contacts
Corrects or improves refractive vision errors such as nearsightedness, farsightedness or other.
