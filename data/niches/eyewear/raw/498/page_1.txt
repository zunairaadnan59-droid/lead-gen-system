Meet The Doctor
Hi! I’m Dr. Chin and I was born and raised in Spring, Texas. In middle school, I failed a vision screening at school and had my first visit with the local neighborhood optometrist. I still remember my first eye exam and my fascination with the phoropter (the machine we use when we ask “1 or 2?”).
