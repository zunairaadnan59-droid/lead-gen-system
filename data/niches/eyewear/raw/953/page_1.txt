About Ballard Optical
Founded in the late 1970s, Ballard Optical has been a neighborhood fixture on Market Street for over 40 years. Our commitment to offering world-class eye care in a small, intimate setting continues stronger than ever before.
Dr. Harshani Amaraweera is excited and honored to serve the community together with her experienced team of Optometrists, Opticians and Administrators.
We offer convenient morning and evening appointments Monday-Thursday. We accept most major vision and medical plans.
