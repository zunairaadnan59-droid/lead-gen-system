About Our Store
Since its inception in 1927, Cohen’s Fashion Optical has flourished into one of the East Coast’s most beloved destinations for both value and designer eyewear. We take great pride in offering Suffolk County residents a personalized, full-service eye care journey.
Our dedicated eyewear stylists are committed to helping you discover the ideal eyeglasses, sunglasses, or contact lenses to suit your needs. Featuring a plethora of high-end luxury brands such as Gucci, Bvlgari, and Chloé, alongside sporty designer frames from Nautica and Lacoste, our selection caters to diverse tastes and preferences. Additionally, we boast an array of value frames and numerous special offers to cater to every budget. Once you’ve chosen your frames, we’ll complement them with cutting-edge lens technology to elevate your eyewear experience.
Our seasoned eye doctor is on hand to conduct comprehensive eye exams and address any vision-related concerns you may have. We strive to make your experience seamless by accepting most insurance plans. Conveniently located near I-25 and various other nearby thoroughfares, our store is easily accessible by car. Schedule your eye exam online and visit us today!
