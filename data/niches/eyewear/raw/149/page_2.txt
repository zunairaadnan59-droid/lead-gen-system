About Us
Our Practice
Pacific Eye Associates has served San Francisco since 1985. Our founding idea is simple: patients with complex eye conditions should be able to see multiple specialists under one roof, without traveling from office to office. That’s still how we practice — from routine eye exams and same-day urgent care to advanced surgical procedures, all in one place.
Our Physicians
Pacific Eye Associates is home to 9 ophthalmologists and 2 optometrists, each board-certified, fellowship-trained, and sub-specialized in a specific area of eye care — including retina, glaucoma, cornea, uveitis, and oculofacial surgery. Many trained at institutions including Stanford, UCSF, Johns Hopkins, and the Cleveland Clinic.
That breadth means Pacific Eye Associates can treat nearly every eye care need in a single practice: LASIK, cataract surgery, corneal transplant, refractive surgery, glaucoma, dry eye, uveitis, ocular infections and inflammatory eye disease, macular degeneration and other retinal disease, diabetic eye care, eye movement disorders, and oculofacial plastic surgery. Our physicians combine academic training with current surgical techniques and technologies to deliver care shaped by both experience and evidence.
Our Team
Every member of our staff works toward one standard: treat each patient the way we’d want our own family treated. From your first phone call to your last follow-up, our team is here to make the experience as seamless and comfortable as possible.
