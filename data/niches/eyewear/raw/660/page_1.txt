About Central Square Eye Care:
We offer comprehensive eye examinations for children, adults, and senior citizens. Optometrists specialize in maintaining the optimal function of the human eye by treating conditions or diseases that can adversely affect your vision, many of which don’t have obvious symptoms. Dr. White takes a holistic approach to eye care -- accounting for your age, health factors, and lifestyle and hobbies. We take the time to discuss the results of each exam with you, understand your vision challenges, and to tailor treatment to your specific needs.
Our goal is to provide personalized eye exams in a comfortable atmosphere. We offer a convenient and expansive schedule to accommodate the busy lives of professionals and residents of the Cambridge area.
Why Choose Central Square Eye Care?
Experience
Central Square Eye Care has been in business since 2003! Our doctor have over 30 years of experience in the field and have worked extensively in both private and group practice settings.  We draw from that wealth of expertise and knowledge to give our patients top tier treatment and service.
Our optician, George Skelton, has an equally impressive background with decades of experience in crafting, styling, and dispensing prescription eyewear!
Quality
We have hand-selected all of our frames, lens materials, and contact lenses to ensure that our patients will have top quality products at a reasonable price. Our practice has added a finishing lab that enables us to cut your prescription lenses on-site for expedient service.
Community
At Central Square Eye Care we pride ourselves on the long-standing relationships we’ve built with our patients. While our book of clientele is ever-expanding, we’ve been faithfully serving many of the same individuals and families for over two decades. Customer satisfaction is our top priority and we would love to have you as part of our practice and our community!
Dr. Geoff White, OD
Dr. Geoffrey A. White graduated from Montclair State College in 1992 with a B.A. degree in biology.  He received his Doctor of Optometry (O.D.) from the New England College of Optometry in Boston in 1996.  He has opened Central Square Eye Care in Cambridge, MA and specializes in primary and medical eye care and hard to fit contact lens patients.  His philosophy is to spend quality time with each patient to make sure they are properly cared for.  Dr. White resides nearby with his two daughters. Dr. White enjoys soccer, hiking and watching his daughters horseback riding.
