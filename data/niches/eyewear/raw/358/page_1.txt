WHAT WE DO?
We pride ourselves in delivering friendly, reliable and knowledgeable services to all of our patients.
For over 25 year Dr. Brownstein has been providing comprehensive family eye care to the greater-Greenwich area. Full services are available at this location, including eye examinations, complete eyeglass services, contact lens fitting/replacements, as well as therapeutic management of ocular diseases. We pride ourselves in delivering friendly, reliable and knowledgeable services to all of our patients.
At Dr. Brownstein's office our patients' visual needs are our top priority. We spend the time necessary to teach patients how to wear and care for their contact lenses, whether they are beginners or updating to bifocal lenses.
A large assortment of contact lenses- from all the major contact companies- featuring brands such as Acuvue, Freshlook Color, Purevision, Biomedics and more! Dr. Brownstein is also able get emergency contact lenses, should a patient need them, in a short amount of time (usually 24 hours).
A variety of frame selections at reasonable prices- including MARCHON, NAUTICA,  VALENTINO, FLEXON, CALVIN KLEIN, NIKE,RAY BAN, PRADA,TIFFANY,BROOKS BROTHERS  and many others. In addition, many options of lenses are available to meet patients' visual requirements and to accommodate frame selections. In such instances that multiple pairs of glasses are required or desired, discounts are available
