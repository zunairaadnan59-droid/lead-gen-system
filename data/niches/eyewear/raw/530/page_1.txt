Contact Lenses
Enjoy low prices on brand name contact lenses from FramesDirect. We offer contacts by trusted makers to correct a multitude of vision problems, including near- or farsightedness, astigmatism, or bifocal or multifocal vision.
Top Brand Contact Lenses
Browse contact lenses by Acuvue by Johnson & Johnson, Biofinity by CooperVision, Air Optix by CIBA VISION, DAILIES by Alcon, PureVision Multi-Focal by Bausch + Lomb, and many others. Choose from daily or multi-day disposable contact lenses packaged in the quantities you need. We also offer color contact lenses that allow you to safely change the color of your eyes for a fresh, new look, but still keep your eyes oxygenated all day.
Give your eyes the comfort they deserve, and enjoy the optical clarity you need wearing brand name contact lenses from FramesDirect.
Ordering Contact Lenses From FramesDirect
As an authorized retailer, FramesDirect sells only guaranteed authentic brand name contact lenses. Our customer service team is staffed by trained opticians, all of them dedicated to helping you find the right brand or type. You’ll need a prescription for any type of contact lenses you order, even if you’re simply changing your look with color lenses.
