Our Services
Eye Exams
At Vanderbilt Optical, Dr. Jason Eugenides performs routine eye exams for both children and adults. In just one short appointment you can ensure the health and safety of your eyes, update your prescription, and resolve any symptoms that may be bothering you. A visit also includes a detailed imaging of the back of your eyes with an in house retina camera, so no eyedrops!
Learn More
Eyeglasses
Do you have trouble seeing objects either close up or far away? If your eyesight is fading, you might experience headaches, blurry vision or dry eyes. At Vanderbilt Optical, we offer same-day single vision glasss for all of our patients. With our in house lab, we can cut any prescription you have into the glasses you own, or want to pick out with our wonderful staff.
Browse Frames
Contacts
Are you having trouble with your vision but don’t want to wear glasses? To experience what it’s like wearing invisible, comfortable contact lenses, visit Dr. Jason Eugenides at Vanderbilt Optical. We offer spherical, multifocal, toric, and hard contact lenses. With a wide range of lenses to choose from, you’re sure to find the perfect match and achieve perfect vision once again.
