About Our Store
At Cohen’s Fashion Optical, we uphold an unwavering commitment to excellence, delivering unparalleled eye-care services alongside an extensive range of both value and luxury eyeglasses.
Our dedicated team of eyewear specialists is ready to assist you in discovering frames that complement your unique style. Explore our top-selling brands, featuring esteemed designers like Gucci, Salvatore Ferragamo, Tom Ford, and more.
Furthermore, our specialists will skillfully match your lenses with cutting-edge lens technologies tailored to your lifestyle and vision needs.
Conveniently nestled in Town Center at Boca Raton, we’re just a quick drive away from Florida’s Turnpike and I-95. To better serve you, our location is open seven days a week. Feel free to drop by or schedule an eye exam online with our skilled Optometrist.
