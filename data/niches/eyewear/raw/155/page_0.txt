About Eversee Boutique Eyecare
Eversee Boutique Eyecare provides the highest quality, compassionate optometry care to ensure that your family enjoys a long, happy, and healthy life.
​​​​​​​See Better. Feel Better. Look Better.
Eversee Boutique Eyecare opened in the fall of 2022 in Winnetka, IL. It is a family-owned business dedicated to improving the eyecare and eyewear of its community of patients. Eversee Boutique Eyecare focus’ on general, medical, and neuro-optometry, as well as on high-quality, unique eyewear.
Many people think of optometry as having two main components: eyesight and eye health. These are two important aspects of optometry that are the foundation of Eversee Boutique Eyecare. We also focus on an often-overlooked component, visual processing. Visual processing evaluations can exceedingly improve the quality of life for patients experiencing chronic, acute, neurological, and developmental symptoms and conditions.
At Eversee Boutique Eyecare, every patient will receive an individualized treatment plan geared to their needs.
