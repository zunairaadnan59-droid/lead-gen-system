Optical Solution Optometry is an eyecare office in Los Angeles, CA with comprehensive eye exams, contact lens fitting, and treatment services.  We’ve proudly served the greater Los Angeles area for over 10 years and look forward to many more.
Our mission is to provide our patients with high quality eyecare personalized to their unique needs.  Dr. Leyen Lim operates her practice with a patients-first philosophy and her patients are her top priority.
Dr.  Leyen  Lim
Dr. Leyen Lim is a board certified optometrist practicing in the city of Los Angeles.  She received her Bachelor of Science in Psychobiology as an undergraduate and went on to earn her Doctor of Optometry Degree from the Pennsylvania College of Optometry in 2002.  She is board certified to treat and manage ocular disease as defined by the California State Board of Optometry.
​
Her special interest is in hard to fit contact lenses, such as keratoconus and multifocals.
She also co-manages cataract and LASIK procedures with outstanding surgeons.
She
welcomes any questions her patients may have concerning their vision and ocular
health.
