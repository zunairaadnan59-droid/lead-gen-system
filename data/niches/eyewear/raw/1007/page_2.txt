How to Redeem Your Rebate
How to Redeem Your CooperVision Rebate
Choose your product:
Select your qualifying CooperVision lens family, such as MyDay®, clariti® 1 day, Biofinity®, or MiSight® 1 day.
Select your supply:
Choose the eligible supply tier for your product, such as a 6-month supply or 12-month supply, where available.
Confirm wearer status:
Identify whether the patient is a New Wearer or Existing Wearer. New Wearer rebates require the patient to be new to wearing the specified brand or customer-branded equivalent.
Use the correct rebate code:
COMFORTNW26 for New Wearer rebates
COMFORTCW26 for Existing Wearer rebates
Submit your rebate claim:
Submit or track your rebate at CooperVisionPromotions.com. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the lens purchase date.
Provide claim information:
To locate or track a claim, use the patient’s email address, mailing address, or phone number.
Receive your reward:
Qualified patients will receive an email with the option to request either a physical prepaid card or a virtual voucher to use online.

How Do Contact Lens Rebates Work?
Many contact lens manufacturers offer rebates on eligible lens purchases. These savings may depend on the brand, product, purchase date, quantity purchased, and where you bought your lenses.
When you purchase contact lenses through , you may qualify for valuable manufacturer rebates from leading contact lens brands. This page is designed to help you quickly find available
contact lens rebates
, including offers for
Alcon contact lenses
, ACUVUE, CooperVision, and Bausch & Lomb products.
partners with leading contact lens manufacturers, including Johnson & Johnson and Alcon, to help patients access strong savings opportunities. These preferred partner rebates may offer added value when you purchase eligible contact lenses through a office.
Is My Contact Lens Purchase Eligible for a Rebate?
You may qualify for a rebate if you recently purchased eligible contact lenses through an authorized eyecare provider and meet the manufacturer’s offer requirements.
You may be eligible if you:
Purchased qualifying contact lenses
Bought within the eligible rebate period
Have proof of purchase
Submit before the deadline
Follow the manufacturer’s claim instructions
Contact Lens Rebate FAQs
Who is eligible for a contact lens rebate?
Eligibility varies by manufacturer, product, purchase date, and purchase quantity. Review the rebate details carefully before submitting your claim.
Where do I need to purchase my contact lenses to qualify?
Many rebate offers require purchase through an authorized eyecare provider. Check the specific rebate terms to confirm eligibility.
Where can I find Alcon, ACUVUE, CooperVision, and Bausch & Lomb rebates?
You can browse available rebates on this page, including offers for Alcon contact lenses, Acuvue contact lens rebates, Coopervision contact lens rebates, and Bausch & Lomb contact lens rebates.
How long do I have to submit my rebate?
Submission deadlines vary by offer. Be sure to review the rebate form or manufacturer website for the exact deadline.
How will I receive my rebate?
Reimbursement methods vary by manufacturer and may include a prepaid card, digital reward, or other payment method.
What documents do I need?
You may need your purchase receipt, product packaging, UPC codes, prescription information, or other proof of purchase.
