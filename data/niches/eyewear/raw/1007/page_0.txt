What We Do
Your Family's Trusted Eye Doctor Practice
We want Solano Eye Care to serve as your family’s eye care practice for life. We work to earn that trust through investment in new technology for clinical eye care, continuing medical education, a large inventory of contact lenses, an optical boutique offering hundreds of eyeglass frames to choose from, and an on-site lab that allows us to provide quicker service.
Eye Disease Management
We prioritize the early detection and ongoing management of eye diseases such as glaucoma, macular degeneration, and diabetic retinopathy. Managing your eye health effectively helps prevent progression and preserves your vision.
Myopia Management
Solano Eye Care utilizes the latest techniques to slow the progression of nearsightedness in both children and adults. Our methods include specially designed contact lenses and eye drops tailored to each patient's unique needs.
Corneal Refractive Therapy (CRT)
CRT is a non-surgical option that allows you to see clearly during the day without the need for glasses or contact lenses. These specially designed therapeutic lenses gently reshape the cornea while you sleep, providing clear vision upon waking.

Contact Lens Fittings in Fairfield
Learn More About Specialty Lenses
At Solano Eye Care, we fit specialty contact lenses for irregular corneal conditions.
Our expertise includes scleral lenses for keratoconus and other corneal issues, providing customized solutions for each patient. Scleral lenses offer superior comfort and vision correction, ensuring that even those with the most challenging conditions can experience clear, stable vision and improved quality of life.
Additionally, we offer detailed guidance on lens care and maintenance, enabling you to enjoy a seamless and comfortable contact lens experience.
