Pediatric and adolescent care
Our team of doctors takes care of your children from birth to their teenage years. We give check-ups and specialty care to the families in our community, vaccinations and so much more.
NeighborHealth follows immunization guidelines from the American Academy of Pediatrics and the Massachusetts Department of Public Health.

Emergency care
Emergency care
Our Emergency Room is open 24/7, year round. Our primary care doctors know about your emergency visits, and make sure you get the care you need. If you require specialized care, we can arrange for you to be transferred to another department or a nearby hospital.

Community care & resources
Your health needs don’t end when you leave our centers. We offer many wellness resources that connect you and your family with the right services. From food, housing, and childcare to immigration, financial help, and legal assistance, our dedication to our neighbor’s health goes beyond our clinics.
