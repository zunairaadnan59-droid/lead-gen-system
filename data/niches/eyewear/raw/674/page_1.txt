About NeighborHealth
Providing care
beyond the clinic
Optimal health means caring not only for you but for all of us. That’s why, since 1970, NeighborHealth has taken care of you far beyond the clinic. We don’t just serve the community: we belong to it. And we want to make it better. We’re not just providers. We’re partners in health who are committed to taking down barriers and bringing the right care for all.
See what sets us apart.
We believe health care is better in community. For over 50 years we have been dedicated to providing high-quality health care to everyone who needs it.

Diversity, equity, and inclusion
