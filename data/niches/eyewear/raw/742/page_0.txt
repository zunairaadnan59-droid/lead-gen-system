Eye Care Services
LASIK Eye Surgery
LASIK (Laser-Assisted In Situ Keratomileusis) is a revolutionary vision correction procedure that reshapes the cornea using advanced laser technology. This outpatient surgery permanently corrects refractive errors like myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism, allowing patients to reduce or eliminate their dependence on glasses and contact lenses. The quick, minimally invasive procedure typically takes less than 15 minutes per eye, with most patients experiencing improved vision almost immediately and achieving 20/20 vision or better.
Cataract Surgery
Cataract surgery is a precise microsurgical procedure that removes the eye’s clouded natural lens and replaces it with a clear artificial intraocular lens (IOL). As cataracts develop with age, causing progressive vision loss, this surgery restores clarity and visual function. Modern techniques involve small incisions, ultrasound-based lens removal, and customized IOL options, including multifocal and astigmatism-correcting lenses. The outpatient procedure typically takes 15-20 minutes and offers rapid recovery with minimal discomfort.
Cornea
Corneal services address conditions affecting the eye’s clear, front surface responsible for focusing light. These specialized treatments include corneal transplants, treatment for keratoconus, corneal infections, corneal dystrophies, and refractive surgeries. Advanced techniques like phototherapeutic keratectomy (PTK) and corneal cross-linking help restore vision, manage degenerative conditions, and prevent further vision loss through precise, microsurgical interventions.
Glaucoma
Glaucoma management focuses on preventing and controlling increased intraocular pressure that can damage the optic nerve and lead to irreversible vision loss. Comprehensive services include diagnostic screening, advanced imaging technologies, medical treatments, laser therapies, and surgical interventions. Treatment strategies range from prescription eye drops and laser procedures to minimally invasive glaucoma surgery (MIGS) and traditional surgical techniques, tailored to each patient’s specific condition and risk factors.
Retina
Retinal services address complex conditions affecting the light-sensitive tissue at the back of the eye. Specialists diagnose and treat issues like diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, retinal detachments, and inherited retinal diseases. Advanced diagnostic tools and treatments include optical coherence tomography (OCT), intravitreal injections, laser treatments, and microsurgical procedures designed to preserve and restore vision in patients with challenging retinal conditions.
Dry Eyes
Dry eye treatment provides comprehensive management for this common ocular surface disorder characterized by insufficient tear production or poor tear quality. Services include advanced diagnostic testing, personalized treatment plans involving prescription medications, punctal plugs, intense pulsed light (IPL) therapy, and lifestyle recommendations. Cutting-edge treatments like regenerative therapies and specialized eye drops help alleviate symptoms, improve comfort, and prevent potential complications.
Cosmetics
Ophthalmic cosmetic services combine medical expertise with aesthetic enhancements, addressing both functional and visual concerns. Offerings include cosmetic eyelid surgery (blepharoplasty) to correct drooping or puffy eyelids, and Latisse for eyelash growth. These services are performed by specialized oculoplastic surgeons who understand the delicate anatomy of the eye and surrounding structures.
Optical Shops and Contact Lenses
The optical shop provides comprehensive vision correction solutions, offering a wide range of high-quality eyewear and contact lens options. Expert opticians help patients select frames that complement their facial features and lifestyle, while contact lens specialists provide personalized fittings for various vision needs. Services include comprehensive eye examinations, prescription verification, advanced contact lens technologies, and expert guidance on maintaining optimal eye health and visual comfort.
