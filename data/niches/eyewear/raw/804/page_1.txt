Miami International Mall is a family-friendly international shopping experience located in Doral, providing a gathering place for consumers to shop, dine and be entertained in a safe, comfortable environment. The mall is home to select retailers like Macy's The Women's and Kid's Store, Macy's Men's and Home, JCPenney and coming soon, Elev8 Fun.  Additionally, Miami International Mall features twenty-three eateries including BJ's Restaurant & Brewhouse, Flor de Sal, 100 Montaditos and The Knife, and offers more than 120 of the latest retail and fashion brands, including American Eagle Outfitters, H&M, Mango, Pandora, Swarovski, Tous, and Victoria's Secret | PINK.
Miami International Mall also offers visitors unparalleled ease of access, including plentiful parking options. Miami International Mall spans more than 1 million square feet and is visited by more than 12 million guests each year.

By Car
Miami International Mall is located seven miles from the Miami International Airport, and is also near the Port of Miami.  Located in Doral, one of the fastest-growing business communities in the nation, Miami International Mall is at the intersection of SR 836 and NW 107th Avenue.
Car
From the North
Florida Turnpike South - Homestead Extension to Exit 27 (NW 12 St) Head East to NW 107
th
Avenue.
From the South
Florida Turnpike North to 836 West (Dolphin Expwy). Merge onto NW 107 Avenue toward FL-985 N.
From Miami Beach
Take 836 West  (Dolphin Expwy). Merge onto NW 107 Avenue toward FL-985 N.
Bus
Metrobus-Public Transit:
Fares are $2 each way, exact change only. Bus-to-rail transfers cost $.50 each. If you are a senior citizen 65 years and over, or a Social Security beneficiary and are a permanent Miami-Dade resident, you are eligible to ride transit free with a Golden Passport EASY Card. Metrobuses operate Monday-Friday 4:30 AM-2:13 AM.
305-770-3131
Greyhound
operates 4 stations in Greater Miami & the Beaches; N. Miami Beach, Downtown Miami, Miami Int'l Airport area & Homestead. 1-800-231-2222
Train
Metrorail
:
21 mile elevated rail system which serves Downtown Miami and extends W. to Hialeah & S. to Kendall, with stops approximately every mile. Fares are $2 each way. (Please note: does not arrive directly at the mall)

Security & Police Substation
The goal of Miami International Mall Security Department is to maintain a safe and pleasant shopping environment for our visitors and tenants. Some of the ways security is working for you:
Year-round vehicle patrols in the parking lot.
Year-round foot patrols throughout the mall.
Assist in locating lost vehicles.
Assist in locating lost loved ones.
Escort visitors and tenants anytime upon request.

Wireless (Wi-Fi) Internet
We offer free wireless internet to our shoppers at this Simon shopping center. Any shopper with a Wi-Fi enabled laptop, tablet or smartphone can easily log onto the Wi-Fi network. All shoppers have to do is open their web browser or select "#Free Simon Wi-Fi" (the SSID name) from their available list of wireless networks to get to the Simon Malls Wi-Fi landing page. All shoppers that choose to use the Wi-Fi network at this mall must agree to Simon Wi-Fi Terms & Conditions of Use prior to using this free service.
