About Us
Welcome to Austin Optometry Group
Professional Family Eye Care For Over 40 Years
It is our goal to provide our patients with the most comprehensive eye care available with integrity and respect. Our office strives to provide excellence in each patient encounter.
We provide comprehensive eye exams and medical treatment for ocular disease, injuries, and a myriad of emergency situations. The extensive selection of designer frames in our optical boutique appeal to both the practical minded and the diverse. Over the past 40 years we’ve earned an outstanding reputation for fitting contact lenses from the straightforward to very complex. We are privileged to provide contact lens services and technical support for Austin’s movie and television industry.
Our doctors work hand in hand with our carefully chosen physicians in co-managing care for our patients undergoing cutting edge refractive and cataract surgeries. Our office utilizes advanced diagnostic technologies which include Corneal Topography, Retinal Photography, HD Optical Coherence Tomography, and Humphrey Visual Field Analysis to assist in complete analysis and diagnosis. We think your vision is important and strive for the highest quality care.
