Where is Sharp Rees-Stealy Downtown Optical Shop located?
Sharp Rees-Stealy Downtown Optical Shop is located in San Diego, California. Our
downtown location
is a convenient option for those living and working in downtown San Diego, Gaslamp, East Village, Little Italy and surrounding communities.
What services are available at Sharp Rees-Stealy Downtown Optical Shop?
We offer a range of optical services — from comprehensive eye examinations to eyewear and contact lens service.
Our certified opticians will help you find the perfect pair of glasses. Our optical shop features more than 400 different styles of eyeglass frames for men, women and children. We also offer the Sharpeyes Contact Lens Service.
Can I get contact lenses at Sharp Rees-Stealy Downtown Optical Shop?
If you've already been fit for contact lenses by a Sharp Rees-Stealy doctor, our
MySharpEyes Contact Lens Service
allows you to order your contact lenses and have them shipped directly to your door for little-to-no cost.
Give us a call at
858-526-6034
to order your contacts.
Do I need an appointment to visit Sharp Rees-Stealy Downtown Optical Shop?
Patients are seen by appointment only.
Schedule a routine eye exam online
or
call us to schedule an appointment to visit the Sharp Rees-Stealy Downtown Optical Shop.
What insurance plans are accepted at Sharp Rees-Stealy Downtown Optical Shop?
At Sharp Rees-Stealy, we honor most vision plans and offer competitive pricing so that you don't have to worry about lack of coverage or hidden fees.
Our covered in-network insurance plans include:
Vision Service Plan (VSP)
EyeMed
Medical Eye Services (MES)
Spectra
Vision Care Plan (VCP)
Buying a second pair of glasses will save you 30 percent off regular prices if purchased within 30 days of the first pair of glasses.
