About Eye Clinic of Austin
A part of the Austin community for over 30 years, Eye Clinic of Austin is one of Central Texas’ leading eye care and eye surgery centers.
Dr. Thomas Henderson founded the Eye Clinic of Austin in 1982 with a commitment to use the most advanced eye technology. The clinic welcomed
Farhan A. Irshad
to the team in May 2018. Seeing the clinic’s commitment to excellent eye care, Dr. Irshad knew it would be the perfect practice for him to join. Dr. Henderson retired in September 2018 and handed the reins of the clinic over to Dr. Irshad. Today, under Dr. Irshad’s leadership, Eye Clinic of Austin continues its founding commitment to excellence by providing the most cutting-edge technologies available including
LASIK
,
laser cataract surgery
, and
corneal transplants
.
Eye Clinic of Austin also provides comprehensive eye care including routine
eye exams
and we are certified as an accredited
Dry Eye Center
.
With unparalleled experience plus the latest technology, we strive to give our patients the absolute best results possible.
