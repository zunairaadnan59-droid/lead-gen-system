At Travis Family Optometry, Our Focus is You!
Dr. Huey O.D, Dr. Chih-Don Hsiao O.D, and the entire staff at Travis Family Optometry are thankful for the opportunity to take part in your family’s eye care needs. We care about your eye health, and we believe in providing the best vision care possible. Our office uses the latest in technology and takes pride in helping patients make the most informed eyewear decisions to find your optimal vision solution.
We recognize you have a choice for your eye care needs, and want to express our sincerest thanks for choosing us at Travis Family Optometry. We are committed to exceeding all your expectations.
Meet the Doctors
Dr. Larry Huey O.D and Dr. Chih-Don Hsiao O.D take pride in providing patients with the one-on-one interaction required to solve eye health and vision problems.
Dr. Huey O.D and Dr. Chih-Don Hsiao O.D enjoy caring for the patients in Fairfield, CA and the surrounding communities. Dr. Huey O.D, Dr. Chih-Don Hsiao O.D, and the entire staff at Travis Family Optometry are dedicated to providing excellent eye care services to your entire family.
About Our Staff
Your eye care and experience as a patient is what Travis Family Optometry is focused on. All of our staff members are well trained in their field of expertise and attend continuing education classes throughout the year.
The staff of Travis Family Optometry creates an office culture designed for excellent patient care and personalized service.
