More About Us
Surgical & Medical Eye Care
Specialized, comprehensive eye care right here, in Boca Raton. With an emphasis on quality eye care, we are committed to uncompromising service to our patients and the communities we serve, as it will be the standard by which the success of our practice is measured.
Vision & Optical Services
In addition to comprehensive eye care, we also have a full service optical onsite. Our opticians can assist you in finding just the right frames to fit your lifestyle and needs. We offer top quality frames and a broad selection of designer brands.
Special Services
Each of our doctors concentrates in specific areas of eye care that are of special interest to them and for which they have had additional training and experience. Our eye care services extend beyond routine exams to cataract, refractive lens replacement, retina, glaucoma, pediatric and neuro-ophthalmology, oculoplastic, reconstructive surgery and optometric care. We provide contact lens fittings and care and have a complete optical showroom for all your eyeglass needs.
