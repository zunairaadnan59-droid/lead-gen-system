Truly Personalized Eye Care in Norwalk, CT
Led by Dr. Scott Spector, Spector Eye Care has been treating patients since 1989. We have developed a team of eye specialists and a comprehensive collection of state-of-the-art diagnostic technologies. Wide-field retinal photography, special diagnostic equipment for the cornea and the front of the eye, and special lens evaluation materials that assist in lens-based eye surgery. The Spector Eye Care team offers world class quality eye care in a local and personalized environment.
We welcome you to our growing family of happy patients.

Our Ophthalmology Practice
Spector Eye Care is an ophthalmic provider for comprehensive medical, surgical and routine eye care. We are a group of doctors, technicians, medical assistants and patient care specialists dedicated to providing top quality eye care services using the latest technology. We are committed to helping preserve and improve the sight of the people in our region. Take a look around to learn more about your vision and discover how you can experience life more clearly.
