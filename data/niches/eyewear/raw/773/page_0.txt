Trusted for 50 Years in Optical Properties Characterization
Surface Optics offers a single engineering and manufacturing source for solutions requiring the application, characterization, or control of the optical properties of surfaces.
Founded in 1977 to provide optical property measurement services, today Surface Optics Corporation (SOC) specializes in advanced imaging systems, scientific instruments and thin film vacuum coatings for surface engineering.
SOC is pioneering innovative solutions for our research, aerospace, defense, semiconductor, automotive, and energy customers worldwide.
