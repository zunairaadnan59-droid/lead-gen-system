Welcome to Today’s Vision
Eyes are important indicators of overall health, and comprehensive eye care goes beyond a prescription for glasses or contact lenses.
At Today’s Vision, we take the time to get to know you, your eye care history, and your vision needs.
Our optometrist provides the expert care, advice, options, and follow up you need whether you are a new patient or an existing one. We are pleased to welcome you to our optometry practice in Austin, Texas.
Today’s Vision commitment is not just in meeting your expectations, but in exceeding them!
We know how much your eye health and appearance can mean to the quality of your life, so we are committed to excellence in servicing your complete eye care needs. Our office specializes in difficult to fit glasses and contact lenses.
Dr Mark Chavana is a therapuetic optometrist specializing in lasik correction, cataract surgery, glaucoma and infections of the eye.
Our office accepts most insurances including Vision Service Plan, VSP, Eyemed, Superior Vision, Spectera, Aetna, Humana, United Health Care, and Blue Cross Blue Shield, BCBS.
