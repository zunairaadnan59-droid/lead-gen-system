About Southampton Optiks
Looking for a trusted optometrist in Southampton, NY? Since 1975, Southampton Optiks has helped families across the East End protect their vision with full-scope, compassionate eye care rooted in clinical excellence and community values.

Established in 1975 & Still Family-Focused
We’ve been part of the Hamptons community for nearly 50 years. Patients who came to us as children now bring their own kids for eye exams. That continuity means everything and it’s why we focus on long-term, relationship-driven care.

A Hamptons Optometrist That Feels Like Home
Our office doesn’t feel like a clinic, it feels like part of the neighborhood. We’ve decorated our space with coastal art, soft colors, and a warm welcome. It’s quiet, calm, and designed to make you feel at ease from the moment you arrive.

Involved, Local, & Community-Minded
Our commitment goes beyond the exam room. We proudly support the Lions Club of the East End, helping provide vision care to underserved residents. For us, success is defined by community impact, not luxury labels or high-end aesthetics.

Led by Dr. Calabrese & Team-Based Care
Our model prioritizes teamwork over individual bios. At Southampton Optiks, you’ll be seen by a knowledgeable, friendly provider who knows your history and treats you like a person, not a number. We believe good care starts with good relationships.

Serving Southampton & the East End
While we’re based in Southampton, our patients come from Montauk, East Hampton, Amagansett, Sag Harbor, and Bridgehampton. Whether it’s your child’s first exam or ongoing care for a chronic condition, we’ll be here with clear answers and personalized solutions.
