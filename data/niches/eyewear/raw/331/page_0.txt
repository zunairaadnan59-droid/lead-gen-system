Trusted Eye Care in Southampton, NY for over 50 years
Welcome to Southampton Optiks, where multigenerational families across the Hamptons turn for dependable eye care. From your child’s first exam to managing complex conditions, we’re here to help you see clearly, and comfortably every step of the way.

Eye Exams
»
Our comprehensive eye exams check vision, screen for eye disease, and include advanced imaging,  helping you protect long-term eye health at any age.
Dry Eye
»
We treat dry eye symptoms like irritation and fatigue using medical-grade drops and punctal plugs. No fads, just tailored relief that fits your lifestyle.
Contact Lens Exams
»
We fit contacts for all eyes, including astigmatism and keratoconus. Exams include corneal mapping and tear analysis to ensure a comfortable fit.
Same-Day Glasses, Right Here in Southampton
Need new glasses in a hurry? Our in-office optical lab lets many patients receive their eyewear the same day  without compromising quality or style. Browse our curated frame collection, featuring designer brands like Tom Ford, Versace, and Oakley, and enjoy expert fittings by our optical team.

Pediatric & Myopia Care from a Team You Trust
Early vision care is key, and our team is experienced in supporting children and teens with everything from routine checkups to myopia management using NaturalVue lenses and atropine therapy. With a family-friendly approach and care tailored to every age, we’re here to support your child’s eye health at every stage.

A Longstanding Practice, Rooted in the Hamptons
Founded in 1975, Southampton Optiks has earned the trust of families throughout Southampton, Montauk, East Hampton, and beyond. With a commitment to accessible, practical care and strong community ties including our work with the local Lions Club, we’re proud to serve as the Hamptons’ go-to eye care provider.

Cataract Co-Management
»
We provide pre- and post-operative cataract care in partnership with trusted local surgeons. Our team helps you navigate surgery preparation and supports your healing journey, so you can feel confident every step of the way.
LASIK/PRK Co-Management
»
We co-manage LASIK and PRK procedures, offering expert referrals and thorough follow-up care. If you’re wondering whether LASIK is right for you, we’ll walk you through your options.
Presbyopia Treatments
»
Struggling to focus on up-close tasks? We offer a range of solutions to manage presbyopia, including specialty lenses and contact lens options that help you read, work, and enjoy your day-to-day life without strain.
Accessible Eye Care. With a Personal Touch.
Whether you’re visiting for a quick eyewear update or navigating a complex condition, our team offers guidance without pressure and care without pretense.
We’re here to help you see better and feel good about your care in an environment that reflects the easygoing nature of our beach-town community.
