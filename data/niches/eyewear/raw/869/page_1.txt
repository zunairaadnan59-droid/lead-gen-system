About Los Angeles Eye Center & Clinic
For more than five decades,
Los Angeles Eye Center & Clinic
has been a trusted part of the South Los Angeles community. Located at the corner of Vermont and Vernon, our practice has cared for generations of families, providing everything from everyday glasses to advanced eye disease management.
