Since 1975
Welcome to Family Eyecare of Scottsdale. Since 1975, we’ve proudly served Scottsdale, Phoenix, and Fountain Hills with comprehensive, compassionate eye care for all ages.
Healthy vision is essential to a happy life, and our team is committed to helping you maintain optimal eye health at every stage. Whether it’s managing myopia in kids and teens, finding the right contact lenses for your lifestyle, providing dry eye relief, or managing eye diseases, our team delivers care personalized to your needs.  We’re more than an eye doctor’s office—we’re your partners in vision health. Mancha

She earned her Doctorate of Optometry degree magna cum laude from the University of Houston College of Optometry.  After practicing in Texas and Arizona for 14 years, Dr. Mancha enthusiastically joined Family Eyecare of Scottsdale as a partner and proudly acquired the practice from Dr. Bark in 2020.

Lambert

Meet the Team
Dr. Smiley
Dr. Smiley is originally from the state of Washington. She earned her Doctorate of Optometry degree from the Arizona College of Optometry. Her clinical interests include ocular disease management, especially glaucoma and complex contact lens fittings, as well as personalized vision solutions designed to support each patient’s lifestyle and long-term visual health.

Dry Eye Treatment
in Scottsdale
Personalized Dry Eye Relief Starts Here
Tired of scratchy, irritated eyes or constant watering that still leaves you feeling dry? You might be experiencing dry eye disease - but here’s the good news: relief is possible.
Our team takes a close look at everything affecting your eyes - like tear production, tear quality, screen time, and even your environment - to create a treatment plan tailored just for you.
From innovative in-office treatments like noninvasive radiofrequency and IPL therapy to simple at-home solutions, we’re here to help keep your eyes comfortable and refreshed. Schedule your appointment today and take the first step toward soothing your dry eyes.
