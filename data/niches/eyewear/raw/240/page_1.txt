About Us
About Family Eyecare of Scottsdale
Welcome to Family Eyecare of Scottsdale. Since 1975, we've proudly served Scottsdale, Phoenix, and Fountain Hills with comprehensive, compassionate eye care for all ages.
Healthy vision is essential to a happy life, and our team is committed to helping you maintain optimal eye health at every stage. Whether it's managing myopia in kids and teens, finding the right contact lenses for your lifestyle, providing dry eye relief, or managing eye diseases, our team delivers care personalized to your needs. We're more than an eye doctor's office—we're your partners in vision health. Book your eye exam today.
Our Commitment to You
We are dedicated to exceeding every patient expectation, so you can’t wait to recommend us to your family and friends!
At Family Eyecare of Scottsdale, exceeding expectations means giving 100% every time—not just with the precision of our modern technology, but by explaining your results in a way that makes sense to you.
