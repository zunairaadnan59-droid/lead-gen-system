Boca Raton’s Trusted Optometrist Since 1993
Since 1993, Dr. Rosenbusch and VisionCare Unlimited have served as a dedicated and caring provider of quality vision services and products to all of Boca Raton and the surrounding areas.
As a patient, you can expect attentive care from our experienced
optometrist and staff
. Leveraging advanced diagnostic technology, our services include comprehensive eye exams, vision correction, medical eye care, and more. Dr. Rosenbusch prides herself on our customer service and the amount of time spent with each patient. Our patients are scheduled in 1-hour intervals (unlike most offices, which schedule patients every 15-minutes). This allows Dr. Rosenbusch to spend extra time in order to provide a detailed comprehensive examination and to get to know each patient as an individual, with individual needs.
While we are non-participating providers in most insurance plans, we will gladly submit to your insurance carrier for reimbursement on your behalf. This is a non-issue for most of our patients because they appreciate the personalized and extensive services provided by Dr. Rosenbusch during their visits.
We offer many types of
contacts
and
eyeglass frames
— from high-end designer to affordable and lesser-known brands. Some patients worry that our frames and/or lenses will be more expensive than big box stores and buying online. This is not true! We work hard to carry frame and lens stock at all price points because we understand that each patient has unique financial requirements.
