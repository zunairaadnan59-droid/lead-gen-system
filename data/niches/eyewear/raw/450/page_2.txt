About VisionCare Unlimited
We are a licensed provider of quality vision care products and personalized optometric services in Boca Raton, Florida and the surrounding areas.
Since 1993, Dr. Rosenbusch and her team have been a leading provider of quality vision care products and personalized optometric services to folks in Boca Raton and the surrounding areas.
Dr. Rosenbusch
offers comprehensive vision examinations and is trained in the diagnosis and treatment of a wide array of
eye diseases, conditions, and problems
.
In our mission to provide the best eye care services possible, Dr. Rosenbusch uses only the most advanced, state-of-the-art diagnostic technology and eye care products available. We are committed to educating our patients and providing personalized eye care services to the people of Boca Raton. In fact, Dr. Rosenbusch schedules all exams for 60 minutes, rather than the customary 15 minutes, so she can truly get to know each patient as an individual, with unique needs. At VisionCare Unlimited, Dr. Rosenbusch and her staff genuinely care about your health and are dedicated to providing exceptional and personalized service to everyone who walks through our door.
We offer a wide array of lenses and frames to make sure we have something that fits everyone’s style and budget. Our frames and lenses cover all price points, ensuring our patients have the ability and desire to purchase directly from our great selection.
Schedule an appointment
with Dr. Rosenbusch at our Boca Raton optometrist office and give your vision the level of care and attention it deserves.
