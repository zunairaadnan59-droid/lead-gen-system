High-Tech Solutions
For Outstanding Care
We believe that technology is crucial to providing the best care possible. Our San Diego office is equipped with:
Optos Nikon High-Resolution Retinal Camera
This tool captures high-resolution retina images and eliminates the need for pupil dilation, an uncomfortable side effect that can persist for hours after your appointment.
Topcon Automated Visual Field Analyzer
Detects glaucoma, optic nerve damage, retinal conditions (macular degeneration), neurological issues (strokes or brain injuries affecting vision), and diabetic retinopathy.
Essilor Eye-Ruler and VR Headset
Accurately takes progressive lens measurements and helps your doctor deliver high-quality, comfortable eyewear with precise prescriptions. This ensures that each patient receives the best possible vision correction possible.
Topcon
Auto-Refractometer
This advanced tool easily and accurately measures refractive error. Similar to our other systems like the Essilor Eye-Ruler, the auto-refractometer provides comfortable and accurate refractive measurements for prescriptions.

Dr. Wong:
Your Trusted Professional
Dr. Gordon Wong began practicing optometry in 1991 and his current areas of emphasis include refractive surgery consultation, surgical co-management, and specialty contact lenses. Dr. Wong did his undergraduate work at the University of California at San Diego, Revelle College. After graduating, he served as both the chief resident of the Cornea and Contact Lens Department at the University Eye Institute and part-time academic and clinical faculty at the University of Houston College of Optometry in Texas.
Currently, Dr. Wong serves as co-clinical director of NVision Laser Eye Center of La Jolla. There, he is responsible for selecting optimal candidates for laser eye surgery, developing treatment plans with the surgeon, as well as coordinating the pre and post-operative care of the San Diego optometric affiliate network. He also serves as an expert examiner for the National Board of Examiners in Optometry and is a clinical preceptor for the Pennsylvania College of Optometry.
In his spare time, Dr. Wong enjoys spending time with his wife, Joy, and his dog, Chloe. He also loves participating in all types of sports, particularly basketball, horseback riding, rock climbing, mountain biking, and snowboarding.
