About the Store
Ann Taylor Factory is located at Napa Premium Outlets, 821 Factory Store Drive, Napa, CA 94558. Situated just off the First Street exit on Highway 29, this open-air outlet destination sits at the gateway to California's celebrated Napa Valley wine country, making it a natural stop for locals and visitors alike. Whether you're a Napa resident running errands or a wine country traveler building in a shopping day, the location offers convenient access and a relaxed, walkable outdoor environment.
Ann Taylor Factory brings the signature style of Ann Taylor to an outlet shopping format, offering exceptional savings on beautifully crafted workwear, tailored suiting, polished dresses, and refined everyday separates. Shoppers will find a mix of outlet-exclusive factory styles and specially priced selections across Ann Taylor's classic collections, making it an ideal destination for women who want elevated, feminine apparel without paying full price. From boardroom-ready blazers to occasion dresses and versatile layering pieces, the store delivers the Ann Taylor aesthetic at factory store prices.
If a particular style or size is not available on the floor, our associates are happy to help you explore other options. Please note that return eligibility for online orders at outlet locations may vary. If you have an anntaylor.com order you would like to return or exchange, bring your item along with your packing slip or order confirmation and our team will assist you at the register.
Getting Here
By car: Ann Taylor Factory at Napa Premium Outlets is located at 821 Factory Store Drive, Napa, CA 94558. From Highway 29, take the First Street exit directly into the City of Napa and follow signs to the outlet center. The location is easily accessible from communities throughout the North Bay, including American Canyon, Vallejo, Sonoma, and the greater San Francisco Bay Area. Napa Premium Outlets offers ample surface parking, providing easy, drive-up access to the store and surrounding retailers throughout the open-air center.
By transit: The VINE, Napa County's public bus system operated by the Napa Valley Transportation Authority, provides transit service throughout the city of Napa and regional connections to destinations including American Canyon, Vallejo, and El Cerrito. Route and schedule information is available at vinetransit.com. That said, most shoppers visiting Napa Premium Outlets find driving the most convenient option, as the outlet center is positioned near Highway 29 and is best suited for visitors arriving by car.
About the Area
Napa Premium Outlets is an open-air outlet shopping center located at 629 Factory Stores Drive in Napa, California, with Ann Taylor Factory situated at the adjacent 821 Factory Store Drive address within the same complex. The center is a Simon Property Group destination and features over 40 brand-name and designer outlet stores, with shoppers drawn from across Northern California and beyond. The outdoor layout, combined with easy freeway access off Highway 29, makes it a popular stop for day-trippers and overnight visitors who want to combine outlet shopping with everything else Napa has to offer.
Napa is the largest city in Napa County and serves as the southern gateway to one of the world's most celebrated wine regions. Known for its proximity to hundreds of wineries and tasting rooms, Michelin-starred restaurants, luxury spas, and scenic vineyard landscapes, the Napa Valley draws visitors from across the country and around the globe year-round. The city itself has seen a vibrant revitalization, with a growing dining scene, riverfront attractions, and upscale lodging that make it a destination in its own right. Ann Taylor Factory at Napa Premium Outlets serves the style needs of the many women who call this region home, as well as the countless visitors who make the trip to wine country and are happy to find a familiar, trusted brand offering quality and value along the way.
FAQs
Where is Ann Taylor Factory located at Napa Premium Outlets?
Ann Taylor Factory is located at 821 Factory Store Drive, Napa, CA 94558, within Napa Premium Outlets. The outlet center is accessible directly from the First Street exit off Highway 29, making it easy to reach from throughout the North Bay and greater San Francisco Bay Area.
What are the Ann Taylor Factory Napa store hours?
Store hours may vary throughout the year and during holidays. Call (707) 991-1441 or visit anntaylor.com for the most current store hours before your visit.
Is there parking at Napa Premium Outlets?
Yes. Napa Premium Outlets offers ample surface parking throughout the open-air center, providing easy access to Ann Taylor Factory and the many other outlet retailers, dining options, and brand-name stores located throughout the complex.
What kinds of savings can I find at Ann Taylor Factory in Napa?
Ann Taylor Factory offers savings on a curated selection of workwear, tailored suiting, dresses, and polished separates in an outlet format. The store carries outlet-exclusive factory styles as well as specially priced selections from Ann Taylor's core collections, making it possible to find elevated, beautifully crafted women's apparel at factory store prices every day.
Does Ann Taylor Factory carry petite, curvy, or tall sizes?
Ann Taylor Factory carries a range of sizes designed to fit a variety of body types, subject to current inventory. If you are looking for a specific size or style that is not currently on the floor, our associates are happy to assist you in exploring your options.
Can I return an anntaylor.com order at the Napa outlet store?
Return eligibility for online orders at outlet locations may vary. Bring your item along with your order confirmation or packing slip, and our associates will assist you with the return process.
Is Ann Taylor Factory at Napa Premium Outlets open on holidays?
Holiday hours may vary. Outlet centers often have extended hours during peak shopping seasons. Call (707) 991-1441 or check anntaylor.com before visiting to confirm current operating hours.
