About Us
Say hello to the new you and your improved vision!
Your vision is important to us.
We  will verify the results from your new prescription for glasses, to be  sure that you will see better with your new glasses than what you are  currently wearing. This will assure you that your investment in your  vision will be a good one. If you have ever purchased glasses that never  seemed to work correctly, please bring them in so we can perform  an analysis and identify the problem.
At  Westlake Optical, we care about your vision. We use the latest  technology and equipment to assist us in solving your vision needs.  Whether you need a stronger contact prescription and/or new glasses, we can help you. You can trust Westlake Optical to take care of you and  your family's vision needs.
