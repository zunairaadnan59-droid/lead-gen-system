Comprehensive Care for Myopia at Total Vision Rancho Bernardo
At Total Vision Rancho Bernardo, we are committed to building brighter futures for your children through
myopia management
to help control and slow the progression of nearsightedness in children. Our personalized
Total Myopia
program includes solutions such as:
Orthokeratology (ortho-k) contact lenses
Low-dose atropine eye drops
Peripheral defocus contact lenses
These treatments are tailored to support your child’s vision development while minimizing the risk of long-term eye health complications. Through
regular check-ups
, we prioritize your child’s future visual clarity and wellness.
Contact us
today to discover how our customized approach to myopia control can benefit your child.
