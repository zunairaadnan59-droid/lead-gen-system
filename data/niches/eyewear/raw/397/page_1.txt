Clear Vision, Local Eye Care
Welcome to Darien Eye Care!
We are an independent, locally owned practice striving to provide exceptional eye care services to our valued patients and visitors. Whether you need a comprehensive eye exam, contact lens exam, same-day emergency eye care, advanced dry eye treatment using OptiLight, or personalized eyewear styling, we are here to meet your needs. We also have a full-service optical with an on-site lab for all your eyewear needs. Feel free to come visit our office and thank you for choosing Darien Eye Care. We look forward to serving you with excellence and compassion.

Meet Our Providers
Dr. Shefali Ahmadi is a compassionate Optometrist with an eagerness to provide exceptional eye care, while embodying her core values of trust, communication, and passion. She received a Bachelor's degree in Biological Sciences from the University of Connecticut in 2013 and continued her graduate level studies at the New England College of Optometry, earning a Doctorate of Optometry in 2017.
