A Legacy of Eye Care Excellence in Glenwood Springs & Carbondale, Colorado
Welcome to 20/20 Eyecare, your premier destination for eye care services. Founded over 50 years ago by Dr. William Zilm and now proudly continued by his son, Dr. Mark Zilm, our practice stands as a testament to our enduring commitment to the health and vision of our community.

Local Roots, Global Standards: Meet Our Optometrists
We cherish our local roots and emphasize a personalized, customer-centric approach in everything we do. Beyond correcting vision, 20/20 Eyecare works hard to enhance your overall quality of life through tailored solutions, conditions, like dry eye, and even expert co-management for LASIK procedures. Our state-of-the-art on-site lab also ensures quick and precise eyewear creation, allowing us to offer same-day services for regular and sports eyewear. The atmosphere at 20/20 Eyecare will make you feel at ease when you step in. Thoughtful touches like art on display and refreshments reflect our dedication to creating a warm, inviting environment.

Eye Disease Treatment & Management
Our eye doctors can diagnose, treat, and manage various eye diseases using top systems like Cirrus OCT digital imaging, Optos, and more. Glaucoma, cataracts, macular degeneration, and diabetic retinopathy are no match for the level of technology and expertise at 20/20 Eyecare!
