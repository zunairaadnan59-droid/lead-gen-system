Eye Care for Students & Faculty
Life at Georgia Institute of Technology moves fast. Whether you're studying late into the night, teaching in the classroom, working on campus, or competing at the highest level of collegiate athletics, clear and comfortable vision plays an important role in your performance. From contact lenses and eyewear to annual eye exams and sports vision support, we're here to help you see clearly.
