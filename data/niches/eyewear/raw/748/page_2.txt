Our Team
Joceline Gallegos
Optician
Joceline is a San Diego native and a graduate of SDSU. She has been a part of the Vision Boutique team for the past year and enjoys assisting patients and working alongside great doctors. Outside of work, she loves spending time with her friends and her chocolate lab, Oso.
Viviana Morales
Technician/Front Desk
Viviana, a San Diego native, is our technician/front desk employee. Starting with no prior experience in optometry, she worked hard to gain knowledge and skills. It has allowed her to grow personally and professionally.
Family remains at the heart of everything she does, motivating her to continue to strive and succeed.
Alexa Flores
Technician/Front Desk
Alexa is our technician/front desk employee who started her career in optometry in 2022. She is from South Dakota and has been in San Diego for 2 years. She loves trying new restaurants and spending time with her husband and dog.
