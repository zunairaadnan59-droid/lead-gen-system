Our Team
meet our doctors
Vision Boutique Optometry provides the highest quality, compassionate optometry to ensure you and your family are healthy and happy. Meet our caring and compassionate team committed to providing exceptional eye care for your family.
Dr. Jason Tu
Optometrist
Dr. Tu is the owner of Vision Boutique Optometry. He and his family are immigrants from Vietnam, narrowly escaping in 1978. After surviving in a refugee camp for one year, they were finally able to find a sponsor in the US and came over in 1979. They have since committed their lives to achieving the American dream and giving back to this great country.
Dr. Orlando Guiang
Optometrist
Dr. Orlando Guiang, a Fellow of the American Academy of Optometry, is an experienced optometrist with over 33 years of providing comprehensive eye care in the San Diego community, including primary vision examinations, advanced contact lens fittings (with expertise in multifocal, hybrid, and scleral lenses), Myopia Management, and pre-/post-operative evaluations. He is known for delivering thorough, patient-centered care with a strong emphasis on clear communication and clinical precision.
