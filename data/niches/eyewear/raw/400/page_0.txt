Why BLOS?
Did you know that after you get your eye exam, the prescription is yours to fill anywhere?
I've built  my reputation on reliability & precision lenses, and built my business around filling outside prescriptions so that I can dedicate all of my time and attention on what I love most, helping patients and making eyeglasses!
After your exam simply request a copy of your prescription from your doctor and schedule your time to come see me!
Options. After your eye exam the lenses are the most important part.
Because we aren't limited by insurance plans, we have options galore! We can source just about any lens style or coating you can dream up! Typically we begin by sitting together to talk.  We like to learn about you so that we can get a sense of your needs and come up with a solution that works for you, together!
We carry frames for men and women of all sizes!
We have made sure to include everyone! we have a variety of frame sizes, including
petite & x-wide
frames. While we do carry kids (under 8) eyewear, outside of the husky collection, the selection is limited. We also carry

How does my purchase help the community?
Quality Eyewear for Everyone!
Your purchase helps support our mission of making quality eyewear accessible to members of our community who cannot afford the glasses they need to work, learn, and live their daily lives.
Your support helps cover the costs of lab services, advertising, staff, and other expenses that allow us to provide this service to our community.
