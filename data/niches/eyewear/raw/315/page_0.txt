About us
As part of Northwell, we bring a wide range of eye care services right to your community. We offer access to vast resources including state-of-the-art technology and renowned expertise from the Northwell Health Eye Institute. This exceptional, personalized care is just one of the many ways we have earned the trust of patients, families and referring physicians.

Northwell Health Eye Institute
The Eye Institute is a select network of Northwell physicians and private providers with more than 90 subspecialty ophthalmologists across 40 locations throughout the New York metropolitan area.
We have dedicated physicians that are board-certified and fully licensed in all ophthalmic subspecialties, including comprehensive care, refractive surgery,
oculoplastics
, glaucoma, neuro-ophthalmology and pediatrics. These services include advanced screening, diagnosis and treatment, with a special focus on prevention and research through innovative clinical trials.
