About Unique Eye Care and Optical
Unique Eye Care and Optical established in 1982 as an optical has now expanded to encompass optometric care to provide a full-service experience from getting your eyes examined by our highly trained doctor to receiving a great pair of glasses and/or contact lenses.
We strive to give you the best quality eye care services possible. Check out our highly-trained team of professional doctors below. From regular checkups to full-eye exams, we will give you the vision that you have always wanted!
Unique Eye Care and Optical is truly a hidden gem in the Rice Village. We care about our patients and care to give them the best eye exams, fashionable eyewear, the latest technology, and the very best services in town. Schedule your visit today!
