About
Stamford Ophthalmology is a state-of-the-art eye care facility conveniently located in the heart of downtown Stamford. Our experienced team of specialists is dedicated to providing our patients the highest quality ophthalmic care with kindness, compassion and efficiency.
Please call
+1 (203) 327-5808
to make an appointment with one of our doctors.
Comprehensive Eye Examinations
Stamford Ophthalmology offers complete eye examinations to assess the overall health of the eyes and address any specific medical or visual issues.
A
Comprehensive Eye Examination
usually includes:
Measurement of the visual acuity at distance and near, and a refraction to determine any lens correction needed.
Measurement of the eye pressure (intraocular pressure).
Measurement of the pupil reactivity, ocular movement and alignment.
Detailed examination of the external and internal anatomy of the eye.
Examination of the retina and optic nerve, usually with dilation of the pupils.
Diagnosis and Treatment of Eye Disease
Stamford Ophthalmology offers diagnostic testing and medical and surgical options for most eye disorders.
Disorders commonly diagnosed and treated include:
Cataract
– with surgery being done using state-of-the-art phacoemulsification techniques.
Glaucoma
Corneal disorders
Pediatric Disorders
, including strabismus, amblyopia and tear duct obstruction
Eye infections and inflammatory conditions
Diabetic eye disease
Contact lens related eye problems
A Full Range of Optical Correction is Available
Stamford Ophthalmology’s in-house
Optical Shop
offers wide selection of frame and lens choices with expert fitting and adjustment by certified opticians.
Dr. Maria Pribis specializes in contact lens fitting. Contact lens options include lenses for astigmatism, multi-focal lenses, disposables, and more.
Premium Lens Implants
to improve your range of vision following cataract surgery.
