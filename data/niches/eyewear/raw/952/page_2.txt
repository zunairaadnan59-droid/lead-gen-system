About Paris Miki USA
Experience a legacy built on one simple 90-year philosophy that prioritizes you as our customers, our employees, and our company’s legacy. Since our founding in 1930 in Himeji, Japan, Paris Miki USA has grown into one of the world’s largest optical chains. Craft your perfect eyewear experience with our expert team, who provide flexible services to ensure your complete satisfaction.
Worldwide Brand
Paris Miki is a globally recognized Japanese eyewear retailer with more than 700 stores across Asia, Australia, Europe, and the United States. Founded in 1930 in Himeji City, the company began as a small shop offering a variety of goods, including eyewear, and has grown into one of the world’s most trusted names in optical care.
Unmatched Expertise by Licensed Opticians
Get the perfect glasses with our expert team and on-site labs. Our skilled technicians have the flexibility to create custom frames and lenses, giving you complete control over your final look. We offer efficient services, including same-day appointments, free adjustments, and expert insurance guidance, creating a simplified experience so we can meet your needs quickly with care.
Personalized Eye Care with Dr. Liu
Your eyes do more than one job — your glasses should too. In collaboration with trusted optometrists, we provide specialized prescriptions for computer, reading, progressive, and lifestyle needs, ensuring your vision stays clear and comfortable wherever life takes you.
