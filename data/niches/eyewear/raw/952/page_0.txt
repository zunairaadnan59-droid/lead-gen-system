📣 Looking for Asian Fit Glasses? Then Paris Miki Is the Place for You.
We’re an eyewear boutique born in Japan in 1930. Nearly 100 years later, our Asian Fit frames are built around a higher bridge that actually fits your nose — no more sliding, pinching, or constant pushing-up — paired with ultra-thin, Japanese-made 1.76 index lenses for a comfortable, personalized fit for everyone.
