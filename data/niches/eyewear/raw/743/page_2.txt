About Windward Eye Care & Alpharetta Eye Clinic
Trusted Eye Care in Alpharetta and North Atlanta Since 2003
At Windward Eye Care & Alpharetta Eye Clinic, our mission is simple: to provide exceptional eye care, personalized service, and the latest vision solutions to patients of all ages throughout Alpharetta, Roswell, Milton, Johns Creek, Cumming, and the surrounding North Atlanta communities.
For decades, our practice has been dedicated to helping patients achieve and maintain healthy vision through comprehensive eye examinations, advanced diagnostic technology, and customized treatment plans. Whether you need a routine eye exam, treatment for an eye condition, specialty contact lenses, or management of a complex eye disease, our experienced team is here to help.
Comprehensive Eye Care for the Entire Family
We proudly provide complete eye care services for children, adults, and seniors, including:
Comprehensive Eye Exams
Regular eye examinations are essential for maintaining clear vision and detecting eye health issues before they become serious. Our doctors evaluate your visual acuity, eye health, and overall ocular function to help protect your sight for years to come.
Diagnosis and Treatment of Eye Diseases
Our experienced optometrists diagnose, monitor, and manage a variety of eye conditions, including:
Glaucoma
Dry Eye Syndrome
Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)
Eye Infections
Red Eyes
Cataracts
Macular Degeneration
Diabetic Eye Disease
Systemic Health Screenings
Did you know your eyes can reveal signs of serious health conditions? During comprehensive eye exams, we can often detect early indicators of:
High Blood Pressure
Diabetes
High Cholesterol
Autoimmune Disorders
Other systemic health concerns
Specialty Contact Lens Services
Not every patient can achieve their best vision with traditional contact lenses. We offer specialty contact lens fittings for:
Keratoconus
Astigmatism
Presbyopia (Bifocal Contact Lenses)
Corneal Irregularities
CRT/Orthokeratology
Hard-to-Fit Contact Lens Cases
LASIK and Refractive Surgery Co-Management
Considering LASIK or PRK vision correction? We provide comprehensive pre-operative evaluations and post-operative care to help ensure optimal results and long-term eye health.
Cataract Care
Our doctors provide cataract diagnosis, surgical referrals, and complete pre- and post-operative cataract management to help patients restore clearer vision and improve quality of life.
