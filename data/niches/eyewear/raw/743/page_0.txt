Windward Eye Care
The expert doctors at Alpharetta Eye Clinic and Windward Eye Care accommodate each patient’s unique optical needs; no two patients or prescriptions are the same. We believe in providing the most technologically-advanced eye care possible. Our dedicated doctors and staff treat patients with respect, and our ultimate goal is to improve our patients’ quality of life. When our patients are satisfied, we can say we are successful.
We’d love to get to know you, provide the clearest vision you can imagine, and help you choose the perfect eye-wear for your lifestyle.

About Atlanta Eye Care
Alpharetta Eye Clinic
Windward Eye Care
Atlanta Eye Care, owned by Drs. Thomas Arey, Sean Alexander, and Michael Walker, operates two locations in the Alpharetta area. Windward Eye Care opened in 2003 when Drs. Arey, Alexander, and Walker saw an opportunity to serve their patients in a quickly-growing community.
They expanded Atlanta Eye Care in 2009 by opening a second location: Alpharetta Eye Clinic on Old Milton Parkway across the street from Avalon.
