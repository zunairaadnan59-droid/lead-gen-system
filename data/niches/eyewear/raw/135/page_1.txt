The Elan Optical Story
Elan Optical has been proudly serving the San Francisco area with quality
eyewear
and
lens
services since 1986.
Since opening, we have become one of Northern California’s most competitive independent optical stores. We offer one of the Bay Area’s largest collections of designer frames and sunglasses and an optical lab supported by years of experience and modern technology.
Visit Us Today
What We Offer
Elan Optical is an American Board of Opticians-certified optical center that features some of the world’s most luxurious and
exclusive frame brands
, including
Cartier
,
Dita
, and
Alexander McQueen
.
Using the Essilor Visioffice, we can pair all of our frames with
quality lenses
, from single visions, bifocals, and progressives to specialty products like Transitions and anti-reflective coatings.
And if you need a repair or adjustment, we can help with that, too.

Services
At Elan Optical, we are proud to offer a variety of high-quality products to our customers, from prescription eyewear to repairs and adjustments. Our products and services include:
Designer eyewear
Sunglasses
Optical frames
Prescription lenses: Polycarbonate, Trivex, and hi-index
Single-vision, bifocal, and progressive lenses
Transitions: Light adaptive lenses
2 Year unlimited warranty on lenses with Crizal anti-reflective coating
Polarized/tinted prescription sunglasses
Certified Essilor lab
Varilux specialists
Expert Frame Repairs: Repairs, replacing nose pads, as well as adjustments
Essilor Visioffice dispensing technology
Blue light filter options
We are also proud to be certified by the American Board of Opticians.
