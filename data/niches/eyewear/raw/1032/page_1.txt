Eye Exams & Affordable Eye Care in Napa
Welcome to the Site for Sore Eyes eye care store in Napa, CA. We have been delivering high quality optical care to people of all ages since 1988. Dr. Gareth Valles, Independent Doctor of Optometry and a knowledgeable, skilled staff are committed to providing everything – from thorough eye exams to advice on new eyeglasses, sunglasses, contact lenses and sports eyewear. Site for Sore Eyes in Napa is proud to offer comprehensive eye exams from our professional optometrist. A thorough eye exam is the cornerstone of excellent vision. Our skilled optometrists deliver comprehensive assessments, typically in just 30 minutes, focusing on both your visual acuity and overall eye health. We’ll ensure your eye prescription is accurate, whether you prefer glasses or contact lenses, and discuss the best solutions for your lifestyle. Regular visits are key to early detection of eye conditions and maintaining optimal eyesight throughout your life. If a prescription is required we have the one of the largest selections in value and designer glasses and frames available to find the right style for you.

About the Doctor:
Gareth C. Valles
Independent Doctor of Optometry at Site for Sore Eyes
Gareth C. Valles, an Independent Doctor of Optometry. Valles was born in Bombay, India then raised and educated in Montreal, Canada before getting his B.S. Degree in Biochemistry at Loyola College of Montreal. After graduate studies in Nuclear Medicine at the University of New Mexico in Albuquerque, Valles earned his Doctorate in Optometry at U.C. Berkeley.
His optometry practice has included the medical model at Marin’s Premier Ophthalmology Group Practice in San Rafael and at Site for Sore Eyes – Napa since 1997.
Valles currently lives with his family in Napa and they enjoy international travels with their friends.’
