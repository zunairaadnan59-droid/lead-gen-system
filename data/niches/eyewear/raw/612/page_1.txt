About Us
The Very Best In-sight Since 1984
Your Trusted Local Optical Provider
Since 1984, Your Vision is Our Mission
We are a locally owned and operated company that is fully licensed, certified, insured, bonded and accredited. We are confident that you will be completely satisfied with your
new frames, sunglasses or contact lenses and we offer warranties on all of our available products
. Quality and customer service are our top priorities and we always strive to ensure that our customers are completely satisfied with everything we have to offer.
We are proud of the personalized service we offer to each and every customer we meet. We want to be your #1 choice for eyewear and eye health care.
Clear Vision, Expert Care
If your eyesight isn’t as sharp as it used to be or your prescription feels outdated, visit us today. We’ll schedule your eye exam, provide the right prescription, and help you choose from over 1,000 stylish frames in our showroom. With our on-site eyeglass fabrication lab, you’ll get fast, high-quality service. For top-tier eyewear and eye care in Colorado Springs, trust the experts at In-Sight Optical to keep you seeing and looking your best.
Our Core Values
Integrity
