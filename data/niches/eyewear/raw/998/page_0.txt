Our Services
Comprehensive Eye exam
At The Eye Works, we use top technology and a variety of procedures to test your vision quality and ocular health.
Tests range from having you read a simple eye chart to assessing the tissues inside your eyes by looking through a high-powered lens.
LASIK Consultation
LASIK is a laser eye surgery used to correct refractive errors of the eye. Patients usually opt for LASIK when they tire of wearing corrective lenses, such as contacts or glasses.
During your visit, our doctors will let you know if you are a good candidate.
Contact Lenses
At The Eye Works Optometry, we provide cutting-edge products and services in the field of contact lenses, incorporating the latest innovations in lens fitting and materials. Wearing contact lenses has never been more convenient and  comfortable, with personalized options to suit your needs.
Ocular Emergencies
Eye emergencies happen when you least expect them, so knowing what to do if you or a loved one needs urgent eye care is vital.  Never try to judge the severity of an eye injury on your own. Instead, contact us for instructions on what you should do in your situation. In most cases, we will see you in same day.
Macular Degeneration
Macular degeneration is a disease in which the macula weakens, resulting in a loss of central vision. The condition, also known as age-related macular degeneration (AMD), can cause blurring and even partial vision loss. There is no cure for macular degeneration although an optometrist can help manage the condition.
Pediatric Vision Care
We specialize in pediatric optometry, ensuring a child-friendly experience during examinations. For parents seeking a comprehensive understanding of their child's visual health, we offer in-depth consultations, discussing visual development, identifying any potential vision issues, and presenting both treatment and preventative options.
Cataracts
Cataracts are common, age-related clouding of the clear lens that covers the eye.
Though they are not dangerous or threatening to the health of the eye, cataracts can be an inconvenience when performing routine tasks, such as reading, driving etc.
Diabetic Retinopathy
Diabetic retinopathy is a progressive eye disease that slowly deteriorates vision over time.
In its initial stages, the disease may present few or no symptoms. This is called mild non-proliferative retinopathy, which can only be diagnosed in a dilated eye examination.
Glaucoma
Glaucoma is a term used to refer to a group of eye diseases that ultimately cause damage to the optic nerve.
Glaucoma often presents no other symptoms than vision loss, which is why it is important to visit an eye doctor periodically for comprehensive eye exams.
Advanced Imaging
In addition to retinal photography, which we always explain as being almost like an  MRI for your eye, we can perform retinal scans using a technology called Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT). This advanced imaging method provides a higher level of detail by revealing the intricate structure of the retina, showcasing its ten distinct layers.
