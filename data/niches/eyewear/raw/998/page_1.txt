Our Doctors
Dr. Craig, SULTAN, O.D
Education
Dr. Craig Sultan, O.D., is an alumnus of the State University of New York College of Optometry where he honed his expertise in eye care. In addition to his optometry degree, he expanded his academic horizons with a Master's Degree in Immunology from the University of California, Davis.
His educational journey reflects a blend of specialized eye care knowledge and a broad understanding of immunological health.
Experience
With a rich professional tenure spanning over 40 years in the Bay Area and 20 years in the Napa/Solano region, Dr. Sultan brings a wealth of experience to his practice.
He completed a pivotal residency in pediatrics at the State University of New York, working with thousands of children from six months of age and older. Dr. Sultan is not only known for his expertise in optometry but also for his dedication to personalized care.
He states, "My commitment to my patients is ongoing professional education. As technology and the world around us changes, we keep updated because we desire to provide the best care possible for our patients.”
Additionally, he has a significant background in co-managing LASIK, having been a part of the California Optometric Laser Associates (COLA) for 15 years and bringing personal experience to the table, having undergone the procedure himself.
Personal life
Dr. Craig Sultan's personal interests extend beyond his optometric career into the realms of creativity and animal companionship. A passionate writer, he delves into the art of storytelling through screenplays and children's books, where he weaves his rich imagination with elements of health and wellness. These literary endeavors reflect a multifaceted personality that cherishes the power of stories to educate and inspire.
In addition to his literary pursuits, Dr. Sultan has a profound love for dogs, underscored by his unique experience of caring for a full-breed wolf for 13.5 years-ATHENA.
This extraordinary bond not only demonstrates his deep affection for animals but also his appreciation for the natural world. This blend of creative writing and his special connection with his pet paints a picture of a man with diverse interests and a deep sense of commitment in both his personal and professional life.
Dr. Todd, HARTER, O.D
Education
Dr. J. Harter, O.D., lays his academic foundations in the prestigious institutions of the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign and the University of California, Berkeley, School of Optometry. His educational journey, rooted in these renowned universities, has provided him with a comprehensive understanding and skill set in the field of optometry, preparing him for a distinguished career in this domain.
Experience
With over 36 years of experience in optometry practice, Dr. Harter has dedicated the majority of his career to private practice in California, including a significant tenure at David Grant Medical Center at Travis Air Force Base.
His role as the Statewide Optometric Consultant for the California Department of Rehabilitation further accentuates his vast experience and contributions to the field. Dr. Harter's recent addition to the Eye Works Optometry team marks a continuation of his commitment to providing top-notch vision care and therapeutic treatments for various medical eye conditions and injuries.
Personal life
Dr. J. Harter enjoys a fulfilling personal life in Napa with his wife, Pamela. They share a passion for travel, often exploring new places together. Dr. Harter is also an enthusiast of fine wines, a hobby well suited to the Napa Valley's rich vineyard culture.
Despite the distance, he cherishes the moments spent with his three adult children and grandchildren, making the most of every opportunity to connect with his family.
