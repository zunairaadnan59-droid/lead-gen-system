The people behind
the OPTYX standard
OPTYX has cared for New York's eyes since 1971. Across 13 locations, our team brings together world-class optometrists, certified opticians, and style icons – every one of them focused on the same standard of care, fit, and curation.
We don't just help you see the world more clearly. We help the world see you at your very best.
Harvey Ross
Founder & President
A veteran of the global eyewear industry, Harvey Ross founded Viva International Eyewear before selling it to Highmark - then launched OPTYX, rooted in the belief that eyewear should do more than correct vision.
Today, OPTYX runs on the same standard Harvey built it on: world-class optometry, globally curated eyewear, and personal care.
“A great pair of frames can change your character and become a confidence builder.”
— Harvey Ross, Founder & President
