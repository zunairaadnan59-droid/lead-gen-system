Common conditions
we treat
Dry Eye & Irritation
Dry eye can cause burning, redness, and discomfort. We identify the cause and recommend treatments to restore comfort.
Astigmatism & Vision Clarity
Astigmatism causes blurred or distorted vision. Customized lenses help restore clear sight.
Retinal Disorders & Detachments
Conditions affecting the retina that can impact vision. Early detection through exams and imaging helps protect eye health.
Glaucoma Monitoring
Glaucoma can damage vision without early symptoms. Routine exams help detect and monitor it early.
Cataract Screening
Cataracts cause the eye's lens to become cloudy. Exams help detect them early and guide next steps.
Presbyopia (Age-Related Vision Changes)
Presbyopia affects near vision with age. Solutions include reading glasses, progressive lenses, or contacts.
Myopia (Nearsightedness)
Myopia makes distant objects appear blurry. Eye exams help monitor changes and provide the right correction.
Digital Eye Strain
Screen time can cause tired eyes and headaches. We provide solutions to reduce strain and improve comfort.
Diabetic Eye Disease
Diabetes can affect the retina and vision. Regular exams help detect changes early.
