WELCOME TO OUR PRACTICE!
We are pleased to welcome you to our office. If you're looking for quality care with a personal touch, we hope you'll give us a call. We specialize in Ortho-K and Vision Therapy for nearsighted vision control in children.
We look forward to the opportunity of serving your family's eyecare needs.
We have multiple locations to serve you with the newest technology and ability to have digital vision charts and full automation.
We also have the newest camera to take a pictures of the inside of your eyes if you choose. Our comprehensive eye exams always include cataract and glaucoma evaluations.
Our Milpitas location is located conveniently in the Safeway Milpitas Town center, and our office in Fremont is located in the Fremont Times Square.
We would be delighted to have you as a patient. We have a full selection of eyeglasses and contact lenses. We take care of patients of all ages.
New Patients are always welcome!
