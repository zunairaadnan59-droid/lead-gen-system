Scottsdale Lasik - Scottsdale Vision Correction - Scottsdale Eye Doctor - Scottsdale Center for Site - Dr Petelin
Scottsdale Center for Sight was one of the first practices in the Valley to offer LASIK without the use of a blade to its patients. Intralase Lasik has proved to be one of the most significant advances in field of laser vision correction over the last fifteen year history. Dr. Petelin founded Center for Sight in 2002 to fill a Valley need for a leading eye center under one roof and dedicated only to the highest standards of patient care and technology in the field.
As an eye surgeon, Dr. Petelin is uniquely qualified. He received his medical degree from the University of Arizona and completed his Ophthalmology training at one of the nation's most prestigious programs: Emory University. Even more importantly, Dr. Petelin is one of only a handful of Fellowship trained eye surgeons specializing in corneal refractive surgery. He received this training in Kansas City, with one of the countries top ophthalmologists.
We  gladly accept new patients and we accept most insurance plans.
At Scottsdale Center for Sight together Dr. Petelin and Dr. Shorb offer a wide range of eye care services, and are able to skillfully treat most conditions of the eye including elective procedures such as LASIK and Premium Lens implants to eliminate or reduce the dependancy on glasses after cataract surgery.
We welcome Dr. Stanley Shorb, a Retinal Specialist to our practice.
Dr. Shorb has been practicing in The Valley for over 30 years.  Fellowship trained, he is a specialist in diseases of the eye including conditions affecting the retina.  Dr. Shorb brings Scottsdale Center For Sight his unique background with regards to treatment of macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, as well as other diseases or conditions affecting the retina such as retinal tears or retinal holes.
