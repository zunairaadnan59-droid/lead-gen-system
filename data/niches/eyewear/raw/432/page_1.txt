About Us
The Mitchell Eye Center is Boca Raton’s trusted ophthalmology practice, offering a wide array of eye treatments for patients.
Our ophthalmologist and skilled team are dedicated to helping you achieve and maintain great eye health and vision. We thank you for visiting our website and invite you to learn more about us by clicking on the links below.
We hope you find this site helpful and informative. Please feel free to
contact us
