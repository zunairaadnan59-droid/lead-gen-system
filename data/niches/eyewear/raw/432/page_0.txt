What Sets Our Eye Doctors Apart?
Dr. Alan L. Mitchell and all of our staff at the Mitchell Eye Center are your trusted ophthalmology and
eye surgery specialists in Boca Raton, FL. We are committed to offering you expert eye care and
providing patients with a professional, personal, and positive vision experience in our state-of-the-art
facility.
Mitchell Eye Center provides comprehensive eye care—from routine eye exams, to specialty treatment for
conditions like glaucoma or diabetes, to surgical procedures for cataracts and LASIK eye surgery, as
well as cosmetic injections and treatments like BOTOX®.
After your appointment you can stop by The Optique, our convenient on-site boutique that carries a
diverse assortment of sunglass and eyeglass brands to help you find your perfect pair. Whatever your
needs, our team is here to provide you with individualized service with a smile!

featured service
LASIK
We specialize in LASIK eye surgery, a cutting-edge procedure that can correct various vision problems
using laser technology. LASIK stands for Laser-Assisted In Situ Keratomileusis and involves reshaping
the cornea to improve vision. Our practice utilizes state-of-the-art technology for LASIK, including the
latest lasers and diagnostic equipment, to ensure the highest level of precision and safety for our
patients.

featured service
Cataract surgery
At our practice, we specialize in cataract surgery, a procedure that can effectively remove cataracts and
restore clear vision. Our experienced surgeons utilize the latest techniques and technology, including
advanced intraocular lenses, to provide our patients with the best possible outcomes. If you are
experiencing vision problems due to cataracts, we encourage you to schedule a consultation with us to
discuss your options for treatment.

Aesthetics
In addition to our comprehensive eye care services, our ophthalmology practice also offers a range of
aesthetic services to help our patients look and feel their best. Our experienced team provides
non-surgical treatments such as Botox and dermal fillers to address common cosmetic concerns such as
wrinkles and fine lines around the eyes. With our aesthetic services, we aim to enhance the natural
beauty of our patients while ensuring their overall health and well-being.
