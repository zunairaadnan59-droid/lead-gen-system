Secure payments
We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, ApplePay, Affirm, Klarna and PayPal. All online payments on oliverpeoples.com are completely secure, thanks to the Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption that is used for every transaction in order to ensure that your order is secure.
Shipping & Returns
We offer free shipping on all orders within your country. Orders are usually delivered in 5 business days and are fully insured against loss, theft or damage.
Free Returns Return any product purchased on oliverpeoples.com for any reason within 45 days of receiving the shipment, and enjoy free returns from anywhere in the world.
