Why a Licensed Optician Makes All the Difference
Getting the perfect pair of glasses is about more than just picking a stylish frame. It requires clinical precision, specialized training, and a customized fit. That is where a Licensed Optician comes in.
To ensure your eyewear is made to the absolute highest standards, we guide you through a personalized, multi-step fitting process.
1. Precision Frame Fitting
Every prescription fitting requires the frame to be on your face. Because every nose bridge is shaped differently, a frame may sit higher or lower on your face depending on its size. Seeing the frame on your face allows us to precisely calculate your segment placement for multifocal lenses.
2. Accurate Physiological Measurements
No two faces are perfectly symmetrical. We take exact monocular pupillary distances for both near and distance vision. If your pupils sit at slightly different levels, we adjust and compensate for this so your vision remains crystal clear.
3. Customized Lens Design & Consultation
We evaluate your specific prescription, lifestyle needs, and frame choice to recommend the ideal lens materials and coatings. To ensure the easiest transition to your new eyewear, we can even analyze the "habitual base curve" of your current lenses—so please bring your old glasses to your fitting!
4. Rigorous Quality Control
Once your custom measurements are sent to our manufacturing lab, our work isn't done. When your lenses arrive, they go straight to our laboratory bench. We meticulously verify that the finished product perfectly matches your doctor's prescription and our exact measurements.
5. The Final Custom Dispensing
When you collect your new eyeglasses, we don't just hand them over. We carefully adjust, align, and balance them to your face. This ensures maximum physical comfort while aligning the optics perfectly with your eyes so you see—and look—your absolute best.
