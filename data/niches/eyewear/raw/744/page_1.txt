Who we are...
Tracy M. Law, MPT
Tracy is co-owner of APT and a native of Americus. He has over 20 years experience in physical therapy and received his Associate Degree in physical therapy from the Medical College of Georgia in 1991. He later received his Bachelor of Science in Exercise Science from Georgia Southwestern State University in 1999. Tracy received his Master of Physical Therapy from the Medical College of Georgia in 2001.
Tracy has attended several courses in Postural Restoration, McKenzie Method of Rehabilitation, Shoulder and Knee Rehabilitation, and Sport Specific Rehabilitation, which he incorporates in his daily treatments.
Tracy is very involved in his community serving as Chamber of Commerce Chairman for several years as well as serving on numerous boards and committees for organizations here in Americus and Sumter County. Tracy also gives back to the community by assisting Southland Academy with their athletics by providing care for their athletes on the field.
Tracy and his wife, Lisa, are the proud parents of twin boys, Luke and Landon. They attend First Baptist Church in Americus where Tracy enjoys singing in the choir.
Alex G. Saratsiotis, MPT
Alex is co-owner of APT and a native of Americus. He received a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics from Georgia Southwestern State University in 1998 and a Master of Physical Therapy from the Medical College of Georgia in 2000.
Alex has furthered his education and skills by attending several sports rehabilitation courses with emphasis on foot, knee, hip, and shoulder rehabilitation. He has attended courses on the McKenzie Method of Rehabilitation and Postural Mechanics that are incorporated in his daily treatments.
Alex is very involved in the community serving as chairmen of the One Sumter Foundation as well as serving for many years on the Sumter County Developmental Authority.
Alex and his wife, Mandi, are the proud parents of Mary Katherine, Isabelle and Caroline.
Julie Eubanks, MPT
Julie moved to Americus 7 years ago from Naples, FL and she considers herself a Georgian now. She has over 20 years of experience in a variety of physical therapy settings including home health, neuro-rehab, and outpatient. She received a Master of Physical Therapy degree from Nova Southeastern University in Ft. Lauderdale, FL in 1999 and went on to complete a certification in Mechanical Diagnosis and Therapy for cervical and lumbar treatment through the McKenzie Institute in 2003. Julie has taken a number of continuing education courses on specific joint rehabilitation techniques, a variety of taping methods, and core stability training utilizing stability balls. She is committed to learning all she can to best serve her patients. Julie is an active member of Williams Road Church, and she also acts as their Administrative Assistant. Julie has been married to her high school sweetheart, Keith, for 31 years and they have 2 children, Jake and Delaney. If you don't see Julie at APT, she's probably taking care of horses, chasing an animal of some sort, or antique/thrift shopping.
Megan Davis,
Licensed Physical Therapist Assistant
Megan Davis is one of our licensed Physical Therapist Assistants. She has worked as a PTA in a variety of settings including outpatient orthopedics, pediatrics, school systems, hospitals, and nursing homes. She brings with her 14 years of experience overall with the majority of her career focusing on orthopedics. She enjoys being able to assist patients after surgery and/or injury in returning to their normal activities of daily living and helping them to be able to participate in the hobbies they once enjoyed.
Megan is a Dooly County native and has lived in Vienna, Ga. all of her life. She has a husband of 18 years, 2 kids ages 10 and 13, 2 dogs, a horse, a goat and a cat. When not working Megan enjoys spending time with family, spending time outdoors with her animals, running, and she also likes reading in her spare time. Megan and her family attend Central Baptist Church here in Americus, Ga.
Megan graduated from the Darton College PTA program in 2008 and began practicing in 2009. She has served as a clinical instructor as well as a compliance/continuing education supervisor. She is hoping to eventually transition to becoming a Doctor of Physical Therapy where she can use her knowledge and skills gained while practicing to further enhance her education.
