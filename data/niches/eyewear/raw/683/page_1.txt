Visiting Our Boston Optometrist
You’ll find the best eye care solutions from our Boston optometrist. From routine exams to diagnosis and treatments serious conditions, the team at Eyes Over Copley cares about you and your eyes! As your Boston eye care provider, we understand the importance of not only being able to see, but having healthy eyes.
Excellent Eye Care For Boston
We value our customers, providing quality eye care for Cambridge residents and the surrounding areas. There are many different services offered at our facility, including:
Eye exams
Vision tests
Contact lens exams
Pre- and post-op LASIK Evaluations
Eye care education
Eye Exams with Your Boston Care Provider
Our eye exams are comprehensive and thorough. As your eye optometrist in Boston, our diagnostic and evaluation exams search for acuity, eye strength, underlying conditions and more. Visiting our Boston eye doctor on a annually can help prevent risks to your vision and eye health.
If you are looking for contact lenses, the vision tests associated with this type of eyewear are a little more detailed. Our optometrist will provide a series of test to provide you with the perfect pair of contact lenses. The type of lenses you wear will also depend on your lifestyle, including whether you work on a computer all day or participate in sports. Our job is to make sure your eyes are at their safest every day.
