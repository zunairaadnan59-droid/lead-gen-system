Dr. Tran is a San Diego native and proud to serve the community she calls home. She earned her Doctorate of Optometry from Western University of Health Sciences, College of Optometry, where she received specialized training in diagnosing and managing dry eye and ocular diseases, including glaucoma, cataracts, and macular degeneration. She is licensed to treat glaucoma and has full therapeutic privileges to prescribe topical antibiotics and steroids for a wide range of eye conditions.
Dr. Tran has a special clinical interest in myopia management and contact lens fittings, including lenses for astigmatism, monovision, multifocal designs, and MiSight contact lenses.
Outside the office, Dr. Tran enjoys camping, running, and spending time with her family. She is deeply connected to her community and shares that love with her three kids; you'll often find her at school drop-offs, local parks, or riding bikes and practicing soccer together.
