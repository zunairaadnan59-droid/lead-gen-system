About Us
Eye Elegance is Houston’s premiere optical boutique for handmade independent eyewear from around the world. We bring you an incredible selection of unique, beautiful and truly one-of-a-kind spectacles and sunglasses. Each individual frame has been hand selected for it’s exceptional design, craftsmanship and materials. Eyewear is a complex category. It’s as much fashion as it is prescription, making each eyewear purchase a very personal decision. Our boutique provides a relaxed and personable shopping experience shielded from pushy sales associates and mass produced frames. Our diverse team of optical stylists specialize in finding the right frames and lenses to meet your visual and lifestyle needs. We invite you to come see the difference at one of our Houston locations. Because in the right glasses you should not only SEE amazing, but LOOK and FEEL amazing too!
