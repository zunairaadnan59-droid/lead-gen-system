About Our Store
Cohen’s Fashion Optical offers a wide array of frames from renowned brands such as Tom Ford, Dior, Gucci, and many others. Our commitment to providing personalized eyewear extends to our team of dedicated eyewear specialists, who are ready to assist you in finding the perfect frames to match your unique style. We also offer the latest innovative lens technologies tailored to fit your lifestyle and vision requirements.
Our skilled optometrist is available Monday through Saturday to address all your eye care needs. For added convenience, we offer same-day service. Located at Stamford Town Center, we are easily accessible from various areas throughout Fairfield County, thanks to our proximity to nearby I-95. Whether you’re in need of an eye exam or looking to upgrade your eyewear, visit us today or use our online scheduler to book an appointment.
