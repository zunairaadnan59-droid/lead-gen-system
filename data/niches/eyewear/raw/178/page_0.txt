WELCOME
TO VILLAGE EYECARE
How We Are Keeping You & Our Employees Safe.
Village Eyecare is an established optometry practice providing comprehensive eye care in Chicago for over 50 years. Our goal is to provide our patients with the highest quality eye care in an inviting, pleasant and professional environment. We strive to offer the finest in optometry services, while giving you the personal attention you deserve from a qualified, caring eye doctor.
We understand how important it is to stay current with the latest in eye care advances, which is why we are proactive with continuing education. Whether it is technology or the latest in designer eyewear fashion, we believe our patients deserve the best. We stay abreast of the latest changes in treatments and products, offering you the highest quality service available in the area.
WHY
CHOOSE US?
OUR DOCTORS
Our experienced optometrists and staff care about your well-being and work to provide the best eye care possible.
DESIGNER EYEWEAR
Find the right eyewear for your face and lifestyle, with our wide variety of unique frames from around the world.
ADVANCED TESTING
With the latest diagnostic equipment, we can detect eye problems sooner, resulting in more effective treatment.
KERATOCONUS
At Village Eyecare, we have been treating patients with keratoconus and irregular corneas for over 20 years and now have one of the largest keratoconus clinics in Illinois.
ORTHOKERATOLOGY
Orthokeratology, also known as Ortho-K, is an FDA-approved, non-surgical treatment in which custom-designed corneal molds (similar to contact lenses) are used to gently reshape the cornea of the eye.
DRY EYE
Village Eyecare is equipped with the latest in diagnostic equipment that aids in the determining the cause of the dryness which allows for a more effective treatment plan.
ON-SITE LAB
Get your eyeglass order faster and made exactly to your doctor’s specifications.
PREMIUM LENSES
High quality lens materials, designs and coatings ensure maximum clarity and comfort.
COMMUNITY FOCUSED
We believe in delivering the best service because our community deserves it.
