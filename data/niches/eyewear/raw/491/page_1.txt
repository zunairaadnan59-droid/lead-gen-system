LUXURY EYEWEAR
For dreamers and doers, purists and free spirits — Occhiali Modern Optics is Dallas’ destination for
handcrafted luxury eyewear
that celebrates individuality and timeless design. Ordinary, simply isn’t in our vocabulary.Discover our thoughtfully curated collection of designer prescription glasses and sunglasses, featuring world-renowned brands known for craftsmanship, comfort, and distinctive style. Whether you have a prescription or simply appreciate fine design, we invite you to visit our boutique. Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments are prioritized. Experience the art of eyewear and see yourself in a new light at Occhiali Modern Optics.
DESIGNER SUNGLASSES
In our Dallas showroom, we offer a large selection of designer sunglasses, curated for those who appreciate true luxury and standout style. We offer most of all our designer brands in prescription and non prescription sunglasses. If you’re looking for high-end designer sunglasses in Dallas, Occhiali is your destination for Cartier, Gucci and many other designer frames, personalized service, and a high end optical experience. Discover sunglasses that do more than protect your eyes — they define your look.
STYLE AND FUNCTION
Every frame at Occhiali Modern Optics tells a story of passion, precision, and design excellence. We personally select each collection to bring our clients distinctive styles that embody quality, comfort, and sophistication. Beyond our curated eyewear, we offer premium lenses and a team of highly trained professionals dedicated to delivering exceptional vision care and a truly personalized experience.
