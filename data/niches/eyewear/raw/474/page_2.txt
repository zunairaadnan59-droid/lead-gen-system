Frequently Asked Questions
Who is Key-Whitman Eye Center?
Key-Whitman Eye Center is a comprehensive eye care practice
with 13 locations across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. Founded
over 60 years ago, the practice is led by board-certified surgeons,
including chief surgeon Dr. Jeffrey Whitman, and provides LASIK, cataract surgery, cosmetic eyelid procedures, and general eye
disease management.
What eye care services does Key-Whitman Eye Center  provide?
Key-Whitman offers vision correction (LASIK, PRK, EVO ICL),
cataract and lens replacement surgery, glaucoma treatment,
diabetic eye disease management, dry eye therapy, cosmetic and
oculoplastic procedures, and a full-service optical shop for glasses
and contacts. Patients with macular degeneration are monitored
and referred to the appropriate specialist as needed.
How many Key-Whitman Eye Center locations are there in  North Texas?
Key-Whitman has 13 locations including Dallas, Plano, Mesquite, Rockwall, Richardson, Frisco, McKinney, Ft. Worth, North and South Arlington, Grapevine, Greenville and Colleyville. So most patients in the metroplex have a location within a short drive.
Is Key-Whitman Eye Center a good choice for LASIK or  cataract surgery in Dallas-Fort Worth?
Key-Whitman's surgical team includes board-certified
ophthalmologists, and four of the practice's most experienced
surgeons hold additional board certification in both LASIK and
cataract surgery from the American Board of Eye Surgery, a
distinction held by only a small share of surgeons nationwide. The
practice also uses FDA-approved, state-of-the-art technology,
including femtosecond laser-assisted cataract surgery, and Dr.
Jeffrey Whitman has been recognized among D Magazine's “Best
Doctors” for over two decades.
Does Key-Whitman Eye Center accept insurance and offer  financing?
Yes. Key-Whitman accepts most major medical insurance plans,
and vision insurance often applies to routine eye exams and
eyewear. LASIK, EVO ICL, and premium lens upgrades are elective
procedures not covered by insurance; the practice offers financing
for these, and patients can confirm exact benefits by calling their
local office
