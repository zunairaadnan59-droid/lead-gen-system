surgery for cataracts

Lasik Eye Surgery
Leave your glasses and contacts behind. Getting FDA-approved LASIK Surgery with Dr. Hecht is one of the best ways to improve your vision.
Cataract Surgery
Cataract surgery is the most commonly performed medical treatments for people over 65. Dr. Hecht is an expert in the latest cataract surgery procedures.
Macular Degeneration
Macular degeneration is a leading cause of vision loss in Americans 60 and older. Visit Dr. Hecht for all macular degeneration screenings.
