Caring for the Eyes of Texas
Caring for the
Eyes of Texas
The Texas State Optical Clear Lake eye doctor believes in looking at the big picture when it comes to your eye health, so we take the time to get to know you, your eye care history and your vision needs. Dr. Joshua Devera knows that getting the right prescription involves balancing several factors, including clear eyesight, visual efficiency and your ability to process visual information seamlessly. Whether you need a comprehensive eye examination, an eyeglasses fitting or treatment for eye disease (such as glaucoma or macular degeneration) or eye surgery (such as LASIK), our Clear Lake optometrists will provide you with the best care, advice, options and follow up.
Schedule an appointment with our Clear Lake eye doctor to see how we can help you get the most accurate prescription for your eyes.
