Our Team
Clear Lake Eye Doctors & Staff
Welcome to the Texas State Optical eye doctors and eye care staff page! Our optical team is dedicated to providing quality eye care to the people of Texas. We pride ourselves on being professional and knowledgeable, and we are committed to working with patients of all ages to ensure that their eye health is properly taken care of. Whether you need a routine eye exam or have a more serious eye condition that requires attention, our experienced eye doctors are here to help. Our eye care staff is friendly and welcoming, and we strive to make every patient feel comfortable and at ease during their visit. Thank you for choosing Texas State Optical for your eye care needs.
Dr. Devera
Optometrist
Hi, I’m Dr. Joshua Devera. I became an optometrist because I genuinely enjoy helping people improve their quality of life through better vision.
Hi, I’m Dr. Joshua Devera. I became an optometrist because I genuinely enjoy helping people improve their quality of life through better vision.
I’m originally from Friendswood, Texas, and attended Texas A&M University, where I earned my bachelor’s degree in biology. Afterwards, I received my Doctor of Optometry degree from the University of Houston. In 2020, I started a new journey and opened a Texas State Optical in Clear Lake. I feel eye exams should never feel rushed. I take pride in ensuring you understand your eye health and feel confident in your treatment plan.
When I’m not in the office, you’ll usually find me spending time with my wife and two daughters, out on the golf course, playing games, or enjoying the outdoors. I’m also passionate about serving and staying connected to my church community.
