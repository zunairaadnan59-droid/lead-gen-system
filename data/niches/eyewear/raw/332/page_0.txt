Welcome to Legacy Optical
At Legacy Optical, serving Hampton Bays, NY, and the nearby regions, our eye doctor has been providing patients with reliable eye care services for many years. An eye exam to determine your prescription will help us fit you for eyeglasses or contacts. We also evaluate patients and determine if eye surgery is right for them.
Services We Provide
You may come to our office for a routine eye exam. During this visit, our optometrist will give you a visual acuity test. Assessments are comprehensive and will also include an evaluation for astigmatism as well as an eye health screening. The care we provide also includes writing prescriptions for contact lenses and eyeglasses. We take the time to help you find the right brand of lenses or style of glasses to fit your lifestyle, needs, and budget. If you are diagnosed with a condition that will require surgical intervention, we can make sure you are prepared before referring you to an eye surgeon we trust.
Why Us?
When it comes to the health of your eyes, you want an eye doctor you can count on. You want one that's easy to work with and who'll answer all of your questions. At Legacy Optical, we pride ourselves on our ability to foster a meaningful relationship with every patient. You'll feel comfortable in our hands, and we believe that's what the patient experience should be like.
About Our Staff
Our team consists of multiple opticians, optometrists, technicians, and other members who work together for one common goal -- patient satisfaction. Each member of our team has the appropriate training and licensure and will take every step possible to make your experience as easy for you as possible. Going to the doctor's shouldn't be something you dread. It should be something you feel good about doing for yourself. That's why we make sure our patients have a wonderful experience in our office. At Legacy Optical, we have the staff, tools, and dedication to make every aspect of your experience with us a positive one.
