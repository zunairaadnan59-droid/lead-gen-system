Appointments
To make an appointment with a Yale New Haven Health physician, you can contact their office directly (use our
Physician Finder
) or call the Yale New Haven Physician Services Referral Center at 888-700-6543.
Billing and Insurance
If you have questions about your bill, insurance coverage or financial assistance, please call
855-547-4584
. Our customer service staff is available to assist you Monday - Friday from 7:30 am - 5 pm. Please have the guarantor account number listed on your bill available when you call. Log in or register with
MyChart
to view detailed visit information and to send us a secure message.
