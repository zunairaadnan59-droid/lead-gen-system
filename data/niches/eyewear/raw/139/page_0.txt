Procedures
If you are living with vision problems or an eye condition or disease, Pacific Vision Institute offers treatment with the latest advances in technology to resolve even the most complex eye health and vision conditions.
Our state-of-the-art diagnostics and treatments for a wide range of vision problems include custom all-laser LASIK, cataract surgery, PRK/LASIK, EVO ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens), refractive lens exchange, Intacs for corneal reshaping, PTK, and advanced corneal transplant surgeries. Your eyes will be under the care of a team of acclaimed eye surgeons who have contributed to the development of innovations in eye surgery now employed in modern vision correction clinics across the
globe.

Research
Dr. Faktorovich has made several significant contributions to the field of refractive surgery, including award-winning research on corneal healing following laser vision correction. She has authored numerous published articles, abstracts and book chapters on advanced corneal transplant techniques and laser and refractive surgery, and frequently lectures to other professionals in her field on her groundbreaking techniques, both in the United States and abroad.

Lasik surgery
At Pacific Vision Institute, we offer our patients state-of-the-art, wavefront-guided, custom LASIK surgery. With digital optic mapping for a custom evaluation of your unique eye structure, the laser treatment profile is guided by digital mapping to achieve an unprecedented level of precision. Our advanced screening technology along with the latest generation all-laser LASIK systems elevates the accuracy of treatment, with faster healing and superior results. Advanced
Technologies
At San Francisco Pacific Vision Institute, we offer our patients the latest advances in technology, and a commitment to achieving superior outcomes. Our investment in state-of-the-art technology makes it possible for us to consistently achieve an unprecedented level of accuracy in treating a range of vision problems and eye conditions.
While not all patients are candidates for LASIK, we offer alternatives including PRK, Epi-LASIK, Wavefront-guided LASIK, Contoura Vision Topography-Guided Laser vision correction, IOLs for reading vision correction, IntraLASIK, Implantable Collamer Lenses, Widefield OCT Diagnostics, a full array of the latest developments in vision correction technology.
