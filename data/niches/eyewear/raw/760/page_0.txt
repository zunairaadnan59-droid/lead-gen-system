Not Just Another Optical Shop
If you've ever felt rushed through an eye exam, overwhelmed by endless frame choices, or disappointed that every optical store carries the same styles, you're not alone.
Urban Optiks was built for people who want something better: thoughtful eye care, expert guidance, and eyewear that reflects their personality - not the latest mass-market trend.
For more than 16 years, we've helped San Diegans see better, look better, and feel confident in eyewear they truly love.
Get To Know Us
A Place You'll Actually Enjoy Visiting
Most optical stores feel clinical. Others feel like retail chains.
We wanted something different.
Our Hillcrest boutique was designed to feel welcoming, creative, and personal - filled with natural materials, living plants, and carefully selected details that reflect the same individuality found in our eyewear collection.
﻿
The moment you walk in, you'll know you're somewhere different.
Tour Our Store
For People Who Want More Than Ordinary Frames
The average optical store carries many of the same brands. We don't.
Our collection focuses on independent designers and handcrafted eyewear from around the world with frames chosen for their design, craftsmanship, and individuality.
Whether your style is bold, artistic, understated, classic, or somewhere in between, we'll help you find eyewear that feels uniquely yours.
Because glasses aren't just something you wear. They're part of how you present yourself to the world.

Why Patients Keep Coming Back
Many of our patients first visit us because they're looking for something different. They stay because of the experience.
﻿
From first-time visitors to patients who've trusted us for years, we're proud to have earned hundreds of five-star reviews from people who appreciate personalized care, unique eyewear, and a team that genuinely values relationships.

Trusted By San Diego For More Than 16 Years
We're honored to have been recognized by readers, patients, and local publications as one of San Diego's favorite destinations for eyewear and eye care.

Better Technology. Better Vision.
You shouldn't have to choose between personalized care and advanced technology. At Urban Optiks, we combine both.
Our state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment allows us to provide highly accurate prescriptions, advanced eye health evaluations, and digitally customized lens solutions - all designed to help you see as clearly and comfortably as possible.
Because exceptional eyewear starts with exceptional vision.

Experts in Eyewear. Focused on You.
Finding the right eyewear is about more than choosing a frame. It's about finding something that complements your vision, your lifestyle, and your personality.
Our experienced doctors and opticians take the time to understand what matters most to you, offering expert guidance and personalized recommendations every step of the way.
