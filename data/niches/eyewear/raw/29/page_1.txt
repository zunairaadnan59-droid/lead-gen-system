About Assil Gaur Eye Institute
Absolute excellence in eye care since 1949
How it all started…
The Assil Gaur Eye Institute (AGEI) represents the highest standards of ophthalmology and patient care in the country. This commitment dates back to 1949, when the Institute was chartered by the father of modern cataract surgery, Dr. Robert M. Sinskey.
Dr. Kerry Assil began his career as an academic ophthalmologist, pioneering techniques and inventions in refractive surgery.  From there, Dr. Assil became a renowned surgeon and leader in the field of Cataract and LASIK surgery.
In 1995, Dr. Assil was invited by Dr. Sinskey to pursue their mutual vision of building a world-class private eye institute in Los Angeles. Dr. Assil became Chairman and Medical Director of The Sinskey Institute and grew it into what it is today: the Assil Gaur Eye Institute.
The ASSIL GAUR EYE INSTITUTE TODAY
Under Dr. Assil’s leadership, the Assil Gaur Eye Institute has assembled a team of top ophthalmology specialists from across the country who continue AGEI’s tradition of providing patients with the highest-quality specialist eye care in the United States. In keeping with AGEI’s founding principles, our doctors have maintained the comfort and familiarity of their clinics, much as family-run medical practices used to be.
Today, the Assil Gaur Eye Institute is nationally recognized for its compassionate patient-centric care, its commitment to pioneering advances in ophthalmology, and its dedication to supporting the health and well-being of not just its patients but also its community.
A worldwide leader in developing state-of-the-art technologies and procedures
There is a meaningful difference between a practice that buys the latest laser and a practice that helps decide what the next laser should do. Assil Gaur Eye Institute has participated in dozens of FDA clinical trials, helping bring new procedures and devices to the public.
Dr. Kerry Assil
himself has trained more than 10,000 ophthalmologists worldwide in state-of-the-art surgical techniques. Many of the surgeons whom patients might see elsewhere learned their craft, at least in part, from him.
Dr. Assil’s lifelong commitment to advancing the field of ophthalmology is evident in his body of work, including the development of new surgical techniques and advances in laser surgery. He lectures internationally and has taught state-of-the-art surgical skills to well over 10,000 ophthalmologists worldwide. The Assil Gaur Eye Institute has participated in dozens of FDA clinical trials, either as the lead investigator or as a member of a multi-center team, testing new ophthalmological devices and surgical techniques.
Every eye sub-specialty under one roof
Except for academic institutes, most eye practices focus on one or two procedures. LASIK clinics do
LASIK
laser eye surgery.
Cataract
centers perform cataract vision correction procedures.
Retina specialists
handle retina cases.
When a patient has more than one issue, or other vision problems develop over time, they almost always have to be referred to another practice. That often means repeat tests at a new office and starting over with a doctor they have not met or who has not reviewed their history.
Assil Gaur Eye Institute is built differently.
For example, if a cataract evaluation surfaces an unrelated retinal concern, which is not uncommon, a colleague in the same practice can see the patient straight away. In addition, records are unified. Imaging is shared. Care plans are coordinated by all the doctors who have treated you because AGEI’s specialists collaborate every day.
That depth of care is something only a true institute can offer. Assil Gaur Eye Institute, that kind of ophthalmology practice.
What does AGEI’s exceptional care cost?
Here is the part of the conversation that usually gets skipped.
Yes
, AGEI is a world-class ophthalmology practice.
No
, it is not more expensive than other top-tier ophthalmology practices in Los Angeles.
Patients are not paying extra for the prestige or extensive experience. They are paying market rates and receiving elite care, with substantially more specialized care and experience than they would get at any comparably priced peer.
Compared to any other ophthalmology practice in town, Assil Gaur Eye Institute offers a fundamentally very different product and level of care, and the gap is not small.
AGEI, giving back to the community
While the doctors at Assil Gaur Eye Institute are proud of their academic accomplishments and reputation for providing state-of-the-art vision care, one of the Institute’s founding principles is giving back to the community.
That’s why AGEI has participated in charitable partnerships at the local and international levels. Locally, AGEI has partnered with the Southern California Lions, providing pro bono services through the Assil Sight Fund, which continues to deliver charitable eye care to Southern Californians weekly. Our surgeons also travel internationally on medical missions to underserved populations.
AGEI, Los Angeles’s world-class eye institute
Los Angeles’ leading LASIK, cataract, and retinal surgeons are available to you, all in one practice.  State-of-the-art treatments for all eye conditions, including glaucoma, macular disease, dry eye, and diabetic eye conditions, to name just a few.
Assil Gaur Eye Institute remains laser-focused on our founding mission of providing exceptional health care in a compassionate and caring environment. We continue to be driven to advance the field of ophthalmology and give back to our community every day.
