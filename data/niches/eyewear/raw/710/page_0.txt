Eyeclectic Vision Source is an independent optometry practice dedicated to outstanding, personalized care.
We are passionate about optimizing eye health and bringing the most advanced eye care to the Greater Atlanta area. This includes innovative treatments, precision diagnostics, and the best frame and lens technology.
Our optometrists focus on overall eye health, pre-and post-operative care, eye disease, myopia management, and custom lens fitting. We create an individual plan with our patients to resolve their issues and ensure the protection of their vision.
Visit us at Howell Mill Plaza and take advantage of our convenient hours, same-day service, and high-quality eyewear. Get your glasses made here any day of the week!
