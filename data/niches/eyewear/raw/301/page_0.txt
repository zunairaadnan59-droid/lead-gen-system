Welcome to Main Street Optics
If premium quality and timeless style are what you look for when choosing new glasses, Main Street Optics is the place for you. Although we are proud to be the number one choice for discerning eyewear customers in Southampton, NY, we service customers from Manhattan to Montauk and everywhere in between.
Modern vision solutions that are tailored to fit you.
Our commitment to luxury means that our selection of glasses is more than just eyewear—they're an extension of your personality. We have the most exclusive selection of designer frames in all shapes, colors and sizes. You can choose from popular brands like Lindberg, Tom Davies, Mykita and Chanel. We have all the latest styles on hand, and we're always adding new frames to our inventory.  With our wide selection of sunglasses and prescription eyewear, you'll be sure to find a pair that's perfect for any occasion.
