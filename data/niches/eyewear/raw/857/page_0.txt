Personalized Contact Lens Care for South LA
If you’re considering contact lenses, our eye doctors provide detailed exams and fittings to make sure you get the right lenses for clear and comfortable vision. Whether you’re trying them for the first time or updating your current prescription, we’ll walk you through your options and help you find the best fit for your eyes.

Onsite Lab Service: Fast, Accurate, Local
We can make your lenses here at Los Angeles Eye Center & Clinic!
With our on-site lab, we create a wide variety of lenses, including single vision, multifocal, and progressive styles, available in clear or tinted options. Many prescriptions are ready the same day, often while you wait.
If you need your glasses even faster, our rush service offers next-day pickup in most cases. The fee for expedited orders typically ranges from $10 to $40, depending on your lenses.

Eye Exams for Kids, Adults, & Seniors
Vision Care & Eye Exams in South Los Angeles
We strongly recommend regular eye check-ups starting from six months of age, as we believe in the vital importance of maintaining your invaluable sense of sight. It's crucial not to take your eyesight for granted.
By scheduling annual eye exams, you can safeguard your vision and detect any subtle changes or hidden problems that might affect your eyesight early.

Diabetic & Glaucoma Eye Care You Can Rely On
Protect Your Vision with Trusted Eye Disease Management
Managing diabetes or glaucoma requires consistent, reliable care, and that’s what we provide. Using advanced technology like OCT imaging and visual field testing, our eye doctors monitor changes to protect your vision. With regular eye exams and a proactive approach, we help you maintain your eye health for years to come.
