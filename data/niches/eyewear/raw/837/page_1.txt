We Are a Leading Security Protective Services Company
Our team was formed to meet the demand for protective services in Mexico and Latin America in the 2000's. Since then, the company has gradually moved beyond our initial niche, to secure our clients'​ assets, infrastructure and personnel around the world.
Who We Are:
We are a strategic level security management group comprised of highly experienced professionals that come from diverse backgrounds, ranging from industrial security, to financial services, law enforcement, military special operations, and intelligence arenas.
Our Philosophy:
With EESS, every person on our team, every service we provide, every deliverable we commit to will demonstrate a genuine level of care and commitment. We do not search out the lowest common denominator for the highest profit margin. Instead, we focus on meticulous screening and systematic training of qualified personnel who are motivated and committed to do their best.
