What Is Neuro-Optometry?
Neuro-optometry is the specialty that looks at how the brain and visual system work together. Dr. Geiger evaluates and treats vision problems that can occur after neurological conditions like concussions, strokes, brain injuries, or movement disorders. By identifying how these issues affect binocular vision and daily function, she helps patients improve quality of life while working closely with a team of healthcare professionals for comprehensive care.
How Can Neuro-Optometry Help?
Neuro-optometry uses the science of neuroplasticity to retrain the brain after injury or neurological conditions. Through specialized techniques like therapeutic lenses, prisms, filters, and vision therapy, Dr. Geiger helps patients improve visual comfort, coordination, and processing skills. These treatments support recovery and enhance daily function.
