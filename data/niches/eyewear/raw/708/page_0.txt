Welcome to Eye 1st Vision Center - Your Optometrist In Sandy Springs, GA
Optometry for Sandy Springs, Marietta, Brookhaven, Roswell, and Atlanta, GA
At Eye 1st Vision Center in Sandy Springs, GA, we are committed to providing exceptional eye care services for you and your family. Our team of experienced eye doctors is dedicated to helping you maintain optimal eye health, whether you need an eye exam, prescription eyeglasses, contact lenses, or specialized treatment for conditions like astigmatism, glaucoma, or macular degeneration.
Comprehensive Eye Care Services
As a trusted optometrist near you, Eye 1st Vision Center offers a wide range of eye care services to address all your vision needs. From routine eye exams to specialized care for conditions like diabetic retinopathy, cataracts, dry eye, and Keratoconus, our team is here to ensure your eyes remain healthy and your vision stays sharp. We also offer contact lens exams to ensure you find the right contact lenses for your specific needs.
Expert Eye Exams for Your Vision Health
Regular eye exams are essential to maintaining your overall eye health. At Eye 1st Vision Center, our eye doctors provide thorough eye exams to detect early signs of common conditions like glaucoma, cataracts, and macular degeneration. If you're experiencing symptoms such as blurry vision, we’ll help determine the cause and recommend the appropriate treatment. Whether you need new eyeglasses or contact lenses, we provide solutions that suit your lifestyle and vision requirements.
Eyewear and Contact Lenses
At Eye 1st Vision Center, we understand the importance of clear and comfortable vision. Our office offers a wide selection of high-quality eyeglasses and contact lenses for all vision types. From daily contacts to lenses designed for astigmatism or dry eye, we have options to meet your unique needs. Our team will help guide you in choosing the best eyewear or contact lenses for comfort, clarity, and style.
Advanced Treatments for Eye Conditions
Our eye doctors are trained to manage a variety of eye conditions, including dry eye, glaucoma, and diabetic retinopathy. We take a proactive approach to diagnosing and treating conditions like Keratoconus and astigmatism to help preserve your vision and prevent further complications. If you're noticing symptoms such as blurry vision, it’s essential to schedule a comprehensive eye exam with our team to catch any potential issues early.
Why Choose Eye 1st Vision Center?
When you need a trusted eye doctor in Sandy Springs, GA, Eye 1st Vision Center is here for you. With a team of skilled optometrists, we provide personalized care and offer the most up-to-date treatments available. We take the time to understand your specific needs and deliver solutions tailored to you. Whether you need a routine eye exam, prescription eyeglasses, or contact lenses, our team is here to provide the best eye care possible.

Optometry Services at Eye 1st Vision Center in Sandy Springs
Eye 1st Vision Center offers a full suite of eye health and vision care services for all ages and eye conditions. You'll benefit from such services as:
Comprehensive eye exams
- Regular eye exams allow us to check your eye function, coordination, tracking, peripheral vision, color vision and other essential for accurate vision.
Diagnosis and treatment of eye diseases
- Dr. Shida can detect sight-threatening diseases such as glaucoma, macular degeneration, cataracts, dry eye syndrome, keratoconus, etc. Our optometrist can then recommend the proper course of treatment for your condition.
Diagnosis of disease-related eye disorders - If you are showing signs of a diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma aggravated by hypertension or other eye disorders linked to systemic ailments, we will advise you on the proper avenues of treatment to control these conditions before they can rob you of your eyesight.
Pre-operative and post-operative eye surgery care
- If you are undergoing cataract surgery, LASIK vision correction or some other form of eye surgery, our team can run the necessary evaluations to ensure that your surgeon can perform the most accurate procedure possible. Dr. George Shida can then perform all the necessary follow-up eye evaluations and vision testing to monitor your recovery.
Vision testing
- We can check your vision on a regular basis to determine whether you're having trouble due to refractive problems such as nearsightedness, farsightedness, astigmatism or presbyopia.
Corrective lenses
- If you need corrective lenses, we can write the necessary prescription and provide you with a variety of options, including a wide range of contact lens types.

Dr. George D.
Shida, O.D.
Optometric Physician
Dr. George Shida graduated from the University of Maryland with a B.S. in Pre-Med Biology. He played football, lettering for three years. Dr. Shida received his Doctor of Optometry degree in 1980 from the University of Alabama/Medical Center at Birmingham. He began his private practice career in 1981 in the Atlanta area, where he continues to practice. His specialty is family and medical eyecare, pre and post operative surgical care, as well as eyeglasses

Dr. Clare
Masson
Dr. Masson, having grown up as the daughter of an optometrist, has years of experience working in the optometric field. She graduated magna cum laude from Eastern Michigan University with a B.S. in Chemistry. She completed her Doctorate of Optometry in Phoenix, Arizona at Midwestern University and earned membership into Beta Sigma Kappa Optometric Honor Society. While in optometry school, she was president of Lions Club, participated in mission trips to Mexico, and volunteered locally at events such as the Special Olympics. Before moving to the Atlanta area, Dr. Masson had the opportunity to work alongside her father in private practice for four years.
