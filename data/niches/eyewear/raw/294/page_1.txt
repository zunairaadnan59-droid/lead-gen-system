About Us
About Drs.' Dery, Eads, Barton & Witten
Drs.' Dery, Eads, Barton & Witten provides the highest quality, compassionate optometry care to ensure your family enjoy a long, happy, and healthy life.
Our Story
Since 1971, Drs.’ Dery, Eads, Barton & Witten has been the leader and preferred provider of quality vision care products and personalized optometric services to our patients in Naples and the surrounding areas. Our experienced doctors and staff offer comprehensive vision examinations and specialize in the diagnosis and treatment of a wide array of eye diseases, conditions, and problems.
In our mission to provide the best eye care services possible, our doctors use only the most advanced, state-of-the-art diagnostic technology and eye care products available. We are committed to educating our patients and providing personalized eye care services to the people of Naples. At Drs.’ Dery, Eads, Barton & Witten, you will find eye care professionals who genuinely care about your health and are dedicated to providing exceptional personal service to everyone who walks through our door.
