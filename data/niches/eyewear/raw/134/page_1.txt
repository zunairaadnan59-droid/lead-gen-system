About Drs. Hiura & Hiura Optometrists
At Drs. Hiura & Hiura our patient’s health and visual needs are our top priority. Our goal is to take time, listen, and properly address each visual problem. We carry a huge selection of spectacle eyeglass frames and sunglasses to fit within anyone’s budget. We also dispense almost every type of contact lens and specialize in hard to fit patients such as those with astigmatism, gas permeable contacts, scleral, and multifocal contacts.
We are a third generation private practice optometry office that has been providing each patient with the highest quality vision solutions and exceptional customer service since 1949! With over 60 years of experience and the most up to date technology we strive to keep your vision and ocular health in excellent shape.
