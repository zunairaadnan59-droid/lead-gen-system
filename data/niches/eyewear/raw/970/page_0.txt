What is an AI Competitive Visibility Report?
An AI Competitive Visibility Report shows how your practice appears across Google, AI assistants, directories, reviews, and other visibility signals that influence patient decisions. It helps you understand where you stand today, how you compare to local competitors, and where opportunities exist to improve visibility—including through ongoing
competitive analysis
and the
AI Visibility Growth Plan
.
How is AI visibility different from traditional SEO?
Traditional SEO focuses on helping your website rank in search results. AI visibility focuses on whether AI platforms and recommendation engines recognize, understand, and recommend your practice when patients ask for provider recommendations. Both matter, but they are not the same.
Is the AI Competitive Visibility Report really free?
Yes. The report is provided at no cost and gives you a snapshot of how your practice compares across key visibility signals. Our goal is to help eye care practices understand their online visibility before deciding whether additional improvements are needed.
How long does it take to receive my report?
Most reports are delivered quickly after submission. The exact timing may vary depending on the number of competitors analyzed and the data available for your market.
What information will I see in my report?
Your report includes visibility scores, competitive comparisons, visibility gaps, local rankings, and insights into the factors that may be helping or limiting your visibility across Google, AI recommendations, reviews, and
online business listings
. See how you compare in our
competitive analysis
overview.
Why does visibility matter if I already have a good reputation?
A strong reputation is important, but patients can only choose providers they find. Even highly regarded practices can lose opportunities if competitors are easier to discover, appear more often in recommendations, or have stronger visibility signals across the web.
What happens after I receive my report?
You’ll have a clear understanding of your current visibility strengths and weaknesses. If you want help improving your visibility, Eyevertise can review the findings with you and discuss strategies through the

How often should a practice evaluate its visibility?
Visibility should be reviewed regularly because competitors, patient behavior, search engines, and AI platforms continue to evolve. Regular evaluations—and
competitive analysis

Patients and AI systems evaluate many signals beyond reputation alone, including
reviews
