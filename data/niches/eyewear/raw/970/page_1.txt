About Eyevertise.
Answered.
Common questions about who we are, who we serve, and how eye care practices work with Eyevertise.
What does Eyevertise do for eye care practices?
Eyevertise helps eye care practices become more visible wherever patients search and research online—across Google and maps, reviews, directories, AI assistants, and practice websites. We monitor performance, identify opportunities, and help practices improve visibility over time through the
AI Visibility Growth Plan
.
How long has Eyevertise worked with eye care?
Eyevertise has served eye care practices for more than 20 years. That experience helps us understand how patient behavior, search engines, directories, and AI platforms affect how practices are discovered and chosen.
Do you work only with eye care practices?
Yes. Eyevertise is 100% focused on optometry, ophthalmology, and vision care. We do not serve general businesses or other healthcare specialties. That specialization shapes our reporting, recommendations, and support.
How is Eyevertise different from a general marketing agency?
Most agencies sell campaigns or one-time projects. Eyevertise focuses on ongoing visibility management for eye care—
reviews
,
listings
,
search
,
Website Quality Management
, and
competitive analysis
—with reporting that shows whether patients can find and trust your practice across the channels that matter today.
Can Eyevertise support multi-location practices?
Yes. We work with single-location and multi-location eye care organizations. Visibility, reviews, listings, and competitive analysis can be reviewed across offices so leadership understands performance at both the practice and location level.
