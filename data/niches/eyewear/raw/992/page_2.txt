Colaizzo Opticians is located at 1623 Queen Anne Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109, in the heart of the beloved Queen Anne neighborhood. Just a short drive from downtown Seattle, the shop is easy to reach from all parts of the city.
From I-5 North or South
: Exit at Mercer Street, continue west, turn right onto Queen Anne Ave N, and head up the hill. Colaizzo is on the left between Blaine and Garfield Streets.
From Ballard or Fremont:
Head south on Fremont Ave N and cross the Fremont Bridge, turn right on Florentia St and then left on to Queen Anne Ave N.
Conveniently Located in Queen Anne, Just Minutes from Downtown Seattle
Easy Street Parking & Walkable Neighborhood:
Street parking is easy to find along Queen Anne Avenue, with both free and paid options nearby. Surrounded by walkable shops and cafes, Colaizzo Opticians makes it easy to stop in and enjoy the neighborhood.
