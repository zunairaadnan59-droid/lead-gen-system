About Colaizzo Opticians in Queen Anne, Seattle
At Colaizzo Opticians, we believe eyewear should be as intentional as it is expressive. Our Queen Anne boutique showcases independent designers known for precision, artistry, and bold design — each frame hand-selected to reflect your unique style.
Founded on Vision, Fueled by Style
A Queen Anne original since 1989, inspired by art, individuality, and fearless design
Established in 1989, Colaizzo Opticians has built a reputation for bold, design-forward eyewear and a distinctly modern boutique experience. Located in Seattle’s Queen Anne neighborhood, we curate a standout selection of independent brands that blend art, fashion, and individuality. For those who believe frames should make a statement, our boutique offers a carefully chosen collection that’s anything but ordinary.
Discover Your Next Pair
Our certified opticians bring decades of expertise, ensuring every frame is expertly selected and adjusted for a perfect fit.
Our Team of Opticians
Our specialists don’t just customize tints for style and clarity—they oversee every detail of your lenses. With over 20 years of experience, our in-house lab ensures a precision fit by individually tracing each frame. We proudly use only the highest quality lenses from Zeiss and Shamir to deliver both function and flawless craftsmanship.
