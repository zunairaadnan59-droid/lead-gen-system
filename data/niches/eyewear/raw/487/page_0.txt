About Us
Glasses For Every Occasion
Image Eyewear is a designer eyewear boutique, founded, owned and operated by Jeff and Michelle Brown. We opened the original location on Oak Lawn in 1993 and for over 29 years have been serving the finest eyewear to the Dallas, Park Cities, University Park and Uptown communities. We offer extensive collection of designer eyewear to suit the most discriminating tastes. Jeff and Michelle provide a team approach to each client’s needs & it is our emphasis on personalized customer service that has made Image Eyewear the place to shop for your prescription eyewear, including sunglasses.
Innovation For You
No one wants to wear glasses that everyone else wears; eyewear is how we express our individuality. That's why we focus on unique products that will make you stand out from the crowd.
We offer a complete range of prescription lenses. Single vision, bifocal and progressive lenses will be made in accordance with a current prescription from your Optometrist/Ophthalmologist.
Feel free to contact us with any questions and we are here to help!
