Why Choose Us
Northwestern Medicine is proud to have five hospitals ranked among “America’s Best” including Northwestern Memorial Hospital, the only Illinois hospital ranked No. 1 for 15 straight years, by
U.S. News & World Report,
2026 – 2027. Our physicians, care teams and scientists come together every day with a shared commitment to provide excellent patient care.
