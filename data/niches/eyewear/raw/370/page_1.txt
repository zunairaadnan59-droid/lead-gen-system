Our Office
Education first
At our office, we know the key to preventing disease is to fight it before it starts, which is why we pride ourselves on educating our patients on preventative medicine techniques such as reminding them to floss and schedule routine cleanings. We strive to give our patients “dental health care” instead of “disease care". Our thorough exams check the overall health of your teeth and gums, checking for oral cancer and taking x-rays when necessary. Always be sure to talk to your doctor about any medical history that may impact your dental health, such as new medications or related illnesses.
We prioritize safety
Around the office, we are always sure to provide a sanitary environment, because infection control is of the utmost importance to us. In our dental office, we maintain sterilization and cross contamination processes using the recommended standards of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), and the Center for Disease Control (CDC).

Constantly improving
We always want you to be confident in your dental office and know that we are a team of highly trained and skilled clinicians. As your health professionals, we take pride in providing the care you deserve to keep your smile healthy. We are consistently committed to continued education because we want to give you the best possible service and results. Our doctors and staff regularly attend dental lectures, meetings, and conventions to stay informed of new techniques, products, and equipment so that we are sure we are providing you with the most up-to-date services.
Patient focused
If there is anything we can do for you to make your visit to our office more comfortable, please let us know. We know visits to the dentist can be uneasy for some people, which is why we are here to help and be of assistance to you in any way possible. Our staff is dedicated to providing you with high-quality, personalized care. Please do not hesitate to ask for anything you might need to make your visit easier or more enjoyable.
