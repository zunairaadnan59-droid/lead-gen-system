About Us
About New England Work Injury
New England Work Injury is an association of independent sub-specialty orthopedic practices with a common goal of improving access, communication, and treatment outcomes in the care of the injured worker.
Understanding the potentially significant effect a work injury can have on one’s work and personal life, we want to minimize the economic impact, physical suffering, and emotional strain of work-related injuries.
Top Boston Doctors
We are a unique group of recognized experts in the field of Orthopaedic Surgery and Physiatry who have organized in an attempt to help the injured worker obtain expert medical care that leads to successful treatment and hopefully a return to their previous level of function.
Workers Compensation Services in Boston
Outcomes in the treatment of the injured worker lag behind those of non-work-related orthopedic injuries and conditions. By putting together
a group of specialists
who are recognized experts in their particular sub-specialties and who are interested in helping the injured worker we hope to improve access, communication, coordination, and the quality of care, all hopefully leading to better treatment outcomes and less disability.
New England Work Injury Mission
Our mission is to improve orthopedic care for the injured worker. By offering timely appointments and tailoring individualized treatment to the needs of the worker, our goal is to relieve discomfort, improve function, address and attempt to lessen disability, and return the injured employee to work as appropriate.
Why Choose Us?
Personalized Care
We provide individualized orthopaedic and spine  care in a personal and private setting. Our sole concern is what is in the best interest of our patients. We aim to provide a “concierge-like experience, spending as much time as is needed at each appointment and assisting our patients along every step of the pre-operative and post-operative process. Our staffs are professional, caring, attentive, and empathetic. Our goal is to provide you with an accurate diagnosis, review all of the options for treating your condition, have you make the best possible informed decisions, and take good care of you. We do not aim to be a “high-volume” practice – we treat one patient and perform one surgery at a time.
Experience and Expertise
Our surgeons trained at some of the best orthopaedic centers in New England and the United States   They  bring a vast amount of experience to the care of our  patients,  often gained from treating the most complex and challenging of conditions that present at both tertiary care and regional referral hospitals .
Results
The best results following surgery are dependent on obtaining an accurate diagnosis, identifying all aspects of a condition that cause pain and other symptoms, and implementing an appropriate, diagnostic-specific treatment plan. In short, the right diagnosis – the right plan of treatment – surgical excellence. As we aim to achieve the best results possible for our patients, our approach is conservative – making sure non-operative options have been exhausted – and when necessary, offering a surgical plan specific to the patient needs, and not more.
Ethics
Our practice treats one patient at a time, and our sole concern is what is in the best interest of our patients. Our surgeons perform one surgery at a time and do not use multiple operating rooms. Although some of our surgeons work with Orthopaedic Residents and Fellows, they perform the surgery and are responsible for all aspects of your care. They will be in the OR from the moment you fall asleep until you wake up.
No Conflict of Interest
As surgeons in private practices, our doctors are not subject to any corporate or departmental quotas, pressure to refer patients to any specific affiliated entity for imaging, physical therapy, or any other care-related issue, or any supposed “gainsharing” relationships. They are not under pressure to do many cases and generate RVUs. They are independent practices not owned by any hospital corporation,  insurance entity or private equity.
MGH and NEBH
Massachusetts General Hospital
and
New England Baptist Hospital
are the two top orthopaedic surgery hospitals in New England. MGH is the third oldest general hospital in the United States and the first teaching hospital of Harvard Medical School. This world-renowned medical center offers sophisticated diagnostic and therapeutic care in virtually every specialty and subspecialty of medicine and surgery. US News & World Report has ranked MGH in the top 5 hospitals in the United States most years since ranking has begun. In 2017-18 it was ranked the 7th top hospital in the US for Orthopaedic Surgery, and the #1 Orthopaedic hospital in Boston and New England. If your care requires admission to the hospital,  as a general hospital, the patients of MGH also have access to a vast network of expert physicians in all medical and surgical subspecialties.
NEBH is a 141 bed hospital specializing in orthopedic care and complex orthopedic procedures. NEBH is a regional, national and international leader in the treatment of all forms of musculoskeletal disorders. It is consistently ranked as one of the country’s top hospitals for orthopedics, receiving national recognition for high patient satisfaction, quality, and clinical outcomes.
It’s Your Decision
Patients choose surgeons in various ways: by word of mouth, from knowing others who have been treated by a particular surgeon, by researching their options, through recommendations of anesthesiologists and nurses and other health care professionals familiar with a surgeon, reading on-line reviews, and by primary care physician (PCP) referral. In addition, patients injured at work  may be directed to surgeons by Case Managers, Adjusters, their employer or lawyers. Orthopaedic care for an injury experienced at work, including possibly having surgery,  is rarely an emergency, or essential, and for the injured worker  the decisions to proceed with surgery, as well as the choice of surgeon, are yours to make.
