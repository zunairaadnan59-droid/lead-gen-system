Welcome to New England Work Injury
Proudly providing orthopedic work injury treatment in Boston, MA, and New England
Welcome to New England Work Injury. We are
orthopedic surgeons
who are in well-established, separate practices with a common dedication to providing expert care for injured workers. Our doctors are recognized leaders in their fields and believe that prompt and effective treatment offers the best chance of recovering from an injury and returning to an active life. Our goal is to provide high-quality, expert, ethical, and patient-centered care that is specific to the needs of the individual injured worker and his or her situation.
Job injuries can be complicated, and at times a worker can be left nervous and confused. The injured worker will benefit not only from our expert care but from our knowledge and experience in handling sensitive and important work issues. Consistent with this, we will strive to work closely and communicate effectively with the
patient
,
case managers
, the insurance company, lawyers, and the employer.
The injured worker needs great care from experienced doctors who listen and who have the workers’ best interest at heart. At NEWI our goal is the best treatment for you.
Our highly trained practitioners specialize in many areas of Orthopaedic and Spine surgery.
