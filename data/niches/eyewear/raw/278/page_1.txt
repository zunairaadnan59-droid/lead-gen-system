WELCOME TO EYE ASSOCIATES OF FORT MYERS
Eye Associates of Fort Myers is a full service ophthalmology practice specializing in dry eyes and cataract surgery. Our team of experienced ophthalmologists and medical staff are dedicated to providing the highest quality eye care for our patients. We are committed to delivering personalized treatment options and utilizing the latest advancements in eye care technology to ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients.

Our Story
At Eye Associates of Fort Myers, we meet your eye health needs through quality and professional services, with pride and integrity, in an open and honest spirit of teamwork, respecting the self-worth of our patients and families. Founded by Cecil C. Beehler, MD in 1966, Eye Associates of Fort Myers has been at the present location for over 25 years. Our highly qualified staff will exceed your expectations. Our commitment to excellence and quality is the reason our office has been earning patient's trust by providing superb results. Eye Associates provides expert adult and senior eye care with specialties in cataracts, glaucoma, diabetic eye care, and dry eyes. We treat a variety of eye problems including blurry vision, eye pain, and dry eyes.
