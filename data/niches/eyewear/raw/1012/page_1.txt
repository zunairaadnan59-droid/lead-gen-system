Your Trusted Eye Clinic in Vallejo
Redwood Eye Center is proud to serve Vallejo and the surrounding communities with comprehensive eye care that’s easy to access and even easier to trust.
Whether you’re visiting for
a routine eye exam
, a cosmetic consultation, or a surgical procedure, you’ll find our location welcoming, modern, and designed to support every patient’s comfort and needs.
Emergency Eye Care For Your Entire Family
Eye emergencies don’t always happen during business hours. Dr. Agadzi takes part in a county-wide on-call rotation with other ophthalmologists. During his scheduled weeks, he may return to the office after hours to care for urgent issues, including eye injuries.
We also partner with an after-hours call center that connects patients to the on-call provider when our office is closed.
If you are calling outside of regular office hours or on weekends, please stay on the line — the call will automatically forward to the on-call provider after a brief delay.
Post-emergency follow-up care is provided in-office to ensure comprehensive support and continuity of care.
