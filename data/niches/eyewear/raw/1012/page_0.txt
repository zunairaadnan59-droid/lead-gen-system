Quality Eye Care & Treatments
Optometry
»
Our optometrists provide comprehensive eye exams for all ages, including pediatric vision checks and senior care. Dr. Schrupp and Dr. Dao co-manage LASIK and cataract cases, screen for conditions like diabetic eye disease, and deliver continuity of care that makes every patient feel seen.
Aesthetics
»
We provide non-surgical cosmetic treatments to help you maintain a refreshed, natural appearance. Our services include Botox, Juvederm fillers, Latisse for longer lashes, and non-surgical blepharoplasty. All procedures are performed by experienced medical professionals.
Ophthalmology
»
As Vallejo’s only ophthalmology practice, we provide medical and surgical eye care for glaucoma, cataracts, and more. Dr. Agadzi performs SLT laser, iStent procedures, and cataract surgery, supported by in-house co-management from our optometrists.
Our Most Popular Vision Services
Optical Services
»
Our in-house optical features a wide selection of designer and budget-friendly frames, including luxury brands like Gucci, YSL, and Prada. With a full-service dispensary and contact lens fittings by our opticians, we make it easy to find eyewear that feels as good as it looks.

A Tradition of Vision Care
»
Serving Vallejo since 1938, Redwood Eye Center blends legacy with innovation. We partner with local schools to provide vision screenings for underserved students, and with the Vallejo Executive Lions Club to offer community services. Our mission is simple: delivering inclusive, relationship-driven care that reflects the diversity and heart of our community.
