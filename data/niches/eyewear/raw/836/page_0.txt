GET TO KNOW US!
Floats Eyewear was founded in 1986 and has been proudly based in Miami ever since. Starting with innovative polarized sunglasses that float on water, the brand has since expanded to include the Ego Eyewear collection, offering unique styles and quality products. Known for staying ahead of trends, Floats combines fashion, functionality, and affordability.
