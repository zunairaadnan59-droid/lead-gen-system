Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Do you treat dry eye?
Yes. In-office, we offer preservative-free artificial tears, prescription drops, punctal plugs, and warm compress and lid hygiene treatments. For advanced procedures like meibomian gland expression, IPL, radiofrequency therapy, and eyelid debridement, we coordinate care through our dedicated offsite dry eye facility. See our full
dry eye treatment options
for details.
Do you see children, and do you offer myopia management?
Yes. The American Optometric Association recommends a child's first eye exam at 6 months of age, and we offer gentle pediatric exams from there through adolescence. We also offer proven myopia management programs — including Ortho-K, MiSight contacts, and atropine therapy — to help slow nearsightedness when it's caught early. Learn more on our
pediatric eye exams & myopia management
page.
Can I get a same-day or urgent appointment?
Yes — same-day and next-day appointments are available for urgent eye needs. Don't wait for your annual exam if your eye is hurting now; call
480-515-2727
or see our
emergency & urgent eye care
page.
What insurance plans do you accept?
We work with most major vision and medical insurance plans, including VSP, EyeMed, Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Davis Vision, Medicare, and Spectera. See our
accepted insurance plans
, or contact us to verify your specific coverage before your visit.
What designer eyewear brands do you carry?
We carry over 800 styles of luxury eyeglasses, prescription sunglasses, and designer frames from 19+ global brands, curated for craftsmanship and style. Browse our
eyewear collection
or visit our North Scottsdale optical boutique to see them in person.
How fast is your in-house lens lab?
Most prescriptions are ready in about an hour, ground and fitted right here in our on-site lab — no shipping your frames away and waiting days or weeks. A small number of specialty cases may need to be sent to an outside lab, which takes longer. See our
precision lens lab
for details.
Where are you located, and what are your hours?
We're located at 18291 N Pima Road, Suite 120, Scottsdale, AZ 85255. We're open Monday–Thursday 9am–5pm and Friday 9am–2pm, closed weekends. Get directions or
request an appointment
