Meet the Team
O.D. · Optometrist
Dr. Tiffany Uelner
Dr. Uelner graduated in the top 10 of her class from the University of Houston College of Optometry in 2004, earning Clinical Letters of Excellence in pediatrics, contact lens, glaucoma, and family practice. She holds a B.A. in Mathematics and a B.S. in Biology from the University of Mississippi and is a recipient of the VSP Scholarship and the Seymour R. Marco Foundation Scholarship. Patients consistently praise her thorough exams and her ability to explain complex conditions in plain terms.

Dr. Matthew Campouris
