Prepare for your Eye Exam
What about your vision?
Is it 20/20 or better?
What about your visual fields?
Are they complete and match what
is normal for your age group? Have you suffered trauma at some
point in your life?
What about your Family History?
Risk factors for Cataracts, Age-Related Macular Degeneration,
Diabetes, Hypertension, Glaucoma?
What about your Visual System?
Cornea, Anterior Segment, Lens, Retinal, Optic Nerve Issues?
Do you need Special Testing because of Risk Factors?
SD-OCT,
Visual Field Testing, Corneal Topography, Pachymetry,
Retinal Photos, Extended Ophthalmoscopy.
How do your eyes feel?
Do they hurt, feel dry, tear, itch, burn,
turn red easily, are they swollen? Think you have an infection?
What about those lenses?
Digital, high index, trivex, transitions,
Progressive lenses, Polarized, Office Computer lenses?
Why and What do you need to optimize your vision?
What are your lens options?
Soft, Gas Permeable, Scleral,
Specialty, Bifocal?  What about Daily Wear, Monthly, Extended
Wear, Hybrid, Colors?
As our patient, your vision is our primary concern. We provide the highest standard of care
in optometry today. We also emphasize preventative vision care for all of our patients.
The gift of sight is a gift to be cherished!
