Meet the Family
Steven Richlin, O.D., P.C.
Dr. Steven Richlin is a board-certified therapeutic optometrist and founder of Richlin Eye. With decades of experience in advanced eye disease management and comprehensive vision care, he has built a reputation for clinical excellence, providing premier
eye care in Beverly Hills and Los Angeles
. Dr. Richlin continues to mentor the next generation of optometrists while providing personalized care to patients and families throughout the community.
Learn More About Dr. Richlin
Bradley Richlin, O.D.
Dr. Bradley Richlin is a glaucoma-certified therapeutic optometrist specializing in comprehensive eye care, dry eye treatment, and specialty contact lenses. Known for his thoughtful, patient-centered approach, he works closely with patients to develop customized care plans that support long-term eye health and visual comfort.
Learn More About Dr. Bradley
Peyton Glass Richlin, O.D.
Dr. Peyton Glass Richlin is a glaucoma-certified therapeutic optometrist who provides compassionate, full-scope eye care for patients of all ages. She has a special interest in family vision care and the management of refractive and cataract surgery patients before and after their procedures.
Learn More About Dr. Glass
Ora Esfahani, OD
Dr. Ora Esfahani is a glaucoma-certified therapeutic optometrist with expertise in ocular disease management, dry eye treatment, and specialty contact lenses. She is deeply involved in the optometric community and brings extensive clinical experience in managing complex eye conditions for both adult and pediatric patients.
Learn More About Dr. Esfahani
Lisa Limtiaco, O.D.
Dr. Lisa Limtiaco is a low vision specialist dedicated to helping patients with visual impairments regain independence and improve their quality of life. She works closely with patients experiencing conditions such as macular degeneration and diabetic retinopathy, providing customized solutions that maximize functional vision.
Learn More About Dr. Limtiaco
Lindsey Parnell, O.D.
Dr. Lindsey Parnell provides comprehensive eye exams and personalized vision care for patients of all ages. With a strong foundation in primary eye care and clinical training across multiple areas of optometry, she is committed to helping patients achieve clear, comfortable vision while supporting long-term eye health.

A True Family Practice
Richlin Eye is a family practice in every sense of the word. The team itself is a large family that in turn takes care of its patients’ families. Recognized among the
best eye doctors in Beverly Hills
, they offer pediatric eye exams and free vision checks for infants under 12 months old. Above all else, they strive to provide high-quality preventive, therapeutic, and developmental eye care, ensuring patients enjoy clear, healthy vision for years to come.
A State-of-the-Art Vision Center
Richlin Eye is a state-of-the-art vision and eye health care center in Beverly Hills, California. The practice’s team of dedicated professionals specializes in providing general eye care, medical eye care, contact lenses, and eyewear to patients of all ages, from infants to seniors.
Concierge-Level Access & Surgical Partnerships
Think of Richlin Eye as your personal concierge eye care office. Through years of cultivating relationships with many of the top eye surgeons in the community, we're able to connect patients with trusted specialists when advanced medical or surgical care is needed, whether for procedures like LASIK or cataract surgery. As some of the
best optometrists in Los Angeles
, we also know that eye emergencies don't always happen during business hours, which is why we offer an after-hours hotline so patients can reach us when it matters most.
Advanced Technology & Full-Scope Services
Richlin Eye offers modern offices and the latest technology available in the industry. The practice is equipped to perform a vast array of eye care services, such as treatment for glaucoma, eye infections, keratoconus, low vision, and macular degeneration. The practitioners also offer LipiFlow®, the most advanced treatment for dry eye disease. And they conduct exams for glasses and fittings for contact lenses, with more than 100 different types of lenses to choose from.
