About Dr. Richlin, O.D. & Associates
Home
About
Comprehensive, state-of-the-art eye care in Beverly Hills for patients of every age and stage of life.
A Modern Vision & Eye Health Center
Dr. Richlin, OD & Associates, is a state-of-the-art vision and eye health care center in Beverly Hills, California. The practice’s team of dedicated professionals specializes in providing general eye care, medical eye care, contact lenses, and eyewear to patients of all ages, from infants to seniors.
Our modern office is equipped with the latest technology in the industry, allowing us to deliver precise diagnostics and advanced treatment options in a comfortable setting.
Advanced Eye Care Services
We provide a comprehensive range of services, including treatment for:
Glaucoma
Eye infections
Keratoconus
Low vision
Macular degeneration
We also offer LipiFlow®, one of the most advanced treatments available for dry eye disease. In addition, we conduct comprehensive eye exams for glasses and offer contact lens fittings with more than 100 types of lenses to choose from, ensuring personalized solutions for every patient.
A Family-Centered Approach
Dr. Richlin, O.D. & Associates is a family practice in every sense of the word. Our team feels like a family, and we care for our patients the same way.
We proudly provide pediatric eye exams and offer complimentary eye exams for children under 12 months old, helping families establish healthy vision from the very beginning.
Concierge-Level Care & Surgical Referrals
Think of our office as a concierge eye care center. We maintain established relationships with many of the top eye surgeons in the community and coordinate advanced medical or surgical care when needed, including LASIK and cataract surgery. We operate in this capacity 24 hours a day and provide a dedicated hotline for emergency eye care, so our patients are always supported when it matters most.
Our Commitment
Above all, we strive to deliver high-quality preventive, therapeutic, and developmental eye care, ensuring our patients enjoy clear, healthy vision for years to come.
