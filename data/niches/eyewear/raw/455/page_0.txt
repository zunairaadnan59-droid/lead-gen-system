HELPING YOU PRESERVE YOUR VISION
It’s vital that you maintain your vision in order to take care of yourself and your daily needs. Physical changes in the eyes from age, disease, or a chronic condition can make it harder for you to live your life to its fullest. At Bright Eyes Vision Clinic, our eye doctors are ready to assist you with your eye care and give the help you need to maintain or improve your vision. Whether it’s for an annual checkup or an eye emergency, our team is here to help.
It All Starts with An Eye Exam
If you’re experiencing difficulty with your vision, it’s important to visit an optometrist before the issue worsens. Our eye doctors in Murphy, Plano, and Richardson will review your vision history, evaluate the structure of your eyes, look for signs of potential problems, and make a diagnosis based on their findings. After the exam is completed and a diagnosis is made, our team can help you receive the right kind of eye care to address and correct the vision issues you may be experiencing.
Our optometrists at Bright Eyes Vision Clinic use advanced diagnostic technology in conjunction with their years of experience in eye care. Patients benefit from the combination of experience and advanced technology in the form of the best possible diagnosis and treatment for any issue. The eye doctor can detect a problem early on in its formation and help the patient get ahead of the symptoms and conditions associated with the problem. If you are living with an eye disease or a chronic condition, the exam informs tour optometrist of any changes in your condition and the kind of treatment that is needed.
OUR EYE DOCTORS IN MURPHY, PLANO, AND RICHARDSON HELP WITH VISION CORRECTION AND MORE
Eye care doesn’t end with getting a diagnosis or status update on a chronic condition. The next step our team takes is to make a prescription that treats your specific vision issue, whether it be glasses, medication, or surgery. You’ll be able to see clearly again with the help of glasses, contacts, or LASIK eye surgery. Our optometrist can fit you with prescription sunglasses, for those times when you need vision and protection from the sun. Our team can also help you with vision issues caused by chronic conditions like glaucoma, cataracts, macular degeneration, and diabetic retinopathy.
