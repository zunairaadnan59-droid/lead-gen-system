FAQ's
What services does Pearle Vision offer?
Pearle Vision provides comprehensive eye exams, contact lens fittings,
prescription eyeglasses
, sunglasses, and a wide selection of
designer frames
at locally operated EyeCare Centers nationwide.
What vision insurance does Pearle Vision accept?
Yes. Pearle Vision accepts major vision insurance plans including VSP, EyeMed, UnitedHealthcare, and TRICARE at most locations, and also accepts HSA and FSA payments.
How much does an eye exam cost?
Eye exam costs
vary depending on whether you have eye insurance, your specific insurance plan, geographic location, and the services you receive. With insurance, the co-pay for an eye exam can range from $10 to $40, depending on your plan. On average, an eye exam without insurance costs between $50 to $200. We're committed to providing everyone the eye care they deserve, which is why we accept patients with or without vision insurance.
Does Pearle Vision have any special offers, discounts, or deals?
We offer several
exclusive eyeglasses and eye exam specials
depending on your location. Visit your neighborhood Pearl Vision location to take advantage of our specials and get the personal attention you deserve.
How do I find a Pearle Vision location near me?
Use the
EyeCare Center Locator
on the website to search by zip code or city for the nearest location, hours, and contact information.
Can I schedule an eye exam online at Pearle Vision?
Yes, you can
schedule an eye exam
directly through the website's locator tool by selecting your nearest center and choosing an available appointment time.
What designer eyewear brands does Pearle Vision carry?
Pearle Vision carries
brands
such as Ray-Ban, Oakley, Coach, Prada, Burberry, Michael Kors, Polo Ralph Lauren, Versace, Tory Burch, and many others.
