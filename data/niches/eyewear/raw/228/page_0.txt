Eye Care Treatments
Our services
LASIK Surgery
When you undergo
Bladeless LASIK
or
PRK
surgery with one of our certified surgeons, you will be benefiting from our many years of experience in performing refractive surgeries. We perform all LASIK surgeries in our state-of-the-art surgical center in AZ, where you’ll benefit from proven, effective, and the most advanced technologies that customize treatment to your unique eye conditions.
Cataract Surgery
Among our services, we offer
cataract diagnosis and treatment.
Our doctors are highly experienced in treating cataracts using next generation laser cataract technology. We also advise patients on the most suitable intraocular lens to restore clear vision. New systems allow us to accurately measure and correct cataracts in real-time during surgery.
Dry Eye Treatment
Our team is experienced in treating patients who suffer from
dry eyes,
a common condition that is caused by improper function of eye glands. We offer a variety of in-office treatments and prescription solutions when over-the-counter treatments are not enough.
Annual Exams
The optometrists at our centers are qualified to serve as your primary care eye doctors.
Annual eye exams
ensure that you keep seeing clearly and help detect any potential problems at an early stage. We are able to diagnose, manage, and treat all types of eye diseases and conditions, including glaucoma, cataracts, macular degeneration, and dry eyes.
Medical Treatment Exams
The doctors at Horizon are experienced in treating all types of eye disease and conditions. Our team includes specialists in corneal disease, neuro-opthalmology, retinal disease, oculoplastics and general opthalmology to care for all of your eye health needs.
Prescription Glasses and Contacts
Our optometrists use highly accurate measuring devices to provide you with an updated prescription. We can help you find a comfortable and attractive pair of eyeglasses at our on-site optical shop,
Horizon Optical,
now available at 5 of our Valley locations. We also offer a variety of contact lenses and provide fittings to ensure that you find the most comfortable pair possible
