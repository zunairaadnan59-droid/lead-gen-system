Preferred Footcare, LLC
has been dedicated to providing exceptional foot and ankle care for more than 20 years. Serving the community of
Stamford, CT
and surrounding areas, podiatrists
Jeremy Bier, DPM
,
Victoria Teti, DPM
and
Michael Sapienza, DPM
aspires to provide a professional and responsive environment to all patients.
We treat a variety of foot and ankle conditions such as ingrown toenails, hammertoes, bunions, heel pain, wound care, and diabetic foot problems. Our practice specializes in sports medicine and prides itself in providing the most advanced technology in foot and ankle care, with shockwave therapy, digital x-rays, diagnostic ultrasound, and more. Dr. Bier is a board certified foot surgeon and wound care specialist, and has been the current Director of Podiatry at Stamford Hospital since 2012.
Preferred Footcare, LLC offers
same day appointments
, accepts most insurance plans, and welcomes new patients. Our office is handicap accessible. If you have any questions or would like to schedule an appointment, call our office today!
