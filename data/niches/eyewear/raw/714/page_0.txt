WE CAN’T WAIT TO SEE YOU
Eye Center of Atlanta and Southern Eye Specialists
At Eye Center of Atlanta and Southern Eye Specialists, we’re proud to provide exceptional eye care for the whole family. Led by Dr. Rohit Sharma, our team delivers personalized vision care with a focus on your long-term eye health.
From comprehensive eye exams and emergency treatments to the management of ocular diseases, we offer a full range of services in a comfortable, patient-focused environment. We also carry a curated selection of designer frames, sunglasses, and contact lenses to suit every style.
Conveniently located in Atlanta, we accept a variety of insurance plans to help make quality eye care accessible and affordable.
FEEL GOOD-LOOK GREAT
FASHION OPTICAL FOR EVERY STYLE
Discover a wide selection of eyeglasses, designer frames, and sunglasses that suit every style: from vintage charm to bold, fashion-forward looks. We carry the latest European and American collections in a range of colors, materials, and designs, including titanium, stainless steel, and lightweight plastics. Think of us as your personal eyewear stylist - ready to help you find the perfect pair to match your unique vibe. Stop by anytime with your current prescription - no appointment needed.

FOCUS ON EYE HEALTH AT EVERY STAGE OF LIFE
We take a comprehensive approach to protecting and preserving your vision. Our medical eye care services include diagnosis and management of conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, glaucoma, and age-related macular degeneration. With advanced diagnostic technology and a commitment to patient-centered care, we’re here to support your eye health through every stage of life. Whether you need a routine exam or ongoing medical management, our team is dedicated to delivering expert care you can trust.
