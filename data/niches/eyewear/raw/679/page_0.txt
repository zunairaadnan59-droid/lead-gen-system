Services we provide
Learn about vision
Quality eye care and personal attention are our focus at Dartmouth Street Vision. We are a family optometry practice that prides itself on the individual care and attention we provide all of our patients. Your entire family is welcome at Dartmouth Street Vision. We are located across from the Back Bay T station.  Our specialties include  eye exams, contact lens exams and treating eye infections. Our goal is to serve our patients by helping them with their personal eye health, providing professional, high quality state-of-the-art eye care ~ all which we are committed to!
We invite you to visit our beautiful frame dispensary and choose from the latest in fashionable eye wear. We carry both affordable and high-end frames such as Ray-Ban, Original Penguin, GUCCI, Vera Wang, Paradox, Paradigm and many more!
Please give us a call or send us an email to schedule an appointment for your next eye exam or contact lens exam!
