Meet Our Doctors
Eye Care of Southwest Florida provides the highest quality, compassionate optometry to ensure you and your family are healthy and happy. Meet our caring and compassionate team committed to providing exceptional eye care for your family.
Jill Sagona, O.D.
Optometrist
Dr. Jill Sagona received her Doctor of Optometry at Nova Southeastern University College of Optometry in Ft Lauderdale, FL, and her B.S. in Nutrition at Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, OH. While at Nova, she was Vice President, College of Optometry Class of 2003.
