Individual Style
Leaders in Luxury Eyewear Styling
We are not simply opticians—we are eyewear stylists. Our approach extends far beyond standard frame selection. By carefully evaluating your face shape, coloring, and individual style, we help you select frames that complement and elevate your overall look.
Masterfully Curated Independent Eyewear
Our stylists specialize in distinctive independent eyewear brands from all over the world that are not commonly found in mainstream retail, allowing us to offer truly exceptional, design-forward pieces. To us, eyeglasses are more than vision correction—they are functional works of art, thoughtfully chosen to enhance the face and express personal style.
Premium Lens Designs
We are proud to offer the best lenses the industry has to offer. We know that lenses are not “one size fits all”, so we offer a wide selection of premium lenses from top tier progressive lenses to anti-fatigue lenses to sunglass lenses in a variety of color options. You name it, we can get it!
In-House Lab
We don’t leave the quality of the lens work up to just anybody. With a state of the art lens finishing lab on site, we can control the quality of the finishing work to uphold our high standards of excellence.
