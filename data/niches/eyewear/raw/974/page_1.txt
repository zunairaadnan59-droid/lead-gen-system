About us
Puget Sound Eye Care proudly serves individuals and families throughout Seattle and Bellevue, WA with comprehensive vision services and personalized support. Our eye care clinic focuses on building lasting relationships with patients by providing attentive care, detailed evaluations, and practical treatment recommendations. Many people searching for an optometrist near you want a trusted provider who understands both routine vision needs and more complex eye health concerns. Our eye doctors work closely with patients to support clear vision, healthy eyes, and long term visual wellness.

Services We Provide
We offer a wide range of services to address all your vision and eye health needs. Our expert team uses advanced diagnostic tools to provide comprehensive treatment options. Some of our services include:
•
Routine eye exams for all ages
•             Prescription
eyeglasses
and
contact lenses
•             Management of eye diseases such as glaucoma, cataracts, and macular degeneration
•
LASIK and other vision correction surgeries
•
Pediatric eye care
•             Contact lens fittings, including
specialty lenses

•
Myopia control
to help slow the progression of nearsightedness in children

Ortho-K (Orthokeratology)
for non-surgical vision correction with overnight lenses
A Patient Focused Approach To Eye Health
Every patient who visits our eye care clinic receives care that is tailored to individual lifestyle needs and visual goals. Our optometrist evaluates more than prescription changes during appointments and also checks for signs of eye disease, visual strain, and overall ocular health concerns. Patients often appreciate the time our eye doctors spend explaining findings and discussing treatment options in straightforward language. This patient centered approach helps individuals feel informed and confident about their eye health decisions.
Comprehensive Services For All Ages
We provide a wide range of services for children, adults, and older patients. Routine eye exams, contact lens evaluations, prescription eyewear guidance, and preventive screenings are all important parts of the care we provide. Our eye doctors understand that vision needs can change throughout life, which is why regular monitoring and early detection remain priorities during every visit. Patients benefit from consistent support that helps maintain visual clarity and healthy eyesight over time.
Modern Technology And Personalized Care
Advances in diagnostic technology allow our eye care clinic to evaluate eye health with greater precision and efficiency. Our optometrist uses modern equipment to identify subtle vision changes and detect early signs of conditions that may affect long term sight. Even with advanced technology, personal interaction remains an essential part of every appointment because communication helps patients understand their care more clearly. Many individuals looking for an eye doctor near you value having access to both modern diagnostics and compassionate guidance in one location.
Building Long Term Relationships With Patients
Strong patient relationships are an important part of the experience at Puget Sound Eye Care. Our eye doctors take time to learn about each patient’s work habits, visual challenges, and daily activities to provide recommendations that fit real life needs. Patients in Seattle and Bellevue often continue visiting our eye care clinic for years because they appreciate consistent care and familiar support. This ongoing connection allows our optometrist to monitor changes over time and respond quickly when new concerns arise.
Visit Our Seattle And Bellevue Offices

Our eye doctors remain committed to delivering personalized care, detailed evaluations, and dependable guidance for every stage of life. Whether you need preventive care, updated prescriptions, or ongoing support for vision concerns, our eye care clinic is prepared to help. Call Puget Sound Eye Care today to schedule an appointment and learn more about the services available at both of our convenient locations.
