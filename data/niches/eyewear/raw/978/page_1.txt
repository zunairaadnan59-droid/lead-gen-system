The Market Optical Experience
Founded in Pike Place Market and serving Seattle, Bellevue, and Meridian, ID, Market Optical is an independently owned boutique dedicated to style, craftsmanship, and personalized service.
At Market Optical, finding the perfect pair of glasses is as exceptional as the frames themselves. From personalized styling consultations to expert guidance on fit, shape, and design, every visit is tailored to you — leaving you inspired, stylish, and confident in your new look.
Our certified opticians bring decades of expertise, ensuring every frame is expertly selected and adjusted for a perfect fit.
Our Team of Opticians
Our specialists don’t just customize tints for style and clarity—they oversee every detail of your lenses. With over 20 years of experience, our in-house lab ensures a precision fit by individually tracing each frame. We proudly use only the highest quality lenses from Zeiss and Shamir to deliver both function and flawless craftsmanship.
