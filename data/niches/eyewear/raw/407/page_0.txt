Boca Family Eye Care Services
CUSTOMIZED EYE CARE
We do much more than just check your vision and provide you with eyeglasses. We provide comprehensive eye care.
ADULT EYE EXAMS
Annual eye health exams can detect eye and systemic diseases in the early stages when it is easier to prevent vision loss.
PEDIATRIC EYE EXAMS
Good eye health is important to your child’s learning, and vision problems can affect their performance in school.
SPECIAL EYE TESTING
Special tests are performed to customize treatment for conditions like dry eyes, blepharitis and ocular allergies.   We also specialize in RGP and Scleral lenses for patients with conditions like keratoconus.
