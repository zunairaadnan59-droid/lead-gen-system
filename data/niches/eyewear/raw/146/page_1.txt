Experience and Top-Quality Eye Care
Our eye doctors at Total Vision Richmond, formerly Richmond Vision Care, have been serving families in the Richmond district for many years, keeping our community’s eyes safe, healthy, and happy! We take a genuine interest in your individual needs and provide safe, effective treatment using top-of-the-line technology.
Our team of trustworthy, knowledgeable, and compassionate doctors is here to serve you and your family. From your
toddler’s first eye exam
to Grandpa’s new
transition lenses
, we have the answer to everyone’s eye care needs.
Our staff is happy to provide service in English and Russian.
Bring your family in to meet ours! Call us today!

Dr. Bonnie Lau, Optometrist
×
Dr. Lau is an honors graduate of the Southern California College of Optometry, where she was involved in organizations including the Omega Delta optometric fraternity.
Her clinical training included rotations at the VA Hospital in Salt Lake City, the Moran Eye Center at the University of Utah Medical School, the Hu Hu Kam Memorial Hospital serving the Gila River Indian Community of Arizona, and the Center for the Partially Sighted in Los Angeles, where she received special training in caring for patients with reduced vision.
She holds the most advanced license in California optometry, managing and treating many eye disorders and diseases. Patients enjoy seeing Dr. Lau because of her caring manner and thorough explanations.

Dr. Michael Arrieta, Optometrist
×
Dr. Michael Arrieta is a Bay Area native who is dedicated in providing compassionate eyecare. Dr. Arrieta received his Doctor of Optometry degree from the University of California – Berkeley and completed externships through the San Francisco Veterans Affair Hospital, Hayward Wellness Center, and Livermore Veterans Affair Hospital
Dr. Arrieta is passionate about helping others see better and takes pride in creating a welcoming and comfortable environment for every patient. His interests include prescribing glasses, fitting contact lenses, and screening patients for a wide range of eye conditions. Dr. Arrieta strives to ensure each patient receives personalized care tailored to their unique needs. From routine eye exams to the management of various visual conditions, Dr. Arrieta works closely with patients to achieve their best possible vision.
Outside of the office, Dr. Arrieta enjoys watching basketball, playing golf, and spending time with family and friends.

Total Myopia Program
Myopia, also known as nearsightedness, is a common refractive error that affects lots of children. Myopia causes distant objects to appear blurry and our team screens for myopia in each child’s
eye exam
. We work with you and explore myopia control methods that can help your kids enjoy clear, healthy vision.
We are also thrilled to announce the “
Total Myopia
” program!
The Total Myopia medical professionals and their dedicated team employ modern technology to assess, care for, and develop tailored treatment strategies for every patient. These comprehensive strategies include:
Customized eye drops, soft multifocal contacts, and overnight lenses
A systematic approach with scheduled follow-up appointments to help sustain eye health through consistent medical oversight and management
Assessments aligned with the individualized treatment plan for each patient
Thorough training on the proper usage, insertion, and removal of contact lenses
An ongoing maintenance regimen for effective myopia control, with a focus on achieving lasting results
