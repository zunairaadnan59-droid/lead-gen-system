About Us
University Ophthalmology Associates
University Ophthalmology Associates is an eye care practice with board-certified ophthalmologists providing expert services for Cataract Surgery, Comprehensive Ophthalmology, Cornea & External Disease, Glaucoma, and Refractive Surgery in Chicago.
University Ophthalmology Associates are pioneers and leaders in medical and surgical eye treatment in Chicago and the Chicagoland area.
Established in the 1920s, University Ophthalmology Associates is one of the longest-standing eye care practices in Chicago. Our physicians and staff are proud to carry on an established tradition of practicing high-quality eye care. We provide personal and compassionate services for all of our patients.
Comprehensive & Specialty Ophthalmology Services in Chicago
Our board-certified physicians offer full ophthalmology services using the latest technology available. We are proud to provide an array of eye care procedures. No matter what your condition, you can count on our ophthalmologists to provide you with the highest level of treatment and care.
Why Choose University Ophthalmology Associates
The ophthalmologists at UOA Chicago perform cataract surgery using only the latest technologies. This includes laser-assisted cataract surgery and advanced lens technologies.
In addition to the most current care in cataract treatment, our fellowship-trained corneal specialists perform corneal transplant surgery and LASIK surgery.
Moreover, our corneal specialists are experts in dry eye treatment. UOA also has a fellowship-trained glaucoma specialist who provides medical and surgical treatment of glaucoma.
