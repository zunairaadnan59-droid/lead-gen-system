Meet Jonathan I. Macy, MD | Ophthalmologist
Dr. Macy
earned both his bachelor's and medical degrees at Boston University and completed an internal medicine internship at Los Angeles County - USC Medical Center. A subsequent research fellowship at the Doheny Eye Institute led to an ophthalmology residency at USC, which he completed in 1981.
In addition to operating a busy practice adjacent to the Cedars - Sinai Medical Center, Dr. Macy has previously served as President of the Beverly Hills District of the Los Angeles County Medical Association, President of the California Academy of Ophthalmology, and Chairman of the Board of Trustees of Olympia Hospital.
A clinical professor of ophthalmology at both USC and UCLA, Dr. Macy is the author of more than 100 chapters, papers, and abstracts on various medical treatments of the eye. He is a recognized expert in both
laser surgery for cataracts
and
LASIK in Los Angeles, CA
.
Dr. Macy has delivered lectures on ophthalmology around the world and is frequently called upon to advise state and federal government agencies, corporations, investment bankers, and attorneys. Because of his extensive knowledge of ophthalmology, Dr. Macy has been asked to serve as a consultant to pharmaceutical companies as
a clinical researcher into new drug therapies.

Meet Alexander Knezevic, MD | Ophthalmologist
Dr. Knezevic completed his ophthalmology training at the prestigious Northwestern University, which has consistently been ranked one of the nation’s top medical schools by U.S. News and World Report. Dr. Knezevic continued his surgical training at University of California - Irvine with a Fellowship in Corneal, Anterior Segment, & Refractive surgery.
Dr. Knezevic has published many peer-reviewed articles, presented at national conferences, and written a number of book chapters on the topics of dry eye, corneal surgery, and
cataract surgery
. He has performed an extensive number of sight-saving surgeries abroad, and has been a visiting physician in both India and Nepal. He is motivated to pass his experience on to the next generation by teaching residents and medical students. He holds appointments as Assistant Professor at Cedars-Sinai and Voluntary Clinical Professor at Jules-Stein, UCLA.
Dr. Knezevic believes in a personalized healthcare experience utilizing his training and experience to achieve your goals. He is an expert at state-of-the-art cataract surgery including laser cataract surgery, implants that give you both near and far vision, advanced full thickness and partial thickness cornea transplant surgeries (DMEK, DSAEK, PKP), secondary lens fixation, and LASIK surgery employing cutting-edge technology to deliver the best possible results.

Meet Yasaman Mahdavi, OD | Optometrist
Dr. Mahdavi
is a board-certified optometrist specializing in medical optometry and ocular surgery co-management. She works closely with ophthalmologists in the pre- and post-operative care of patients undergoing procedures such as cataract surgery, LASIK, PRK, and corneal transplant.
Dr. Mahdavi earned her Bachelor’s degree from the University of California, Irvine, and her Doctor of Optometry degree from Western University of Health Sciences. She is fluent in both English and Farsi, allowing her to effectively care for a diverse patient population.
In addition to her surgical co-management role, Dr. Mahdavi provides comprehensive eye examinations and is experienced in fitting a wide range of contact lenses, including soft lenses and rigid gas permeable (RGP) lenses. She also specializes in advanced dry eye disease management, with experience in both medical and in-office procedural treatments such as meibomian gland expression and punctal plug insertion. She manages a variety of ocular conditions such as glaucoma and treats acute issues like red eye and ocular infections.

Cataract Surgery
Cataracts affect over a quarter of Americans between the ages of 65 and 74 and half of those over the age of 75. Cataract surgery can replace a clouded lens with an intraocular lens (IOL), which is a flexible plastic that will never cloud. Dr. Macy and Dr. Knezevic are recognized experts in laser cataract surgery.
Cataract Surgery
IOLs
We offer several types of IOLs to patients undergoing cataract surgery. Our options include traditional IOLs, Toric IOLs for patients with astigmatism, and multifocal IOLs including AcrySof® IQ PanOptix® Trifocal IOL for patients with presbyopia. Whatever your unique vision-correction needs, our doctors will recommend the right lens to give you clearer eyesight.
