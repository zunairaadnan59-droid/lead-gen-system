Frequently Asked Questions
about Versona The Overlook
What is Cato Fashions return policy?
Expand or collapse answer
With your receipt, items with original tags attached and in new, unworn condition may be returned within 30 days of in-store purchase, or within 30 days of a shipped item's delivery date. After 30 days or without an original receipt, we will issue an exchange or store credit at current selling price. If you do not have your original receipt, please contact Customer Service at 1-800-758-CATO (2286). For more information about returns,
click here
.
Can I ship my online order to a Cato Fashions store near me?
Expand or collapse answer
Yes! Your order can be shipped to your local Cato store for free. During checkout, select "Free Ship to Store" as your shipping option. Free Ship to Store orders are shipped with our weekly store shipments, so please allow up to 15 business days. You will receive a confirmation email from us once your order has arrived in store.
Does Cato Fashions have a loyalty program?
Expand or collapse answer
Cato Fashions offers Cato Style Rewards for Cato credit card holders. If you have a Cato credit card, you are automatically enrolled. To apply for the Cato credit card, visit your local Cato store or apply online
here
.
What size range does Cato Fashions offer for women's clothing and shoes?
Expand or collapse answer
Cato Fashions offers an inclusive range of women's clothing from sizes 2 to 28, including trendy junior misses and plus size apparel. For shorter frames, petite sizes (2–16) and plus size petites (up to 28W) are available online and in select stores. Complete your look with women's shoes in sizes 6 to 12, featuring wide width options for select styles. Please note that half sizes are available exclusively online.
Where can I find Cato Fashions coupons?
Expand or collapse answer
Cato Fashions offers the same low prices every day of the week, so you don't have to wait for sales or coupons.
Does Cato Fashions offer a layaway plan?
Expand or collapse answer
Yes, Cato Fashions stores offer a convenient 30-day in-store layaway plan to help you secure your favorite styles. Please note that traditional layaway is not available for online purchases.
