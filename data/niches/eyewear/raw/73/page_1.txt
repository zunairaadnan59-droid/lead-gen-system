About Input Optics
Input Optics was founded in 1971 by two forward thinking Dentists who were looking to create solutions for their Practice Management problems.  After years of continuous innovations, they eventually integrated an optical scanning device to automatically capture bar coded data.  From this process, Scandent Practice Management Software was born.
Thirty five years later, Input Optics mission continues to be the integration of software, hardware and the Web, to create digital solutions for today's Dental practice.
Our early vision of a completely paperless and secure Dental office is the challenge facing  practitioners today.  Input Optics proven track record of providing innovation,  productivity and efficiency qualifies us to help your  practice achieve this goal.
We are constantly striving to develop simple, fast & affordable ways to accelerate today's Dental practice into the future.
